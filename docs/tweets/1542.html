Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

maandag 2 november 2026, 11:48
O mind, become still and listen to the sacred words of the SadGuru, for through his boundless compassion, the Lord lovingly calls His wandering children back to their eternal home. O Guru-pyare, do not mistake the presence of the SadGuru for an ordinary blessing, for his guidance is a precious opportunity to awaken from the slumber of ignorance and discover the truth that the restless world can never offer. He does not come to satisfy the demands of the ego, but to transform the heart, discipline the mind, and awaken the soul to its forgotten relationship with the Divine. Yet how painful it is to witness a heart receiving such precious wisdom only to return to the very attachments from which the Guru is trying to liberate it. We seek his blessings but hesitate to follow his instructions; we long for divine peace but continue feeding the desires that disturb our inner being. O mind, understand that spiritual life is not merely hearing the truth, but having the courage to live it when the world tempts you to abandon it. The glitter of Maya may captivate the eyes, but its promises cannot satisfy the deepest hunger of the soul. Behind outward smiles, worldly achievements, and the endless pursuit of recognition, countless hearts remain restless, searching for a happiness that possessions and praise cannot provide. Do not spend your precious life building an identity that time will eventually dissolve, while neglecting the eternal Self that longs to rest in the Divine. Remember that this earthly journey is temporary, and the hour of departure is unknown; therefore, postpone neither repentance nor surrender. When the final curtain of worldly existence falls, only the impressions of our deeds, the sincerity of our love, and the grace of God will truly matter. Return to the SadGuru with humility, not merely when suffering becomes unbearable, but also when life appears favourable and the ego begins to believe it needs no guidance. Let his words become the discipline of your thoughts and the foundation of your actions. As he teaches, immerse your heart in the sacred remembrance of Sri Vithala Giridhari Parabrahmane Namaha, allowing the Divine Name to purify your consciousness and strengthen your devotion through every trial. Serve the needy, share what you possess, forgive those who wound you, and practise compassion even when no one witnesses your kindness. Let charity arise from love rather than pride, and let devotion express itself through selfless service. This is the Dharma that transforms ordinary living into an offering to God. The life and mission of Vishwananda point towards this sacred calling: to awaken hearts to divine love, to guide seekers towards the truth of Dharma, and to remind them that genuine bliss is discovered through a living relationship with the Lord. O mind, cease wandering in search of what already has its eternal source in God. Hold firmly to the SadGuru’s guidance, surrender the burden of ego, and let every breath become remembrance, every action become service, and every heartbeat become an offering at the lotus feet of Sri Vithala Giridhari. The world may promise pleasure, but only divine grace can lead the soul towards lasting peace. Sri Vithala Giridhari Parabrahmane Namaha.
