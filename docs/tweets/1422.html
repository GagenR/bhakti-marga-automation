Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

The SatGuru’s only lesson is love. It is an inner knowing, a truth that one either authentically feels or doesn’t.
No one is forced to understand that or experience anything. One is always free to walk away. The SatGuru will never ask for anything. He doesn’t need it. People like to say and think many negative things, because it allows them to feel they are being controlled or deceived. Believe it or not, it is much easier to live with the feeling that someone is “pushing me, forcing me, testing me”; than it is to take responsibility and live life the way one fully wants to. So many people have come and gone from the path with the SatGuru. No one has been asked to come, no one has been asked to stay. One is always free. Free to be with Him. Free to not. It is each one’s own life. That is the way Bhagavan created all. Free to choose. This is the way one always is. Free. If the Creator Himself doesn't force anyone to do anything, there is no question of anyone else being allowed to do so. The path with the SatGuru is simple. If one wishes to learn, one is welcome. If one wishes to stop learning, one is free to leave. Most people don’t like to choose for themselves, they prefer blaming someone else. And if one’s free choice is to leave the SatGuru, there won’t be any obstacle that He puts in the way. His repeated and only lesson is genuine and unconditional love for Bhagavan. If it is coming across as something else to someone, they can freely choose a different way that leads them where they wish to go. Each person is a beautiful spark of God, all should live the complete life they have come here for.
