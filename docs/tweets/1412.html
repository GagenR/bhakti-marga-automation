The human birth is for enlightenment.
You are equipped with all the grace to reach there. Every atma, when it takes birth as a human on planet earth, comes here with the full potential of complete enlightenment. The self recognizing the Self. The atma being awakened in its entirety. In fact, enlightenment is the easiest thing to reach for a human being. It is already there. The process of birth simply makes the memory of enlightenment hide. The memory is temporarily lost so that one can take part in God’s endless play of unconditional love. It is simple. Imagine your dearest friend, your most beloved companion. Wouldn’t you wish to spend time with them endlessly exploring and doing new things? Wouldn’t you wish to experience new adventures to enjoy, learn, and grow with them? Wouldn’t you wish to watch movies that are sometimes tragic, sometimes full of humour, sometimes filled with action or anger, sometimes filled with care and love, or sometimes very confusing? You know deep inside all along that it is just a movie which will end and has no impact on you who is watching. But you allow yourself to get immersed in the movie while it’s going on because you want to have infinite experiences with your dearest one who is next to you. This is the play of God. Atma and Paramatma in eternal company. Therefore enlightenment isn’t something far away. It isn’t something difficult. It is something you already are. But you have to release your death grip on the temporary identities and roles of this world. Surrender that mind which is currently trained to look only at the temporary movies of the world. And remind it to focus completely on who is exploring the path with you since eternity. Who is watching the infinite movies with you. Surrender to the One who has always been standing next to you. Your beloved eternal companion who is waiting for you to turn and engage with Him. God walks with everyone. But only few cherish His company. Because they are too busy engaging with the temporary drama of the outside.
