Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 25 november 2026, 13:23
How is the SatGuru able to love absolutely unconditionally and simultaneously put a stone on His heart to keep someone at a distance.
It is truly extraordinary. When humans feel deep love or compassion. What happens? We allow that person close and dear to ourselves on the outside as well. We spend time personally next to them. Yet the SatGuru’s love is extraordinary. He knows that sometimes being allowed personally close will prohibit us from letting go of painful and destructive behavioural patterns. We will instead hold onto them more and more tightly. For example, if being close reinforced the idea in someone’s mind that ‘love equates to personal closeness and proximity. This thinking and behaviour would make one fall into so many problems like lust, pride, despair, and so many others because it is inherently wrong and destructive. To show that the two are not connected, the SatGuru would spare no effort. Even if it means that one misunderstands Him or thinks that He doesn’t care, He will do His utmost to help sincerely. Because He devotes Himself to the true joy and complete freedom of everyone.
Humans always confuse deep and fathomless love with spending time together personally. This keeps playing out in everyone’s mind. This is why love and proximity are often confused by people as being the same and one brings these delusions to the spiritual path as well. Love has absolutely nothing to do with physical closeness or proximity. Love is about giving. Always giving compassion. Even at great personal cost. The SatGuru’s love is to always help His beloved children to grow into true and complete happiness within themselves. Where one can be happy and content regardless of any changes of the outside.
With the limited mind and forgetting everything except for this life, what does one do? The ones who spend time in closer physical proximity are thought of as dearer, better, and more important. And the ones who spend time with further distance are thought of as uncherished, expendable, worse, and far less important. But what does physical proximity have to do with love? The two are completely unrelated concepts. The human mind however still rebels and refuses to accept this simple truth. So many of the most beloved Saints of Vitthal had not seen His Vigraha even once in their entire lifetime. Sant Chokha Mela was beaten every time he tried to go to Pandharpur to have Darshan of his beloved Lord. Sant Savta Mali served Vitthal in his own garden his entire life and never set foot inside the Temple of Pandharpur. And yet Sant Namdev who spent every moment in Panduranga’s personal and physical company, inside and outside the Temple, had to learn to keep away his arrogance and pride. His ego had to be burned at the Lotus Feet of the SatGuru. What does all this mean? Be simple. Focus on the infinite gratitude you should have for being graced with a single moment near the SatGuru’s Lotus Feet. Yet bhaktas allow their minds to be taken away from gratitude, humility, joy, and positivity into pride, despair, greed, jealousy, and foolishness. Don’t forget the SatGuru’s presence within your heart. Make your self a great offering of joy so that His eternal home inside your heart can be as beautiful, comfortable, and joyful to Him as possible.
