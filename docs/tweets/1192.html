Vasant Panchami is the celebration of spring with special prayers to Devi Saraswati.
On this day, Devi Saraswati is revered by everyone, especially spiritual seekers, students, scholars, artists, musicians, and writers. To receive her blessings for success, true learning, wisdom, and knowledge. It is very important to have right knowledge, which can ultimately help one to cross over the ocean of delusion and reach Bhagavan’s Lotus Feet.
On Vasant Panchami, people dress in yellow, which is considered auspicious and symbolizes the vibrancy of spring. Yellow flowers, particularly marigolds, are used for decoration. Haldi (turmeric) and Kesar (saffron) are used to worship Devi as well as in the Prasad prepared for Puja. Not only in homes and temples, but also in educational institutions and office spaces, special prayers are offered to Goddess Saraswati. Instruments, tools, books, pens, and other items used for studying and working are kept at the feet of the Goddess and worshiped during Puja. In Bhagavad Gita, ch10 v35, Bhagavan Sri Krishna declares, “Of the seasons, I am the season of flowers (Vasant).” Hence, Vasant (spring) is considered the king of all seasons, marking rejuvenation, joy, and closeness to the divine. Shubh Vasant Panchami to all.
