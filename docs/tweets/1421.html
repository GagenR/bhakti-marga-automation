Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 19 augustus 2026, 14:03
So many people don’t remain kind and decent people when walking their so-called ‘spiritual path’.
This is the biggest hypocrisy and source of very dangerous spiritual ego. One gives oneself the license to behave anyhow, speak anyhow, and think anyhow. Simply because one has stuck a label or role of ‘spiritual’ on oneself. But it is exactly like sticking a label of gold onto an empty package and then trying to sell it for the same value as real gold. Isn’t this what many people are doing? Because one feels one has achieved something, one slacks off. Doesn’t bother looking at oneself anymore. Do you genuinely believe that anyone gets close to God this way? Has one deluded one’s mind so much that one is obsessed with the outside and kept all loving qualities of the inside away? There are nearly countless ways that hypocrisy arises again and again in the mind. A little bit of unwillingness to clean oneself of negativity and the hypocrisy finds a way to grow within. All qualities exist with everyone always. It depends on each moment what choice you make. Enlightenment is very easy but do people keep it easy? No. Because their choices are contradictory moment to moment. One says one wants God-realization but the next moment one puts the mind in an opposite thought. One is only wasting one’s own time and fooling oneself. The label of ‘spiritual’ means something. It isn’t simply a word to throw around and act like one knows. Spiritual means to live from the inner spirit. To act in such a way that the inner spirit of everyone becomes strengthened, joyful, and light. Spirituality should make you more and more wonderful to be with for everyone. The world should long for your company because you are so caring, happy, cheerful, humble, and loving. This is what Sant Kabir Dev ji meant when he said that when you enter the world, you cry and the world is joyful. But when you die, you should be laughing and the world should cry. You are laughing with joy at death because you lived every single moment in love and care for all, for God in all. And the world cries at your death because they will miss the extraordinary energy of kindness, humility, and most of all love that they felt with you. Before putting the label ‘spiritual’ on yourself, you should be doing the moral work that comes with that lofty state. To reach the inner spirit within you, first at least start cleaning your conscious mind of its many impurities.
