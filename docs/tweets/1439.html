Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

Spiritual ego is far worse than regular ego of material things.
The spiritual ego has two ways of punching others. First, one behaves horribly towards the world. And then one delivers the second worse punch, which is coating the entire bad behavior under the disguise of goodness, morality, or God’s will. Any time someone justifies their bad behavior by telling you that is how the “saints” behave. Or, “this is the way that one learns on the spiritual path.” Please know that they do not know what they are talking about at all. They are not behaving like spiritual people at all. It is pure negativity and ego. Nothing else. True spirituality starts on the inside, continuously purifies oneself on the inside, and blossoms outward as a beautiful flower in the peak of its bloom simply to give away everything without any conditions, without holding any beauty back. Don’t let anyone tell you that they have greater closeness with the SatGuru than you do. Don’t let anyone tell you that they understand or know Him so you must listen to them. Don’t let anyone tell you that they are allowed to behave differently from what the SatGuru is telling everyone to do publicly. Public and private Adesh never contradict one another. In fact, what is spoken publicly by the SatGuru for everyone is the basis of receiving any guidance from Him one-on-one. If one has not made and isn’t continuously making efforts to follow what is taught and given openly, then one will never understand any private conversation with or guidance from the SatGuru. It is so sad to see that people sometimes inflate their egos because they have spoken with the SatGuru about something when others were not there, and now they use that incident to not follow what He tells everyone to do. This kind of behavior has a terrible smell of ego that is most off-putting for God and Guru. Bhagavan runs far away from the slightest smell of this kind of manipulation. Simply because someone said that it is God’s will, does not make it so. God’s will always increases love. Increases freedom. Increases joy. Increases deep wisdom of seeing oneself and others as equal. Be very careful and analyze: are you following someone’s ego or are you following the SatGuru’s true teachings of love and humility.
