Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

maandag 12 oktober 2026, 01:47
After searching through countless paths and contemplating the deepest truths, we discover that the Divine Name of Sri Hari is the supreme refuge of the soul and the sweetest nectar of spiritual realization. Even a moment of sincere remembrance can begin to purify the heart, loosen the bonds of past actions, and turn the soul toward the liberating grace of the Lord. O seekers of wisdom, immerse yourselves in this sacred Name, for within it lies a boundless ocean of Divine Love. The nectarean words of God and His Saints awaken the spirit, illuminate the path of Dharma, and bring a fulfillment that worldly pleasures can never provide. Through Sri Hari and the compassionate guidance of the SadGuru, the soul discovers Bhakti, liberation, and the supreme peace born of surrender. He, the Architect of our destiny and the Cause of all causes, graciously bestows the gift of devotional worship and loving service upon those who seek Him with a sincere heart. Let our ears delight in His glories, our tongues sing His Name, and our hearts remain absorbed in His remembrance. Beyond every appearance and every changing circumstance, He alone is the eternal foundation of existence, the indwelling presence sustaining all creation. How blessed we are to receive this precious human birth, through which we may seek the Holy, serve the Saints, and awaken to the Truth. O Sri Hari, Ocean of Mercy, remove the darkness of ignorance, dissolve our pride, and grant us the grace to remain forever at Your lotus feet. In the sacred company of Saints and Bhaktas, let us glorify His Name with unwavering faith, knowing that true wealth lies not in worldly possessions but in a heart filled with Divine Love. May every breath remember Him, every action serve Him, and every moment draw us closer to His eternal presence. His Name is our refuge, His grace our strength, and loving devotion to Sri Hari the highest blessing of this human life.
