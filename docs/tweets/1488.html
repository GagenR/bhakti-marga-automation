Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

maandag 5 oktober 2026, 00:50
When it was pleasing to the SadGuru, we were blessed to chant the holy name, the Name of the all-pervading Lord. In that moment, what appeared to be my own seeking revealed itself as His grace, for the divine name is not merely a word upon the tongue, but the living remembrance of the One who pervades all existence. The Lord of the Universe extended His mercy towards me, and His grace and love began to illumine the heart from within. His holy feet are forever the dwelling place of peace; there, the restless mind becomes still, desires lose their power, and the heart learns to trust what Bhagavan provides. Whatever fruit is truly meant for the soul is received through His Kripa, and no sincere prayer offered at His feet is ever lost. The Saint upon whom the Great Giver, the Lord of Life, bestows His grace becomes naturally drawn into the Glorious Praises of Sri Hari, not out of duty, but because the heart can no longer remain silent before such love. His soul becomes immersed in loving devotion, and his mind finds its highest fulfilment in pleasing the Supreme Lord of Love. With every breath, the remembrance of Sri Vitthala Giridhari deepens, and the bitterness created by fear, attachment, anger and the restless movements of the mind loses its hold upon one who has entered the sweetness of divine love. Sri Hari, the Creator and Sustainer, has united me with Himself, and through His compassion the Holy Saints have become companions, guides and living reminders of the Truth. Taking me by the hand, He has given me not simply the things I once sought, but the understanding to recognize what is eternal and the grace to let go of what is passing. In the presence of the SadGuru, the scattered pieces of life begin to return to their source; seeking becomes remembrance, remembrance becomes devotion, and devotion becomes surrender. What seemed unfinished is brought towards its natural completion, because the greatest fulfilment is not in possessing something outside oneself, but in awakening to the One who was never separate. Everything has been perfectly resolved. I have found the SadGuru, and in finding Him, I have found the holy name , the Saints, the path, and the grace of Sri Hari Himself.
