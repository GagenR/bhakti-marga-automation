Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

dinsdag 24 november 2026, 02:17
This body will one day return to the earth, the movements of time will continue, and everything that appears permanent will eventually change. Yet the eternal truth revealed by the SadGuru remains untouched by birth and death. The atma, awakened through His grace, begins to recognise the Divine Reality that transcends every changing form. What greater blessing can there be than to live in this remembrance, to breathe His Naam, to recognise His light in every heart and to offer every thought, word and action at His lotus feet? Vishwananda says on this holy Gurupurab, may we seek not only the blessings of the SadGuru but also the transformation that His teachings invite into our lives. May pride give way to humility, judgement to compassion, restlessness to remembrance, and the illusion of separation to the living experience of Oneness. May every heart become a sanctuary of divine love, every home a place of peace, and every life an expression of His infinite kindness. May those who are suffering find comfort in His presence, those who are searching find direction in His wisdom, and those who feel distant from the Divine discover that His grace has accompanied them through every moment of their existence. The greatest celebration of Gurupurab is the awakening of Guru Nanak Dev Ji’s eternal message within our own consciousness, until love is no longer merely something we speak about, but the very essence of how we live. For when the heart truly belongs to the Beloved, even ordinary existence becomes sacred, silence becomes worship, service becomes devotion and life itself becomes an offering of gratitude. May Sri SadGuru Nanak Dev Ji bless every atma with the courage to surrender, the wisdom to recognise the One in all, and the divine longing that ultimately dissolves into eternal peace. May His light never cease shining within us, and may we remain forever at His lotus feet, immersed in Naam, guided by grace and united in the boundless love of the Eternal One.
