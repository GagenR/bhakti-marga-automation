Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 30 oktober 2026, 16:42
Do not postpone gratitude until every desire has been fulfilled. Do not make your peace dependent upon circumstances that are constantly changing. Accept what has come, do what is right within your capacity, surrender what lies beyond your control, and allow the heart to remain anchored in the Divine. Acceptance does not mean abandoning responsibility or refusing to improve your circumstances; it means ceasing to wage an endless inner war against the reality of the moment. It means understanding that you can act wisely without being consumed by anxiety, work diligently without becoming enslaved by ambition, and care deeply without allowing fear to govern your life. Above all, let the love of the Guru become the medicine that heals the disease of perpetual dissatisfaction. When the Guru’s grace enters the heart, we gradually learn that peace is not something to be accumulated like worldly possessions, but something to be awakened through surrender, trust, and remembrance of the Divine. The Guru teaches us to distinguish between what we truly need and what the restless mind endlessly demands. His love helps us recognise that life is not merely a journey of acquisition, but an opportunity to purify the heart, deepen our devotion, serve creation, and realise the sacred presence that sustains us at every moment. Let the Guru transform your relationship with the present. When happiness comes, receive it with gratitude; when sorrow comes, meet it with patience; when uncertainty arises, place your trust in the Divine. Do not cling desperately to what must eventually change, and do not reject the blessings that are already resting in your hands. Let your possessions remain in your care without allowing them to possess your heart. Let your plans be guided by wisdom, but let your peace remain rooted in faith. Life is a precious gift, and every moment spent in needless worry is a moment that could have been offered in love, prayer, kindness, and joyful remembrance of the Lord. Therefore, live fully, love sincerely, give generously, and rest your heart in the Guru’s grace. Accumulate not merely wealth, but wisdom; not merely possessions, but compassion; not merely achievements, but devotion. For when the final moment arrives, it will not be the size of your possessions that determines the peace of your soul, but the love with which you have lived, the goodness you have shared, and the degree to which you have learned to surrender your heart to the Divine. Accept the present, remain happy, and let the medicine of the Guru’s love transform you from within. What you have today is an opportunity to live, to love, to serve, and to remember God. Do not waste it waiting for a perfect tomorrow. Let today itself become an offering at the sacred feet of the Guru.
