Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 7 augustus 2026, 10:47
Without constant meditation on Bhagavan’s lotus feet, one can never hope to know what is His will, the will of the atma in entering this life.
To have any hope of making this life successful, where the atma does not repent but instead leaps with joy when death comes, one has to work very hard. Focused and constant work on the self to submit one’s ego and selfish will to the will of the atma which is aligned with the greater Will of Bhagavan. Every atma plays a small part in His eternal play of love. But whether one wants to play a good role or a foolish one. Whether one wants to act unconsciously or consciously. Whether one wants to hear Him when He speaks and follow word for word with love and dedication, or one wishes to put one’s own interpretation to what He says. These are some of the choices that make up one’s life. These are the eternal choices that your atma carries with itself. The choices made in one life are not forgotten nor do they become immaterial on the other side. They always matter. They stay forever. Just like Sri Ram showed so beautifully the eternal nature of a single word spoken years ago when He wasn’t even born. Every choice the jeevatma makes, stays with the atma. The atma bears it either as a burden or as a blessing. Depending upon whether one’s selfish will was held onto or it was repeatedly surrendered to Bhagavan. Only by knowing Bhagavan’s will for the atma, one’s life can become successful here. Meditate on His feet and keep working to transform the self. Don’t stop this work. Let it become your joy and freedom. Through your work, it will become the greatest path.
