Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 13 november 2026, 02:48
The jeeva has to be reminded of the purity, strength, Sharanagati, Bhakti, and Prem that its own true self, that is the atma itself. This great reminder is given by the SatGuru. The SatGuru isn’t one who looks for praise, acknowledgment, or glory from the outside. SatGuru is the one who can bring every true sadhak to complete self and God-realization by reminding one of the atma and revealing Paramatma. The name and fame of the outside doesn’t mean that every teacher can bestow the ultimate liberation, enlightenment, Mukti, Moksha, or realization. The SatGuru is an extremely rare incarnation who is equally close to each and every atma and embodies the greatest and most hidden grace of Bhagavan, His Prem and Sampurna Sharanagati. The deep connection between the atma and SatGuru is revealed and lived within the sadhak, the bhakta. Because this eternal relationship is beyond the reaches of the mind, heart, or intellect. A SadGuru can give complete and eternal freedom to the bhakta. What’s the use of gaining glory or worship of the world if one is not able to live up to that expectation. The SatGuru’s promise to the world, to whoever truly walks the spiritual path until the end goal, is of SatChitAnanda, of ParaBrahman, the eternal Vaikuntha. It is where the SatGuru dwells in every moment regardless of whether He is incarnated in the world or not. And it is where He can bring one if one is sincere and determined to go beyond the material world and surrender oneself fully to Sri Hari’s Lotus Feet. Never seek glory of the world. There is no use even if you gain it. Because the glory of the world is limited, it has a beginning and it will have an end. But the glory of Bhagavan is eternal, ever-growing, and always pure. The SatGuru reminds one of that eternally glorious Bhagavan and the true nature of the atma. The atma which is a spark of Bhagavan’s light itself. All teachers cannot bring one to point of realizing oneself as the atma, to knowing the true relationship of Master and servant between Paramatma and Atma, of eternal service, respect, and Prem between the two. Be wise and listen to the deep voice within the heart which recognizes the SatGuru. The SatGuru who can bring one to the complete fulfillment of life, with whom lifetimes of prayers are answered.
