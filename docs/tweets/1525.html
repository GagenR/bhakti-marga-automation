Jai Ma Mahagauri.
In her previous form as Kaalratri, Ma Bhagavati intensely cleans the mirror of the mind. She scrapes away the dark soot of ego, reactivity, expectation, and fear from the first five chakras and subtle channels. When the heart, speech, and actions are purified, there is no longer any desire to manipulate reality, convince others, or defend the ego. In that absolute quietude, the energy naturally wishes to rise to the Ajna Chakra to live in complete Sharanagati to the lotus feet of the SatGuru. When Devi Kaalratri has worked so fiercely within the self, then Maa Mahagauri emerges as the clean mirror itself, She is the bridge to reach the Ajna Chakra, the awakening of the atma. She is the pristine, luminous mind which can serve the SatGuru endlessly. To grasp the depth of Devi Mahagauri’s innocence, we must understand a subtle difference between her and Maa Brahmacharini. Mahagauri gives everything to others through her previous 7 aspects, keeping nothing for herself, not even asking Bhagavan for Himself—because she is full of confidence that Bhagavan is always there and He will also give His Darshan fully at the right time. She is even willing to wait for Him till the end of eternity, if she is allowed to serve His lotus feet during that time. This is different from Ma Brahmacharini, who is single-pointedly asking Bhagavan for himself because she feels a deep separation from him. But Devi Mahagauri, through her previous seven aspects—from Shailaputri to Kaalratri—has poured out all her strength, her voice, her protection, her grace, everything to all beings. She has kept nothing for herself. Mahagauri rests in the unshakable realization that Bhagavan was never absent. The striving has ended. She sits quietly on her white bull, clad in white garments, holding a Trishul and Damaru, exactly like Lord Shiva. She has become His second body in the world. She radiates pure, effortless innocence. She does not wait in anxiety or doubt; she waits in unconditional trust. She knows that the atma and Paramatma are forever in union. But She does not assume anything. She does not make any grand proclamations of her internal state. She does not show her greatness. She just is. She is Love. She is Devotion. She is Sharanagati. There is no ‘I’ left to become something. Her will is totally offered and unchangingly aligned to the Universal Will of the SatGuru. Yet She forever keeps His seat above Her head, out of reach unless He wills it. She has become Bhakti, Seva, Sadhana, Dakshina, and Ananda—the perfect instrument of Divine Will. May Maa Mahagauri shine her light through the self and illuminate the path for all bhaktas to reach the pure state that She has achieved.
Om Hreem Mahagauryai Namah
