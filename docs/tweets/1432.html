Learning to be okay with “I don’t know, but I’m happy to learn.”
How many people are alright with saying that? Accepting that one doesn’t know everything. Everyone is always learning, always growing. One doesn’t stop being a student. Because life itself is a school. It is beautiful to keep the mind open to all possibilities regardless of age or any other circumstances. Keep learning. Grow. Be curious. Develop critical thinking. Test your faith against difficult questions. Faith isn’t something to just close one’s eyes and accept foolishly. Faith is built on a solid foundation only when you yourself are sure that it can withstand the test of time, of difficult questions, of still loving and having meaningful discussions with those who don’t have the same thought or path as you. Even within one path, everyone is different. How can everyone be the same? It’s not possible. Each one is unique. The beauty is in accepting that. The exciting thing is to grow by learning from everyone. Love is fostered when you wish to grow with the world. You can see perfection in imperfection. You can see God where earlier you could not. It’s okay to not know everything. Life is to be lived. Loved. Enjoyed. Made extraordinary for all beings that you spend time with. This planet is so filled with life, it is actually so easy to love all the time. There are countless ways to grow your love. Make your mind curious and open. Allow it to soar to the heights of peace and joy. Let your mind become your best friend. Accept what you don’t know, but keep learning. Share your best self with the world, and allow God to take care of you in return.
