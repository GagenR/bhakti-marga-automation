Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 9 oktober 2026, 02:29
The soul may possess everything the worlds can offer, dwell in heavenly realms, attain power, knowledge and countless pleasures, yet if remembrance of Sri Hari has vanished from the heart, all of it remains incomplete. Without the Supreme Lord, abundance itself becomes a form of poverty, and even a beautiful life can become an inner wilderness. The mind wanders outward, captivated by countless forms, desires, achievements and entertainments, while the Eternal Presence quietly shines within. This is the true wilderness—not a place, but a state of forgetfulness, where the temporary is mistaken for the permanent, the unreal for the Real, and the passing for the Eternal. Desire pulls the mind outward, anger disturbs its peace, attachment binds it, and egotism creates the illusion of separation. Thus the soul continues wandering through the endless realms of existence, searching outside for what can only be discovered through awakening within. The Divine SadGuru appears as the compassionate guide who turns the wandering consciousness back toward Sri Hari. Through His grace, remembrance begins to awaken, the false gradually loses its hold, and the heart recognizes the One Reality behind all names and forms. When the final moment comes, worldly possessions, status and accomplishments cannot follow the soul. Only what has been awakened within has lasting meaning. Therefore, let the Name of Sri Hari become the thread woven through every breath, every thought and every action. In remembrance of Him, the wilderness becomes a sacred pilgrimage, wandering becomes a return, and the grace of the SadGuru becomes the light leading the soul from illusion into the Eternal.
