Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

dinsdag 28 juli 2026, 11:11
People most often interpret something written down as fixed in a particular language, understanding, and context. But Divine Words are always alive.
What the SatGuru is giving in the present moment is just as vibrantly and divinely alive as His ancient Word that formed all the universes. The full grace of Paramatma is just as available today as it was in eras gone by. There wasn’t a better or easier time to attain Bhagavan in the past as compared to today’s day and age. He is present, close, and just as much in love with His creation now as He was at the purest of beginnings. Because for Him there is no past, present, or future that is unknown. To honor His beautiful presence in this way, our spiritual path with the tadatmya vedanta, places equal importance on wisdom shared by the SatGuru personally right now as well as that which has been recorded in the past for the use of future generations. The ever-new and the most ancient, both, given by the SatGuru. All time is a different facet of Love. This is why the mind needs to be infinitely flexible. Because the ways of God are completely mysterious. This is why wisdom and love always go together. Because you have to learn to accept that which you find unacceptable out of love, and you have to purify your love in the fire of wisdom for it to capture His heart fully. If you cannot love and respect all, you will never grow close to God. If you cannot bow down to this greatest and humblest Truth, then you have forgotten that God is Love itself because love is the all-inclusive force uniting all with God. This is the great truth of the tadatmya vedanta that the SatGuru wishes to bring to everyone. When you start to love all, without judgement and without limits, God will reveal Himself to you fully. And you will see this closeness you have earned with God reflected exactly in your relationship with the SatGuru. Even before you have understood, accepted, or embodied that truth, the SatGuru will deepen your experience of closeness with Him. Before your mind can even process the realization of Love, Love has already walked close to you. He does not waste a single second. He yearns only for unconditional divine pure love. This is the great wisdom that you need to learn through your studies. If you remember the SatGuru with a single word, my prayer is that the word always be Love.
