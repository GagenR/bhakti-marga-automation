Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 8 november 2026, 15:42
Fireworks, Akash Kandeel, Diyas, Sweets and New Beginnings. Shubh Deepavali to all!
As the world comes alive with the radiance of celebration, may we remember that the true meaning of this sacred festival lies in awakening the divine light within ourselves. Every tradition carries a deeper spiritual significance, offering us an opportunity to reflect on our journey towards God. Just as the Akash Kandeel rises high above, illuminating the surroundings, may we keep our spiritual aspiration elevated, always directing our hearts towards the lotus feet of the SatGuru, with unwavering faith and determination. Let our enthusiasm for the Divine shine like fireworks, spreading joy, inspiration and positivity wherever we go. May the light we carry within never become dimmed by the circumstances of life, but grow stronger through every experience, every challenge and every opportunity to surrender. Like the diya that continues to burn by consuming its own wick and oil, let us understand that spiritual illumination comes through the gradual offering of our lower nature, allowing ego, pride and selfish desires to give way to humility, love and devotion. The more we surrender ourselves, the more space we create for the Divine to manifest through us. Let the sweetness of this celebration remind us of the immeasurable sweetness of Sri Hari’s love, which is not meant to be kept for ourselves but freely shared with everyone we encounter. Never hesitate to express this love or share the beauty of the spiritual path, for every soul carries within its heart a divine spark of Amrit, waiting to blossom through grace, faith and its own unique journey. As Vishwananda reminds all , the path towards God is ultimately a journey of love, where every moment offers us an opportunity to recognise the Divine presence within ourselves and in one another. Celebrating together with our spiritual family makes this journey even more beautiful, as the collective devotion, encouragement and positive energy of the sangha help strengthen our connection with the Guru and Sri Hari. May we learn to see each new morning as a fresh beginning, leaving behind what no longer serves our spiritual growth and embracing life with gratitude, openness and renewed dedication. Let every step we take bring us closer to the Divine, and may our thoughts, words and actions become an offering of love at the feet of the SatGuru. May this Deepavali illuminate not only our homes but also the deepest corners of our hearts, bringing us the experience of inner bliss, divine connection and unconditional love. Shubh Deepavali and abundant blessings to everyone
