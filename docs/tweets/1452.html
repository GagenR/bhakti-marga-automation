There are moments when the fullness of the SatGuru’s unconditional love is experienced. But why doesn’t the mind keep that remembrance always?
The mind is given Darshan, the divine is experienced fully. But one has to make efforts to retain that experience. It is like a flower, it can be forcibly opened from its closed state of a bud. But that would destroy the flower. It won’t blossom, it will wither. The SatGuru comes as the wise and loving sun. He shines brightly and allows the flower to wish for more warmth and light. So the flower is given a glimpse of His love, of His divine light. Then little darkness comes back because the bud of the mind is still completely closed. But because of that difference, the flower slightly wakes up, it wonders and looks around for what made it happy and comfortable just a few moments ago. Its tightly closed self opens itself because of its search. Like this, the SatGuru shines brighter and brighter, allowing the flower of the mind to wish for greater warmth and divine love. As He reveals more and more His true state of unconditional love, the mind also has to open more to hold that realization. If the mind doesn’t want it, it will close back again, towards darkness and forgetfulness. Then, life drops to feeling like a cycle of struggle. But one has asked for unconditional love to manifest itself within oneself. To see Bhagavan everywhere all the time. So the SatGuru awakens the self from within. He sits within the heart and gives endless love. As He shines brighter and brighter within the self, the self has to make itself a fully capable container of that grace. Otherwise, one will forget the very thing that one asked for so strongly.
