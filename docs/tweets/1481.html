Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

maandag 28 september 2026, 01:39
Remember the SadGuru and Sri Hari, awaken to the temporary nature of worldly life, and seek what is eternal. The SadGuru shows the way, while Sri Hari is the ultimate refuge and destination. The Divine Name is the boat that carries us across the ocean of samsara, the cycle of birth, death, attachment and suffering. Remember that our husband, wife, children, body, home, wealth and possessions cannot remain with us forever. This does not mean we should stop loving our family rather, we should love and serve all without believing that temporary things can provide permanent happiness. The countless births we have passed through reveal how precious human life is and how rare the opportunity to seek liberation and attain the lotus feet of Sri Hari is.
The stories of Ajamila the miser, Gajendra the mighty elephant and Pingala the courtesan illustrate this path. Ajamila shows the power and mercy of the Divine Name. Gajendra shows surrender when human strength fails. Pingala shows that worldly desire cannot provide lasting peace. Their stories remind us that no one is too fallen or too far from God to turn toward Him. The nectar of immortality, Amrit, is given through the SadGuru in the form of the Divine Name. Attachment, ego, greed and uncontrolled desire are the poisons that keep us from receiving Sri Hari’s grace. Therefore, why choose temporary pleasures when the nectar of remembrance is available? Abandoning doubts about do’s and don’ts does not mean abandoning morality. It means not allowing doubt or perfectionism to prevent spiritual practice. Begin remembering Sri Hari now. Through the SadGuru’s grace, remembrance becomes devotion, devotion becomes surrender, and surrender blossoms into divine love. Ultimately, the goal is not merely freedom from suffering or worldly gain, but love for the Supreme Lord. The deepest question is: When everything I call “mine” is eventually taken away, what have I cultivated in my heart? The answer is: the Holy Name, His remembrance, surrender and, ultimately, the love and grace of Sri Hari.
