Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

On the spiritual path, many people still hold the mentality that they want to eat their cake and have it too.
‘I want to be close, but also not do the compassionate moral work that will allow me to be close to God and Guru.’ ‘I don’t want to be in the pure transformation of closeness to God, but God and Guru should be close to me in every way.’ But you cannot have it both ways. The two are opposite to each other. You either walk the spiritual path, or you don’t. Either you do the compassionate moral work and become an exemplary human being full of love and kindness - thus becoming a truly spiritual person. Or, you do not do the work, do not become an exemplary human being, and do not walk on the spiritual path. One cannot pretend to walk on the spiritual path and not be loving and kind. Spirituality goes hand in hand with your behavior, words, thoughts, and intentions. Whatever one chooses in life, the consequences are wholly one’s own. But most of the time people want the freedom to choose whatever they like, but the consequences they don’t want to shoulder. The consequences they push to God and Guru. ‘God and Guru are to blame if they don’t magically change the consequences after I have chosen poorly.’ For example, one speaks very poorly about the spiritual path but also says that one is walking strongly on it. No. You are not walking on the spiritual path if you are finding it so impossible to experience love, joy, and freedom in your life. It is much, much better that one leaves and does something else, to experience that missing love, joy, and freedom. If the words that one is using to speak of the spiritual path are so sarcastic or full of veiled negativity, one should ask oneself what’s the point of staying in a place or on a path that is not helping one get to true divine love and joy of God. The SatGuru does not want anything from anyone. His life is fully dedicated to God from the beginning to end. It is because one felt within one’s own life and heart to walk the spiritual path with the SatGuru, that one is walking. Please remember the freedom one always has to either walk the path or not. One should never forget that. One does need to go through any kind of force, intellectual gymnastics, or cognitive dissonance to stay on a path of misery. And if one really wishes to stay because one is finding true value, true love, true joy, true closeness to God - then one needs to analyze oneself. Is one behaving how spirituality shows or not; because they go together. If not, then one also has infinite other things to do in life. It is so simple yet people complicate things very much by jumping through so many hoops in their minds. If you feel a certain way, then speak and act the same way. If you feel a different way, then speak and act that way. It will make your own life easy and beautiful to be aligned within and outside. Let go of this mentality of wanting to have your cake and eat it too.
