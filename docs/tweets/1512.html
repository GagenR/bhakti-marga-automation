Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 15 oktober 2026, 14:03
O Mother, what is left for a soul that has spent its precious life wandering through the darkness of ignorance, entangled in worldly desires, pride, and attachments, while forgetting the very Lord who is the source and sustainer of all existence? We pass our days pursuing what is temporary, believing that tomorrow will always be ours, yet time silently carries us towards the inevitable moment when we must leave everything behind. When Death approaches and the noose tightens around the neck, will the mind still possess the strength to remember Sri Hari, whose divine Name we neglected throughout our life? The wealth we accumulate, the possessions we protect, and the relationships upon which we depend cannot accompany us beyond that final threshold. All that we called our own will return to the world, but the consequences of our actions and the impressions engraved upon our consciousness will follow the soul. What greater sorrow can there be than to receive the rare blessing of human birth and allow it to pass without seeking the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord and the mercy of the SadGuru ? The heart trembles at the thought of standing before eternity with a lifetime of opportunities lost and the Name of Sri Hari left unuttered. Yet, O Mother, even in this darkness, let the soul not abandon hope, for the mercy of Sri Hari and SadGuru is greater than the weakness of the fallen. His Name is the refuge of the helpless, the light of the ignorant, and the eternal shelter of those who sincerely turn towards Him. Let us awaken before the final hour arrives, humble our pride, purify our hearts, and offer every remaining breath in remembrance of Sri Hari Narayan. For when the world can offer no protection and the body can no longer serve us, sincere devotion becomes our prayer, surrender becomes our strength, and the holy Name becomes the treasure that worldly death cannot take away.
