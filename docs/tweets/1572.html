Conversation between Goswami Tulsidas and Hanumanji about the SatGuru, about Bhagavan Sri Ram. Goswami Tulsidasji was stuck at different points during the writing of the Sri Ramcharitmanas. At those times, he could not figure out how to explain the Mahima, the glory, of Sri Ram, how to appropriately narrate the circumstances of Bhagavan’s life. In those moments, from Goswamiji’s heart would emerge only one call, “Ram, Sri Ram, Ram. Who is Ram? Where is Ram? How can I move closer to my Ram?” His intense longing to know the Highest would call Hanumanji who would lead him deeper into the secrets of Bhakti. At one such time, Hanumanji was speaking, “Ram. To understand this word you must focus your mind fully on the feet of the SatGuru within the third eye. Who is this SatGuru that I’m asking you to remember constantly? The SatGuru comes to reverse the flow of creation that naturally tries to move further and further away from God. He is the reversal of creation back into divinity. He is the flow of grace back into the Lotus Feet of Bhagavan. He is the awakening of the kundalini which takes one back to the true state. He is the opposite of what one expects when one is stuck in the limitation of the created world. He is the anomaly. The piece which works against moving further from God. He is the Whole manifest as a piece to become the size of the other smaller parts to push them back to where they have originated from. If he were not relatable to the smaller pieces. If they only saw him as a cosmic entity, larger than life, bigger than what they can see and perceive. They would rebel against the obstruction. The SatGuru obstructs the path to move away from God. And creation impulsively tries to move away from God. If the smaller pieces only perceived the obstruction but did not see the love, the longing, the humanness, the desire to be reunited, of the big Creator, they would keep trying to move away from the source to find themselves. To find identity. Everyone is in search of identity. But individualizing does not bring one to the truth of one’s identity. Uniting with the whole, uniting with the eternal source, that reveals one’s true identity. Hence the mind perceives this as paradoxical. The mind sees that the cosmic laws do not apply to the SatGuru. The mind sees that here is someone who it cannot control. It cannot understand. And it cowers in fear for losing its fiercely held temporary, false identity - the ego. The ego is nothing but false identity. When the truth is identified as anything other than God. When identity is mistaken to be out there rather than inside. When identity is put in a box of restrictions, that is ego. But when identity is put at the end of limitations, in ultimate freedom, that is the reunion with God, that is one’s true state. The movement to individualize is ego. The movement to unite with the eternal is love. The law of God is love. The movement of that law happens through will. When will moves without love, the energy created becomes destructive. When will moves with love, the energy created brings one deeper and deeper towards identifying oneself as having come from the whole, from God. When the cosmic identity of the atma becomes its only identity and the ego is fully dissolved, Viraam, Raam, the end of all limitations is reached. Thus one attains the eternal place at Sri Ram’s feet. One forever loses the ability to identify oneself as great. Because Ram is all that exists. All that ever was. All that ever will be. Ram is me. Ram is the created world. Ram is the Adi, most ancient one. Ram is the Anth, the end of all things. Then the “I” becomes His servant. I lose myself in Ram. When the time comes to serve Ram, to move in His creation, when I serve the world that Ram has created with so much love, when I serve his creatures, I am always a servant of Ram. At all times of solitude, there is no I, only Ram.”
