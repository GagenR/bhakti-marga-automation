Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 21 augustus 2026, 12:06
Shubh Varalakshmi Vratham to everyone.
This day celebrates the utmost love of Mahalakshmi for Bhagavan Narayan and His complete dedication to Her. Keeping continuous vigil for Her beloved today, Mahalakshmi manifested her eternal form and married Shreeman Narayan for all eternity. This Vrat, or vow, of Mahadevi is an example for all. Her love and devotion to Him doesn’t make her weak or less than Him. In today’s world, people have forgotten this. She is the all-powerful Shakti. The One who completes Bhagavan. They are two sides of the same divinity. Eternally together, eternally loving and serving each other. This is the example for the world that they always set forward. But one must learn from these highest states of being. Don’t let them simply remain stories. Make it a reality of your life. This Vrat is also connected to Lord Shiva and Devi Parvati. Lord Shiva personally narrated the importance of this Vrat to Devi Parvati, explaining that sincere observance brings peace, health, and harmony. Encouraging all to follow the highest example of Mahalakshmi and Shreeman Narayan. The ways that people perform this Vrat are beautiful and varied, but the underlying lesson is the same: love and prosperity is something you build with intention, not something that just arrives.
