Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zaterdag 1 augustus 2026, 08:19
The freedom to be exactly and completely yourself. The joy of having your own experiences and your own deepening understanding. The peace of loving and respecting all. The absolute bliss of having the Supreme Lord always and forever with you. This and so much more beauty is the path of Love that the SatGuru walks on.
Freely, without any strings attached, without reducing anything or taking anything away, without hurting or causing suffering. Only receiving more and more joy, peace, and freedom. That is the path of Bhakti, of Love. How many people, places, or practices can say so openly that you can have everything because by loving God endlessly, He Himself comes to reside with you? Search and see for yourself. It is very easy to talk about atheism or becoming your own god and it may seem very trendy in today’s world. But why don’t you explore for yourself before following the crowd? Just look at the precision with which everything is made and running in this world. The body, the mind, of so many creatures. The feelings and instincts that make all beings gravitate to love and run away from hatred. This universal highest love is God and God is love. Explore every field of study. You will see that there are always more questions than answers. This is the deep truth of learning. That one becomes more open-minded and humble. If learning makes you arrogant or makes you think that you know more than others, then you are becoming dogmatic, not learned. The Bhagavad Gita speaks of the path of love as the highest of all learning and paths. Why? Find the answer within your own heart. On the path with the SatGuru, you will never be told to stop investigating, stop self-analysis. You must do that so that your learning can become more and more refined and pure. Your mind should become as sharp as a knife and as gentle as a flower petal, to discern what is a deeper form of love and what isn’t. The knife of your discernment should always be turned inwards like a scalpel cutting away the parts of the psyche that are operating in lower forms of love. Never turn the knife of your mind outwards onto others. The gentleness and beauty of your love should be given to all freely, to yourself and others. The blossoming of love within one’s mind and life, that’s what brings atma and Paramatma close to each other.
