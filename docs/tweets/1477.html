Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 27 september 2026, 14:35
The SadGuru has dispelled the fever that burned within my soul. The heat of sorrow has become coolness, the darkness has become light, and my restless heart has found its resting place in the remembrance of the Beloved, Sri Hari.
What I could not heal by my own strength, He healed through His mercy and love. What I could not understand, He revealed through His grace. O my Lord, You saw my suffering before I could speak of it. You knew every hidden wound, every silent fear, every tear, and every longing within me. Yet You did not turn away. You came near. Your love is greater than my weakness, Your mercy greater than my mistakes, Your patience greater than my wandering, and Your compassion greater than anything I could ever deserve. The SadGuru has touched the place where sorrow had taken root and planted there the remembrance of Your Name. Hari, Hari. When Your Name arises within the heart, fear becomes peace, emptiness becomes fullness, and loneliness becomes the realization that the Beloved was always near. The unstruck melody resounds within, and in its sacred silence I begin to understand that Your love was surrounding me even when I could not see it. You were there when I rejoiced, when I suffered, when I understood, and when I was lost. I thought I had to find You, but You had never stopped seeking me. O Lord, every breath is a gift, every moment of peace is a gift, and even the desire to love You is Your gift. The greatest comfort is not that Sri Hari gives me everything I desire, but that God Himself is with me. You are my shelter when I am afraid, my medicine when I am wounded, my light when I cannot see, my strength when I am weak, and my peace when the world becomes restless. Let Your Name become the breath of my soul. May I remember You in joy and in sorrow, in strength and in weakness, until my final breath.
O Beloved Lord of Love, You do not despise the broken heart or reject the one who returns after wandering. Even when I forget You, You do not forget me. I may change, but You do not change; I may become distant, but You remain near. O SadGuru, O Sri Hari, remove from me the fever of ego, the disease of separation, and the darkness of forgetfulness. Fill my heart with Your Name and Your love. The greatest blessing was never merely the comfort You gave me; it was that You allowed me to discover You. You are the healer, the protector, the giver, the Beloved, and the home of my soul. Heal me according to Your will, gently and completely. Heal what others can see and what only You know. Where there is pain, bring peace; where there is fear, bring Your presence; where there is emptiness, fill me with Your Name; where there is weakness, hold me with Your compassion. I do not ask to understand everything. I only ask for the humility to surrender. Take my wounded heart as it is, my imperfect faith, my tears, my hopes, and my fears. Let me rest in You. If healing comes slowly, give me patience; if it comes gently, give me gratitude. May my healing bring me closer to You and make my heart softer, quieter, kinder, and more grateful.
O my SadGuru, when my mind wanders, gently bring it back to You; when my heart becomes heavy, let it rest in You. I have nothing to prove before You. I only wish to rest in Your mercy. Sri Hari, I am here. I am tired, but I am Yours. I have little to offer except this heart. Let that be enough. No more striving, no more fear, no need to understand everything—only Your Name, only Your mercy, only the gentle remembrance that I belong to You. Hari, Hari. With folded hands and a softened heart, I surrender. May I remain in Your remembrance, rest in Your love, and quietly accept Your will. May Your presence be enough.
