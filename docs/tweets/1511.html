Jai Devi Skandamata.
The fifth aspect of Devi is also known by someone else’s identity—She is the mother of Skanda, commander of the divine army. But this is totally different from being lost in the world without any clue of one’s true nature. Devi’s fifth form shows us that when one lets go of any lower desire and continuously lives in the Anahat Chakra, one becomes disinterested and detached from wanting any personal glory or recognition. One is so immersed in loving and serving others, that one feels great joy when their achievements are recognized, and doesn’t care about receiving glory for one’s own greatness. Such a person does not care about being in the limelight at all, they only want to love and serve endlessly. Skandamata represents the pinnacle of gentle affection, which looks upon the whole world with the eyes of a loving mother. True spiritual strength is rooted in pure, selfless love. But in the midst of realizing Her gentle love, one should never forget that She accumulated enormous strength and fierceness through Her Sadhana in her four previous aspects. It is because She holds such tremendous energy within her that She can nurture a divine warrior like Skanda. A weak person cannot cultivate a strong person, only the one who is the greatest can give true greatness to another. This is the embodiment of the SatGuru. He holds everything within Himself, but gives the greatness to others. Strength, humility, clarity, and no sense of personal doership; through these divine qualities one can completely forget about the self and raise another to the greatest heights in the world and to Bhagavan’s lotus feet. True self-sacrifice for another is extremely difficult. Because the world may never know or acknowledge what one has gone through for others, but one wishes to do it anyway. To simply love like the SatGuru loves. To serve like He serves. May Devi Skandamata bless everyone to realize the joy in giving endlessly and nurturing others’ lives to reach the SatGuru’s sacred feet.
| Om Aim Hreem Kleem Skandamatayai Namah ||
