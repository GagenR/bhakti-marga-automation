Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 6 november 2026, 12:08
Shubh Govatsa Dwadashi! Shubh Vasu Baras! On this most sacred and auspicious occasion, may Gau Mata, the divine embodiment of motherhood, compassion, purity and selfless nourishment, shower her eternal blessings upon all creation. In the sacred traditions of Sanatan Dharma, the cow is revered not merely as a giver of nourishment, but as a manifestation of divine grace, carrying within her the sacred essence of the celestial powers and the boundless compassion of Bhagavan. May the worship of Gau Mata awaken within us a deeper understanding of Dharma, Ahimsa, gratitude and our sacred responsibility towards all living beings. May the divine Kamadhenu, the celestial mother who emerged during the Samudra Manthan, remind us that the greatest blessings are those that nourish not only the body, but also the soul. May Bhagavan Sri Hari Vishnu, the Supreme Sustainer of the universe, bless every household with peace, abundance, protection and unwavering devotion. May His divine grace remove the obstacles that cloud our spiritual path and strengthen our surrender at His sacred lotus feet. Let us remember the eternal bond between Bhagavan Sri Krishna and the cows of Vrindavan, where divine love, innocence and spiritual bliss are revealed through His beautiful pastimes as Gopala, the protector and beloved of the cows. May that same divine love enter our hearts, transforming selfishness into compassion, pride into humility, and ignorance into spiritual wisdom. As Govatsa Dwadashi heralds the beginning of the sacred celebrations of Deepavali, may the approaching festival of lights become an opportunity to illuminate not only our homes, but also the innermost chambers of our consciousness. May the eternal light of Shreeman Narayan dispel the darkness of Avidya, awaken the divine consciousness within us and guide every Atma towards the realisation of its eternal relationship with Bhagavan. Let us offer our prayers with sincerity, our actions with compassion and our lives with devotion, recognising the divine presence in all creation. May Gau Mata’s blessings bring harmony to families, nourishment to the needy, protection to the innocent and spiritual upliftment to all beings. May the grace of Sri Hari remain with us in every moment, and may our hearts forever be anchored in His divine presence. Sarve Bhavantu Sukhinah, Sarve Santu Niramayah. May all beings be happy, may all beings be free from suffering, and may divine peace prevail throughout creation. Jai Gau Mata! Jai Kamadhenu! Jai Sri Krishna! Jai Shreeman Narayan! Hari Om Tat Sat!
