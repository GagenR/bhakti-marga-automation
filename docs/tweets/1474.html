Shubh Anant Chaturdashi.
If one wishes to deep dive into the wisdom, grace, and realizations hidden in every aspect of the Sanatan Dharma, even countless lifetimes won’t be enough. The saints, in fact, realized this very thing. They didn’t fear the cosmic cycle of reincarnation, they didn’t fear life on Earth. They were so in love with Bhagavan and His truly incredible love hidden everywhere, that they wanted to come again and again to fall deeper and deeper in love with Him. They wanted to constantly surrender and immerse themselves even more into the infinite ocean of bliss which is Sri Hari Narayan, Anant SatChidAnanda.
Two beautiful reminders are given on Anant Chaturdashi. (1) Ganesh Visarjan teaches us to look beyond all material limitations. (2) The Ananta Vrat is made to focus our gaze on the Ananta, the underlying, unchanging, infinite reality of Mahavishnu that remains after everything else is washed away. The 14-year Ananta Vrat is one of the most beautiful prayers dedicated to Shreeman Narayan, focusing on His form as Anant Padmanabha—the infinite eternal one who rests upon the cosmic ocean.
Another important reason why remembering Bhagavan and performing Ganesh Visarjan on Anant Chaturdashi is so important, is the arrival of Pitru Paksh in a few days. During Pitru Paksh, one goes into intense inward reflection, Sadhana, prayers for all ancestors, and deep gratitude for everything that came before, is given now, and will come afterwards. Through the beauty of Anant Chaturdashi, Bhagavan shows us that everything of the outside is changeable, but the lotus feet of the SatGuru are forever unchanged. So intensely focus on Him and embed deep gratitude within the consciousness.
Blessings to all.
