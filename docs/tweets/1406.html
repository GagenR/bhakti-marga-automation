The SatGuru always reminds that the external rituals are for growing in love, but does one behave like that?
After some time of meeting the SatGuru, many people reverse their thinking. What do they do? To perform the ritual, one gives up on love. To be in front, one forgets humility and kindness. To get glory, one forgets their initial unconditional decision to follow the SatGuru. One lets go of love to receive a chance to do the external ritual. But this is the opposite of what one should do. It becomes more important as time passes on the spiritual path to be more loving, more kind, more self-sacrificing, more joyful towards all. Do not let go of the beautiful inner qualities to get to perform the outside rituals. Hold onto the positive qualities that helped you meet the SatGuru and see His loving divine self. But the mind doesn’t cooperate if you don’t constantly train it and then even calling oneself a bhakta, one lets oneself fall. But by acting in this way, one may receive the outer glory, but one would have lost the SatGuru’s heartfelt closeness. Holding onto love may not get you in front. It may not make you look impressive. It may not make others like you. But you will have everything because the SatGuru will delight in love being your first, your only priority. And the world will slowly change its ways towards you also. But only if you hold onto love that fiercely. Like a tiger holds its newborn baby in its mouth, when protecting it from predators. That’s how to hold onto Love. Constantly on guard. Using your full strength but with utmost carefulness. Infinitely aware of danger but with single-pointed concentration on the baby. That is the behavior of a bhakta towards their love for God. Only love can get you to Him. You may have performed thousands of external rituals for Him, but that doesn’t matter to Him at all if you don’t value love within them. One single instance of genuine love keeping everything else aside, that will make you His. He will stop at nothing to pull such a bhakta into His heart, never letting go.
