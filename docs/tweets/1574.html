Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 18 november 2026, 11:47
You truly live when your heart is immersed in the remembrance of Sri Hari, for His Divine Name is the breath of the soul and the eternal nourishment of the spirit. To forget Him is to wander in the darkness of forgetfulness, while to remember Him is to awaken to the light of divine love. Yet, how difficult it is for the restless mind to remain absorbed in His Name! The tongue may repeat the sacred syllables, but the heart must first awaken to the longing for the Beloved. There must be a hunger within you, a thirst that no worldly pleasure can quench, a yearning so profound that nothing but the presence of Sri Hari can bring peace to your being. O Guru Pyare, let this longing become the prayer of your soul! Let every breath carry His remembrance, every thought seek His shelter, and every heartbeat become an offering at His Divine Feet. When the mind is absorbed in Sri Hari, the burdens of existence begin to lose their power, for the soul discovers a refuge beyond all suffering. Who can measure the immeasurable glory of the Lord? Countless sages, saints and seekers have spent their entire lives contemplating His greatness, yet His majesty remains beyond the reach of human understanding. Even if all humanity were to unite in singing His praises, His glory would remain untouched, neither increased by our words nor diminished by our silence. He is the source of all that exists, the unseen hand sustaining every breath and the compassionate presence accompanying every soul. Day and night, He provides for His creation without interruption, nourishing us with gifts we often receive without gratitude. He asks for no recognition, demands no reward, and waits patiently in the silence of His infinite compassion. O Vithala Giridhari, what greatness can compare with Yours? The universe itself is but a reflection of Your boundless generosity. The sun rises by Your grace, the rivers flow under Your watchful care, and every living heart receives its strength through Your sustaining presence. You have given us everything, yet ask only that we turn towards You with love and sincerity. May we never become so absorbed in the gifts of this world that we forget the Giver. May our remembrance never grow faint, and may our devotion never become an empty ritual. For those who recognise Your grace in every moment discover that nothing is truly separate from You. As Vishwananda recalls, a heart that forgets the mercy it has received becomes impoverished, however much it may possess. Blessed is the soul that lives in gratitude, breathes Your Name and finds its greatest treasure in loving You. Sri Hari, may Your Name remain the eternal song of our hearts, now and forever.
