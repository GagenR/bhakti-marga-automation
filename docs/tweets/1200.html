Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 28 januari 2026, 13:31
You have to personally burn your old life to fully step into unconditional love.
Like charcoal becomes fire itself, no difference remains between them. Like the wave and ocean cannot ever be separated. Like the warmth and light are equal parts of the sun, they are eternally together. The same way, constant transformation and love are inseparable.
And transformation only comes through one’s ability to endure pain and create something deeply positive out of such crushing moments. If you cannot bear the pain and run away in the middle, there will be no transformation, and no ability to grow towards God. Because unconditional love is just that. The constant growing towards God. Becoming inseparable like the heart and heartbeats. When love is there, both exist together, and the blood that is pumped sustains everything. When love is not there, neither the heart nor the heartbeats, nothing can survive.
Bhai Manjh personally destroyed his old life, his old self, so that he could make place for Guru Arjan Dev Ji to enter his life. No question asked, no doubts entertained, no pain was too much. The sons of Guru Gobind Singh Ji have become immortal because they did not separate themselves from the pain their Guru was facing. They took the stabs and slashes upon their own chests so that they could live forever with Love. They became the Ang of the SatGuru, parts of His own self. Never to be separated. When somebody speaks of one, the other is automatically spoken of. Like Hanuman and Ram, Krishna and Sudama. Love is the miracle. Its existence creates more miracles. And as long as one is willing to give love more importance than the pain one is suffering, miracles will continue to arise in one’s life. God will start to manifest His eternal self for such a soul.
