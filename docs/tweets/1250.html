The fourth Prahar of Maha Shivratri is from 3am-6am.
The fourth Prahar reveres with great gratitude the drinking of the Halahal poison by Lord Shiva. This aspect of the SatGuru is the fiercely protective Kaal Bhairav. The One who destroys evil, destroys time, destroys death, for His bhaktas. When this aspect of the SatGuru emerges for the bhakta, no illusions and delusions remain. All that is false falls away. His divine fire rages and consumes everything, leaving behind only His own true self, pure Unconditional Love. There is no difference left between atma and Paramatma, between bhakta and SatGuru, between Hara and Hari. All ends, is renewed, and lives eternally in Unconditional Love. This can happen when the jeevatma moves into true and everlasting Sanyas, the final Varna Ashram. Complete renunciation of the world and attachment only to Bhagavan. The outside may stay as is, but the inner world is fully transformed in Purna Sharanagati to Bhagavan.
The aspect of Lord Shiva from Vraj that’s worshipped during this time is Nandishwar Mahadev. His name highlights the union between the Master (Shiva) and the bhakta (Nandi). Nandishwar is the most calm and serene aspect of Shiva. Such a paradox - Nandishwar and Kaal Bhairav - completely opposite on the outside but absolutely the same within. All is a Leela between God and His own eternal love. We are all part of Him. The SatGuru accepts complete control over the bhakta’s life only when the bhakta fully realizes that it is actually the SatGuru who continuously serves, loves, surrenders to, and carries His bhakta. We are nothing, all is Him alone. To forever give Him company in love and joy, the atma chooses to retain its innermost shell rather than finish its existence. This innermost shell looks exactly like Sri Hari, a tiny pure reflection of Himself in qualities. A perfect drop of the Infinite Ocean. Like this the long vigil of Maha Shivratri comes to a close, but the true Shivratri is eternal. Never stop holding vigil for Bhagavan in your heart. Many blessings to all.
