Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 5 augustus 2026, 10:33
Your sangha, the people, all beings, they are the dearest gift from God.
When you truly love, you will know. Know that opportunities to give genuine and unconditional love are so few. Where you will receive nothing from the other person or being. Where it may cost you a lot to be kind, but you do it anyway. The sangha is one such great teacher. It will become your divine mirror if you truly wish to know unconditional love and heartfelt kindness. The SatGuru gives Adesh sometimes which may seem like it is contradictory between people. But He never says to let go of your love and kindness to follow His Adesh. To follow His Adesh is of utmost importance, but if you try to do so when becoming hard of heart and mind, you have failed anyway. How you follow His Adesh can lead you to either break hearts or to become a part of His love that heals hearts. The sangha shows you that. It shows you which qualities cause pain and which comfort the soul. Which cause suffering and which cause healing. You cannot blame the sangha for being the perfect mirror. You have to learn what is the lesson for you hiding in the reflection you are seeing. No matter who one thinks oneself to be, in reality no one is greater than the other. Only the SatGuru. Only Bhagavan. He is greater. Greater than all. Everyone else is on the same level. Keep your eyes on His lotus feet always. Never doubt or be sad. Never lose hope or drop your faith in Him. He will deliver you from every pain and free you forever. But only if you hold onto the qualities that made the saints extraordinary. Those who have done wrong, trust that the SatGuru sees it. Trust in Him that He will heal the hurt and make things better than you can ever pray for. But faith only works with patience. Deep and enduring patience that allows His will to manifest in divine right time.
