“My beloved Bhagavan is the eternal beloved, the beloved of the entire world.”
This was Shrimati Radharani’s continuous realization, the source of her endless joy. In seeing Krishna loved and adored by others, she would become ecstatic with bliss. Her bhaav of endless Bhakti arose and grew in seeing Krishna being held dear in everyone’s heart. To see Him happy and well at all times was her only desire in this world. Placing her forehead into the mud, she would pray to the dust which was so fortunate to constantly touch the lotus feet of Satchitananda. That’s why He himself placed peacock feathers in her hair, gave her garlands to wear created with His own hands, became completely unable to live without His Radhe. Bhagavan captured by the sincerity of true surrender. Her deeply humble and concealed Bhakti received complete reciprocation from Murlidhar Shyamsundar, the only One who can see the inner truth that an atma aflame with love tries to hide and disguise. Long before others called Him Yugpurush, Vishnu Avatar, and Dwarikadhish, Shrimati Radharani knew Him to be all there is in this world. The one who created the indivisible tiny building blocks of creation. The one sitting at the dimensionless Bindu of the Sri Yantra that the residents of Vraj would worship in the Mandir of Yogmaya Devi. She knew that every plant, animal, rock, and river in this world had come into being to love and serve Sri Krishna. The highest level of realization, to the point of Her name and identity becoming completely merged with Sri Krishna’s, Radha Maharani received it through love. Just love. She had nothing to offer her SatGuru of the outside because He was disinterested in everything material, but She offered everything of her heart and soul to Him, the only thing He cared about—the atma wanting to be with Paramatma at all times. Loving the way He always yearns for love. May Radha Maharani teach all true Bhakti and Sharanagati to the lotus feet of Sri Hari.
