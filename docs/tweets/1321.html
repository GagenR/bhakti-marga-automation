On Baisakhi Day around 300 years ago, the tenth Sikh Guru Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji, formed the Khalsa.
Baisakhi is the day of the Punjabi New Year and the spring harvest, particularly for farmers thanking God for a prosperous crop. It was also the day when Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji prepared the way for the future by forming the Khalsa, that is the initiation of the ‘pure ones or warrior saints’. This identity held the essence of fighting oppression, upholding righteousness, and maintaining the highest spiritual and moral standards for oneself. Removing all distinctions and promoting a strictly egalitarian community. The blessing invoked and bestowed by Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji was to foster a culture of selfless service (seva) and dedication to a life centered on God (Waheguru). When a true Master is present in this world, the energy carried and spread by Him is pure divine love. Sometimes the energy needs to be fierce to conquer evil. Sometimes the energy needs to be soft to foster kindness to all. No matter in which form the energy of the SatGuru is disseminated in this world, it is always only divine love.
It is lower minds and imperfect thinking which sees distinctions and compares one master to another or one path to another. Never go into this kind of foolish thinking. Become a champion for universal peace and love as your SatGuru directs. This is the only way. The best way. On this beautiful day of Baisakhi, give thanks to God for all the blessings Waheguru always brings into your life. Love to all.
