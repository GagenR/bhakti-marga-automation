Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 14 oktober 2026, 21:25
The SatGuru is the divine tree and His bhaktas are the flowers on His eternal branches. Some flowers grow together in clusters. Some grow by themselves. Some are near the top of the tree. Some are closer to the bottom. Which is greater, which is less? Which is beautiful, which is plain? For the SatGuru, there is no such consideration. He personally grows and gives life to the flowers. From within their heart, their full potential is pushed outward. They blossom and give glory to Him. Yet He remains hidden under their shine. First, the roots, His appearance in this world. Then the trunk, His mercy and love. Then His wisdom moves in the world, the branches. Then the leaves, actions aligned with His will performed by the bhaktas. Then the flowers, with the bhaktas shining their inner spark fully. And finally, the fruits, the sweetest love of the tree is distributed to all who wish to quench their thirst and hunger. Without the life-giving tree, there can be no branches, leaves, flowers, or fruits. But the tree also feels bereft without His tiny expressions of joy and beauty, His beloved bhaktas. When they blossom, even more than them, He is happy. There is no competition in the flowers to be more beautiful than the other one. Each one exists purely for love for Him, to give meaning to their love for His feet. Can any flower be far or close to the tree? No. The thought itself doesn’t make sense. All exist completely connected with Him. Even when a flower reaches the end of its life, it gently falls down and merges completely with the soil of the roots. One is never separate. Never left alone. Never loved more or less than others. There is only the eternal love and nothing else. The same energy comes back from the roots to become a flower once again. The play of innocent unconditional love continues again and again into eternity.
