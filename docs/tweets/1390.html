What is truly worth treasuring?
Money? But no amount of it can bring true peace and happiness if there isn’t any love in your heart. Position, fame, power? But they are so fleeting, here one day and snatched away the next day. Life? But one day this body will pass away, go back to the earth from where it was born. What is truly worth treasuring and giving one’s everything to is the spiritual path. The path itself. Life and death are but adventures on the path. The path is eternal. Whether it is life or death, it is the path that is winning. The kriya of bringing the atma to Paramatma fully. Whether in this life or in another life, the spiritual path is picked up again and again. The jeevatma works on it for some time and lets go, either because of personal unwillingness to continue in that moment or because it is time to move on from that body. The second is very beautiful, because if the jeevatma has remained true to the path till departure from the body, the path amplifies its power thousand times and pushes the atma very high into the eternal realm. Either the atma resides at Bhagavan’s lotus feet never to come back again, or it comes back to work on very specific things to brighten the steps leading to the spiritual path for the world. To widen that very narrow path which becomes subtle and hides the moment one’s willingness to walk on it reduces. The spiritual path itself is energy. Pure, divine, energy. It is within itself the experience of satchitananda. The SatGuru holds this complete experience within Himself, able to freely share that energy to others. This energy knows who can walk on the path and who is not ready for the next step. It does not show up just like that in one’s life. If it has, then it is because the atma has done tremendous work in humility to attract the pure divinity of the spiritual path to itself. Letting go of the spiritual path because of unwillingness due to difficulties is the most foolish thing to do. The spiritual path is in itself one’s eternity. The eternal existence of the atma is the spiritual path. The endlessly loving relationship with Bhagavan. It is impossible to let the spiritual path go. It will be picked up when the time is right again to take the next step. But is one truly willing to wait for thousands upon thousands of years in abject misery, waiting for the spiritual path to open for oneself again?
