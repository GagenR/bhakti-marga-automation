When an atma leaves the world very suddenly, people say it was before its time, but what does that mean?
God helps everyone. He protects and takes care of everyone. No matter when the atma leaves this world, Bhagavan is there with everyone. Even if someone leaves suddenly, He will help them on their onward journey. He will help them to heal fully and realize His lotus feet in their heart. Everyone is loved and saved. Everyone can and will attain His feet. Each one moves at their speed which is known only to that atma and Paramatma. Bhagavan is the lord of unconditional love. In His heart, there is only love for all. His loving mercy and protection of every beloved atma is always present. Sometimes one can reach the ultimate goal in this life, sometimes it is waiting for one in a next life. Don’t judge anyone or worry about anything. Just do the best one can with love and kindness. And trust in Him. He knows how difficult things can get. He knows how hard the lessons are. He never leaves anyone alone. Others looking in may feel that one is not trying hard enough, but the atma knows and Bhagavan always knows. Always be filled with His love and know that He is the most merciful. There is no need to worry excessively. Trust in Him. Let the heart always be comforted in Him.
