Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zaterdag 14 februari 2026, 14:53
Everyone yearns to be something, to become ‘someone’. Even very spiritual people often hold this desire.
But for Lord Shiva, this desire is fully destroyed from within. He is the only one who desires to be nothing; he doesn’t even long to be a piece of dust in front of his Beloved SatGuru, his Bhagavan Sri Ram. To become absolutely non-existent so that his dearest Sri Hari can become everything. This isn’t the same as what foolish people think, where one tries to harm oneself to stop existing. No. It is a state of complete non-wanting. Complete self-offering. Complete self-forgetting. Lord Shiva has forgotten himself so much that he has taken on all the qualities of his Beloved Bhagavan. That’s why Hari and Hara are one. Because the bhakta fully forgets himself. To the point that Bhagavan cannot live without the Bhakta anymore. Bhagavan makes sure that his Bhakta becomes eternal, immortal, is always with Him, a pure reflection of Himself. You cannot want to be ‘something’ and have this level of love from Bhagavan. It is impossible. As long as one desires to be someone, as long as one wishes for any atom of greatness for the self, Bhagavan will still be far. There will still be barriers between oneself and Him. If you can understand the deepness of the scriptures, you will see that Lord Shiva never ever seeks glory for his own self. He is most put off by arrogance, by ahamkar. He keeps nothing for himself, while ensuring that no evil touches the glory, majesty, opulence, and position of Sri Hari. In his enormous love and humility, Lord Shiva takes care of the places everyone reviles as unclean and unsafe like crematoriums and cemeteries. He loves and protects everyone regardless of which saintly or sinful form they are in. He even accepts the most feared, despised, and unwanted job of destroying the old. So that Sri Hari can nurture, grow, protect, and sustain the good and divine. This attitude of taking every poison on himself and giving the fullness of Amrit to his Aradhya Devata, Sri Hari Dhanvantari. This is what makes Lord Shiva so humble yet so powerful. He is the one who Sri Hari has given half His heart to. And the remainder of Sri Hari’s heart holds everything else - Mahalakshmi, the bhaktas, the entire Brahmand. Who can become or replace such a being? There is no one, only Lord Shiva. Hence, in the Hari Bhakta Sampradaya, Lord Shiva is so important, so beloved, so revered.
Meditate deeply on these mantras on Maha Shivratri:
| Om Hraum Joom Sah |
| Om Namah Shivaya |
| Om Tryambakam Yajamahe Sugandhim Pushti Vardhanam Urvarukamiva Bandhanan Mrityor Mukshiya-Amamritat |
