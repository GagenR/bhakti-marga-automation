Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 31 juli 2026, 09:12
Often after a time of closeness with someone, one starts to divert one’s attention or there is a low feeling where one feels far from them.
Both feelings are two sides of the same coin. One starts to divert one’s attention because one takes the person for granted. Or, one feels distant or low after a big celebration or deep closeness with someone because one feels that now those sweet moments are over. With the world this push and pull, low and high, close and far, feelings will always happen. There is no changing that. Because that’s the nature of duality which the world exists with. But as a bhakta of the SatGuru, one should know that He is never far and the sweet moments are never over. He is always coming closer and closer to the atma. When one is low, the SatGuru picks one up and brings one close. And when one is flying high, He pricks the unreliable bubble of ego so that one doesn’t hurt oneself by falling hard. He catches one on the way down when the pride and arrogance have been cleared by Him. Never go into the feeling of distance from Him. Always search for how He is showing His closeness to you in the present moment. The outside is a temporary game, don’t give it so much importance that you ruin the relationship of the heart which is eternal. Always one is close to the SatGuru, just keep yourself close to His feet in your mind and all will forever remain well.
