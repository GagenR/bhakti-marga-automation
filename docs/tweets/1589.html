Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

dinsdag 24 november 2026, 02:14
Dhan Dhan Sri Guru Nanak Dev Ji. Ik Onkar. Satnam Waheguru. Jai Gurudev
On this sacred and blessed occasion of Gurupurab, may the infinite grace, divine light and eternal blessings of Sri SadGuru Nanak Dev Ji descend upon every heart and awaken within every atma the remembrance of its true home in the Divine. There comes a sacred moment in the journey of the soul when the presence of God is no longer something to be understood through the mind, but something that is felt in the deepest silence of one’s being. In that stillness, the heart begins to recognise a love that has existed long before this body was born, a love that will remain when all that belongs to this passing world has disappeared. The SadGuru awakens this remembrance, gently removing the veils of separation, fear, attachment and forgetfulness that have concealed the eternal light within. The journey towards God is not a journey across distance, but an awakening to the One who has always been nearer than our own breath. Sometimes His divine presence fills the heart with such extraordinary sweetness that the entire creation appears illuminated by His love. At other times, that sweetness seems to withdraw, leaving the soul in a profound longing that no worldly experience can satisfy. Yet even this longing is His grace, for it deepens surrender, purifies devotion and teaches the heart to love without demanding the comfort of constant spiritual experience. The SadGuru is present in the silence as surely as in the ecstasy, in the tears of yearning as surely as in the bliss of union. Nothing is outside His divine presence. Every sunrise, every heartbeat, every passing moment carries the hidden fragrance of the Eternal One. Through the sacred remembrance of Naam, the restless mind gradually becomes still, and the atma begins to recognise that the One it has sought through countless experiences was never separate from its own existence. Sri Guru Nanak Dev Ji revealed the eternal truth Ek Omkar, that all life arises from One Divine Reality, is sustained by that same Reality and ultimately rests within its boundless embrace. When this truth begins to flower within the heart, the boundaries created by ego slowly lose their power. One no longer sees the world merely through the divisions of mine and yours, near and far, belonging and separation. Every living being becomes worthy of compassion, every act of selfless service becomes an offering, and every encounter becomes an opportunity to recognise the sacred light dwelling within creation. The true miracle of the SadGuru is not merely that He grants moments of divine sweetness, but that He transforms the very way the soul experiences existence. He teaches us to remain humble in joy, patient in suffering, grateful in abundance and surrendered in uncertainty. He reveals that devotion is not confined to prayer or meditation alone, but is expressed through truthful living, kindness, forgiveness, selfless seva and the constant remembrance of the Divine. The heart that has tasted even a drop of this eternal love can never again be completely satisfied by the temporary pleasures of the world, for something within it has awakened to the fragrance of immortality. Its longing is no longer for possession, recognition or achievement, but for deeper intimacy with the Beloved. And as this love matures, even the desire to possess spiritual experiences begins to dissolve. The soul learns that the highest form of devotion is to belong to Him completely, without conditions, expectations or fear. The SadGuru becomes the guiding light through every season of existence, revealing that what appears to be loss may become surrender, what appears to be waiting may become purification, and what appears to be emptiness may become the sacred space in which divine grace is recognised. There is an extraordinary freedom in knowing that we do not have to hold on to God, because SriHari has never ceased holding us within His infinite presence.
