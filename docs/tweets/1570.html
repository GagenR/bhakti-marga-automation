Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zaterdag 14 november 2026, 12:42
The one who has even a little understanding of true devotional love begins to discover a mystery beyond the understanding of the restless mind. For when the heart is genuinely touched by divine love, what more is there to seek, and what greater treasure could one desire? Just as a drop of water merges into the ocean and loses its separate existence, so does the soul, through unwavering love and complete surrender to the Satguru, come to realise its inseparable relationship with the Supreme Lord. O beloved people of Sri Hari, do not mistake Vishwananda for someone wise, for he is but a simple-minded fool, sharing only what little he has come to understand through grace. The path is before you, but the willingness to walk it must arise from within. If you desire freedom from the endless entanglements of Maya, you must first become watchful of your own mind, for it is often the mind that creates divisions where there is none, doubts where there should be faith, and attachments that keep the soul bound to the transient world. Ego, pride and the desire for recognition are burdens that conceal the beauty of your true nature. What is the value of accumulating worldly importance when none of it can accompany you beyond this temporary existence? Do not spend your precious life trying to become someone in the eyes of the world; instead, learn to become nothing before the Lord, so that His divine presence may shine through you. The Satguru does not merely show you the way; through his grace, he awakens within you the longing to discover the Truth that has always been present. Look beyond appearances and recognise the sacred presence of Sri Hari within your own heart. Do not confine your devotion to the temple, the altar or the moments of prayer, for every encounter is an opportunity to recognise and honour the Divine. When you serve another with compassion, when you forgive without resentment, when you offer kindness without expecting anything in return, and when you see beyond another person’s faults, your devotion becomes a living offering to the Lord. Never imagine that God belongs only to those who worship in a particular way, for the same divine presence sustains all beings. The flower, the tree, the river, the stranger and even the one who tests your patience are all part of His creation. Let your heart become so filled with love that you no longer look for reasons to separate yourself from others. True devotion is not measured by how much we speak about God, but by how deeply His love transforms the way we live. Surrender is not weakness; it is the freedom that comes when we stop allowing the ego to govern our existence. Let go, love sincerely, serve selflessly and walk with humility. For the ultimate realisation is not merely to know that Sri Hari is everywhere, but to see Him, honour Him and love Him in all that exists. There is no place where He is absent, no heart beyond His reach and no creation outside His divine presence.
