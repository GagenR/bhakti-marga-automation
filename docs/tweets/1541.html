Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 1 november 2026, 12:31
My beloved Sri Vithala Giridhari, I am fully satisfied and eternally satiated, nourished by the sacred food of Your Divine Reality. With my mind, body, and tongue, I meditate upon Your Holy Name, the Name of Sri Vithala Giridhari, for Your Name is the essence of my existence, the breath of my soul, and the eternal refuge of my heart. O my Lord, our very life rests in You; without You, nothing has meaning, and in You, everything finds its fulfilment. Our spiritual life consists of ceaselessly chanting Your Divine Name at every moment and in every circumstance, especially in the holy company of the Guru’s beloved Bhaktas, whose hearts overflow with devotion and whose lives are surrendered to Your lotus feet. Blessed is the soul who, regardless of the robes he wears or the outward appearance he bears, sings the glorious Kirtan of Sri Hari day and night, allowing the sweetness of Your Divine Praises to flow endlessly from his heart. Such a soul understands that true holiness is not found in outward appearances, but in the sincerity of love, the purity of surrender, and the unbroken remembrance of the Beloved Lord. Whoever discovers the sacred path of Sri Hari within the innermost sanctuary of the heart rides upon the celestial chariot of divine bliss, transcending worldly sorrows and resting in the boundless ocean of Your grace. O Vithala Giridhari, meditating upon Your lotus feet deep within the mind and soul, Vishwananda has discovered the greatest treasure of all: the priceless wealth of divine love, the unshakable peace of surrender, and the boundless joy of belonging entirely to You. O my beloved Lord, may Your Holy Name forever dwell upon my tongue, may Your Divine Form remain engraved upon my heart, and may every breath I take become an offering of love at Your sacred feet. I desire no treasure greater than Your remembrance, no companionship sweeter than that of Your devotees, and no destination more precious than the eternal shelter of Your Divine Love. Let my mind become absorbed in You, let my heart beat only for You, and let my entire existence become a continuous Kirtan of Your glory. You are my beginning and my end, my nourishment and my fulfilment, my refuge in sorrow and my joy in happiness. O Sri Vithala Giridhari, having found You within the innermost depths of my heart, I have found everything; having surrendered everything to You, I have nothing left to seek, for in Your Divine Love, my soul has found its eternal home.
