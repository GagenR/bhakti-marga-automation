Through the divine grace of the SadGuru, the heart discovers a peace that transcends the changing nature of the world. When the soul encounters the Guru who awakens its love for Sri Hari, a new light dawns within, bringing serenity, fulfilment, and the joy of spiritual awakening. By the SadGuru’s boundless mercy, the chains of ignorance begin to fall away, and the path to liberation opens before the devoted soul. Immersed in loving service to the SadGuru and the Supreme #
Lord
, the heart becomes a sanctuary where the celestial #
music
of divine bliss plays without end. Having taken shelter at the sacred lotus feet of Sri Hari, all reliance upon fleeting worldly support gradually fades, for one has discovered the everlasting refuge of Giridhari, the beloved Lord and Life of the universe. Filled with divine love, the soul sings in gratitude, glorifying the SadGuru and Sri Hari, whose compassion releases the devotee from fear and the relentless bonds of mortality. The deepest longing is fulfilled, for wherever the eyes behold creation, the presence of the #
Beloved
shines through all things. There is no refuge greater than Sri Hari, no truth beyond His eternal presence, and no grace more precious than the mercy that draws the soul towards Him. Through the compassionate guidance of the SadGuru and the protecting hand of Sri Hari, the accumulated burdens of countless births are washed away, and the heart is freed from the sorrow of its long wandering through Maya. O mind, remain absorbed in His sacred remembrance, for the Divine Name carries a power beyond the understanding of the intellect and awakens the eternal love hidden within the soul. By meditating upon Sri Vithala Giridhari Parabrahmane Namaha, one turns towards the #
Supreme
Reality, discovering a bliss that neither time can diminish nor worldly circumstances disturb. Blessed is the SadGuru, who reveals the way to the Lord; blessed is Sri Vithala Giridhari, whose grace sustains every soul. May His Name dwell upon our lips, His presence shine within our hearts, and His love guide our every step until we rest forever in the supreme bliss of the Divine.
