Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

maandag 9 november 2026, 11:47
The grace of the SadGuru is not obtained through idle words, outward appearances or the mere repetition of religious expressions. It is through Divine Grace alone that the soul becomes worthy of receiving such a sacred blessing. One may speak endlessly of devotion, yet without humility, surrender and a heart yearning for God, how can the fragrance of true Bhakti blossom within? Some are born into this world, and others depart from it, yet what magnificent titles they give themselves, how proudly they proclaim their importance, and how tightly they cling to the honours bestowed upon them by men. Some enter this world in poverty, while others are born amidst royal splendour, wealth and greatness; yet before the final hour, what difference remains between them? The grave does not recognise titles, the flames do not distinguish between wealth and poverty, and the dust makes no distinction between those who once commanded kingdoms and those who possessed nothing. O dear soul, do not wait until the evening of your life to discover the truth that was always before your eyes. When youthful beauty fades, when strength abandons the body, when those whom you once called friends turn away, and when everything you held dear slips through your fingers, you may finally begin to understand how fragile and uncertain this earthly existence truly is. The applause of the world eventually falls silent, the crowds disperse, and the names once spoken with admiration gradually disappear from human memory. What then remains of a life spent seeking recognition, accumulating possessions and feeding the pride of the ego? If the heart has never known the intimate love of Sri Hari, if the soul has never surrendered itself to the SadGuru, then all the treasures of this world are of little account. O Sri Hari, in reverent awe and loving surrender to You, may I dwell every moment of my existence. Let my body grow weary in Your remembrance rather than become intoxicated by worldly pleasures; let my heart pine for Your presence rather than seek the approval of men. I have witnessed how those who were once considered powerful, influential and indispensable eventually leave this earthly realm, and their bodies return to ashes, mingling with the dust and clay from which they were formed. Their possessions pass into other hands, their positions are occupied by others, and the world continues as though they had never commanded its attention. This is the impermanence we so often refuse to contemplate. Therefore, let us not become prisoners of false attachments, for everything that is not rooted in Divine Love shall eventually be separated from us. Vishwananda reminds all of the importance of turning the heart towards God and allowing divine love to become the centre of our existence. O dear SadGuru-pyares, do not mistake the temporary affection of this world for the eternal love of the Divine. Love without possessing, serve without expecting, give without seeking praise, and surrender without keeping anything hidden from the Lord. Let the ego be humbled before the lotus feet of Sri Hari, and let every passing day bring the soul closer to the eternal truth. For when the final curtain of this earthly journey falls, it is not the greatness we claimed for ourselves that shall matter, but the love we cultivated, the compassion we extended, the faith we preserved and the grace we sought. Blessed is the soul that discovers this truth before its final hour, for it has found the treasure that neither death nor time can take away: the everlasting love of Sri Hari and the grace of the SadGuru.
