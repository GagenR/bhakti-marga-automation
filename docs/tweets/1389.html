Everyone is fighting a fight that no one knows about.
Never cast your opinion on a person or situation, because the pain behind their actions will also be attracted to you. When you judge or are jealous of someone, the suffering that brought them to the place they are at comes to you amplified. Everyone is fighting a fight that no one sees. What may seem so small to someone may be the result of backbreaking effort for someone else. Yet they are doing it. Pushing themselves internally. Forcing themselves to stand up when it would be so easy to just crumble. This is what amazes the SatGuru about the bhaktas. The true lovers of Bhagavan stand up when there is no outside motivation or reason to stand up. But they do it anyway. Bringing forth the enormous strength from Bhagavan’s presence in their hearts. The SatGuru’s greatest work and joy is to see someone blossoming in that way. Some pain is seen on the outside. But some hide their pain completely. They never show it to anyone. Don’t assume because someone is very positive or always looks successful that they have no fights that they are fighting. You don’t know how much their ankles might be weighed down, but they are giving it their every breath to fight to the surface of that muddy swamp and radiate joy, love, and peace to the world. Learn from the stories of the devotees. Learn from their amazing lives. Be inspired and walk with full force on your own unique path to Bhagavan. Know that the SatGuru’s entire love is with you. He loves each one as though they are His only child. The saints were also regular people. They also faced hard choices, karma, pain, and sorrow. The difference between a saint and a regular person is simply in how they take life. How they handle everything. How they react to things. How they keep themselves inside. How they treat everyone even when in deep pain themselves. It is the attitude that makes a saint or a sinner or a worldly person. When you transform your attitude, all can transform. All can become holy. All can be divinized while here in this world itself.
