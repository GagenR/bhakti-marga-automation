Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

dinsdag 24 november 2026, 11:59
Today is also Guru Tegh Bahadur Shaheedi Diwas (his martyrdom ). No matter how severe the persecution, suffering or threats against his life, Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji neither renounced his faith nor compromised his spiritual principles. With unwavering courage and boundless compassion, he stood in defence of the Kashmiri Pandits and their right to practise their faith. He willingly sacrificed his own life to safeguard the religious freedom and dignity of others, demonstrating that true spirituality transcends the boundaries of religion, caste and creed. His supreme martyrdom stands as an eternal testimony to selfless love, righteousness, fearlessness and unshakable devotion to God. Through his sacrifice, he taught humanity that true strength lies not in earthly power, but in remaining steadfast in truth, even in the face of death. One whose consciousness is deeply anchored in God cannot be conquered by fear, oppression or the impermanence of the physical body. Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji revealed the profound meaning of surrender to God. His life exemplified the state of a God realised soul who remains undisturbed by pleasure or pain, honour or dishonour, gain or loss. Having transcended attachment to the physical existence, he demonstrated that the freedom of the soul is far greater than any worldly freedom and that no external force can enslave a heart that belongs entirely to God. His sacrifice was not motivated by hatred towards his persecutors, but by an immense love for humanity. He taught us that genuine devotion is not confined to prayers, rituals or outward expressions of faith; it must manifest through our actions, our compassion towards others and our readiness to uphold righteousness, even when doing so demands personal sacrifice. To recognise the presence of God in every being is also to honour their dignity and their freedom to approach the Divine according to their own faith. Vishwananda prays that the sacred remembrance of Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji may inspire us all to look sincerely within ourselves and reflect upon the depth of our loyalty, sincerity and devotion to the SadGuru and one path in life. How firmly do we hold on to God when confronted with difficulties? How easily do we allow worldly attachments, fear and personal interests to overshadow our spiritual convictions? Are we willing to live according to the divine teachings we profess to believe in, even when our circumstances become unfavourable? May we develop the inner strength to overcome our ego, rise above selfishness and surrender ourselves more fully to the Divine Will. May our faith not depend upon favourable circumstances, nor our love for God diminish when we encounter suffering. May we understand that the spiritual path is not merely about seeking divine protection for ourselves, but also about becoming instruments of God’s love, mercy and protection for others.
May the sacred remembrance of his martyrdom awaken our conscience, purify our intentions and deepen our longing for God. May his timeless example inspire generations to live with humility, integrity, courage and unconditional love, recognising that the highest expression of devotion is to live not merely for oneself, but for the welfare of all creation. With deepest love, gratitude and reverence, countless pranams at the holy lotus feet of Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Sahib Ji, whose supreme sacrifice remains an immortal light guiding humanity towards truth, righteousness and divine love. Dhan Dhan Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Sahib Ji. Hind Di Chadar the protector of religious freedom and human dignity.
