Many people know about love, very few people behave with love.
This is very similar to what Duryodhan says in the Mahabharat. “I know what is dharma but I do not act accordingly. I know what is adharma but I cannot stop myself from acting that way.” People always think that they are the best and others are the problem. But how many people can truly look in the mirror and meet their own eyes without feeling guilty, ashamed, or regretful of the way they have behaved, spoken, or thought? Not many. Because most people allow the negative qualities to grow within them. They feed the wolf within that likes to take one away from love, not the one who takes one to love. Whichever wolf you feed will become powerful and active within you. But does one truly behave with love? This is a question to be asked and answered within yourself every day. “Am I genuinely behaving with love or am I only thinking about love while my actions, words, and thoughts are still stuck in my own greatness?” Sometimes the ones who are acting the most loving on the outside, are also the ones who are most hypocritical inside. They preach to others and point out their faults, but never turn the scalpel of their judgement on themselves. The SatGuru has never given anyone the work of correcting others. He has always given everyone the work of correcting themselves. If you wish to be a leader then lead by example, not by words. And if you wish simply to love, then stop worrying about anything else. Just behave with love in every moment of your life. Such a life will be its own greatest reward, without even counting the infinite closeness one will achieve with Bhagavan through this. Bhagavan is only won over by those who love humbly all the time and serve all joyfully without judgement.
