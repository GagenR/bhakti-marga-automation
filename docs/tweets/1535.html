Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 25 oktober 2026, 23:24
Life is a journey of changing seasons, and the wisdom of spirituality lies in learning to walk through each season with faith, patience, humility, and devotion. When happiness comes, receive it with gratitude, remember Sri Hari, share your blessings, and never allow prosperity or success to make you forget the divine grace behind everything you have. When difficulties arise, do not lose faith or allow sorrow to distance your heart from the Lord. Hold firmly to His divine name, seek the guidance of the SadGuru, and surrender your worries at His feet. Life will not always unfold according to our wishes, but every experience can become an opportunity for spiritual growth. Joy teaches gratitude, hardship teaches surrender, and uncertainty teaches us to trust in the divine will. The SadGuru reminds us that our true strength does not come from controlling every circumstance, but from remaining connected to Sri Hari through every circumstance. Do not become so attached to happiness that you fear its passing, nor so overwhelmed by suffering that you forget the eternal presence of God. Everything in this world is temporary, but devotion, faith, and the grace of the Lord are the treasures of the soul. Walk forward with a peaceful heart, perform your duties sincerely, treat others with compassion, and accept life’s changing moments without losing your spiritual foundation. Let every blessing inspire humility, every challenge deepen your faith, and every breath become an opportunity to remember Sri Hari.
SadGuru Kripa. Sri Hari Sharanam.
