Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

With the SatGuru, one learns from within the heart that God is just as much here in the imperfect world as He is in the perfection of everything, Vaikuntha.
Often people distance God by thinking, saying, and behaving as though He is only in some perfect place and here in the imperfect world, He is less available or not really available at all. But this thinking is not true at the highest level. God is everywhere and in everything. In His fullness. Always. This world seems imperfect because one is looking through an imperfect lens of the mind. But the enlightened saints never thought that this world is not good. They could see the absolute perfection of the world. Even what others judged and hated, the saints loved and worshiped. Because they could only see Bhagavan. In the imperfection as well as in perfection. And truly He is. Not because He ever becomes imperfect, but rather He wears the cloaks of imperfections so that love can truly become unconditional. This world is God enjoying God’s love. It is always Him experiencing an aspect of His own self in everyone. He sees His own love wearing infinite cloaks of perfection and imperfection. And because He is always growing, that love through the play of imperfect and perfect, becomes purer and more beautiful through every moment. Never look down on this world. It is filled with extraordinary love, as a perfect stage where the jeevatmas can submerge fully in their egoistic role or be fully aware of their true identity as a tiny spark of Bhagavan. Submerged in one’s ego or submerged in God’s endless love and joy. You choose. And you do choose, in every moment. When you become aware of that choice, then your experience of perfection can grow.
