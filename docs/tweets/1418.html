Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zaterdag 15 augustus 2026, 22:50
On the day that Bharat received independence, wishing everyone the joy of true freedom and self-governance.
It has been about 80 years since Bharat became free from long centuries of oppression and robbery. So much wealth, knowledge, and many people have been lost to the fight for self-governance. The principles of the Sanatan Dharma have still withstood the test of time. Because even if history is attempted to be rewritten. Even if thoughts and actions have been forcibly controlled for long ages. Even if the land was plundered endlessly. The basic truth of life as a human doesn’t change. There are certain characteristics that allow life to progress beautifully and certain ones that cause problems in that. The distinction between positive inner qualities and negative ones is simple. How those qualities create virtuous or vicious actions in the world is also simple to see. But one has to self-govern to realize one’s true potential individually and as a society. Self-governance requires true inner vigilance and mastery over one’s actions, yet not instigating violence or selfishly expanding one’s ‘resources’ at the cost of others. The Sanatan Dharma, the eternal way of life, is filled with endless diversity. Only that which is purely selfish on the inside is pruned away, to let the entire tree of life grow well. Self-governance and freedom is a gift that is earned more and more from within. For example, can one transform the self and bring love and light to the world, rather than arguing, spreading hate, and pulling others down? As bhaktas of the Sanatan way of life, don’t let this day just pass by, contemplate on what it means to have true freedom and what a beautiful gift it is. Happy Independence Day to Bharat and to all followers of her timeless wisdom.
