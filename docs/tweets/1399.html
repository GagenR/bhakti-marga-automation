Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 26 juli 2026, 19:06
With the help of our Tadatmya Vedanta, one can experience that understanding the SatGuru is impossible but that Love transcends all.
The SatGuru is the master of holding and letting go. He knows with perfect clarity when to pick something up and when to set it down. When to introduce a thought. When to set down an idea to be picked up later. Which action needs to be performed or stopped when. How someone’s path back to Bhagavan looks in its divine purity. Diving into our Tadatmya Vedanta, one can see that there are so many different roles the SatGuru plays. He gives the balance between gyaan and bhakti. The balance between dhyaan and karma yoga; outward and inward action. The balance between doing and surrendering. Without the SatGuru, it is impossible to walk on the spiritual path or even to just live life. Because He is the One who knows why life is the way it is. No matter what perception one has of the SatGuru, no matter how one expresses their love for Him, He accepts all. Because He is beyond any philosophy or intellectual understanding. Yet He gives countless methods to grow closer to Him in understanding and wisdom. He reminds everyone to love constantly and without end. Love the wisdom that can be received on the path. Love the experiences whether pleasant or unpleasant, planned or unplanned. Love the people whether easy or difficult to do so. Just love. The SatGuru is giving the absolute highest with no other message mixed in. He doesn’t say just love and something else. No. He says just love. Because love is that balance between all things. Love is the great connector of everything. Love is the only thing that can be in all, the foundation of all, and the destination of all. Yet beyond all is also only Love. The SatGuru is love itself. He has only that to give to all. Go deeper into your studies of our path so that your experience of Him can become purer and purer. You can know His sweetest and most easy to access truth, Love. You can know His most difficult to grasp and impossible to attain truth, Love.
