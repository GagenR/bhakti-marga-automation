THE GREATEST OFFERING IS SURRENDER
Surrender does not mean weakness. It means releasing the illusion that we control everything. We can make plans, but cannot command every outcome. We can love, but cannot possess. We can act, but must learn to release attachment to the fruits of our actions. Tonight, let us place our fears, regrets, pride and burdens before the Mother. Let our prayer be simple: “Mā, remove whatever prevents me from loving God with my whole heart.”
A GARLAND MADE OF AWARENESS
The Mother wears a garland of heads. Let us offer her a garland of our own: every bead of the mālā a moment of remembrance, every repetition of the Divine Name an offering of love, every conscious breath a step towards God. The mind will wander; some beads will slip by unnoticed. That is all right. A mother does not demand perfection from her child. She asks only for sincerity. As Kālī Chaudas gives way to Dīpāvalī, let us remember that the greatest light is not merely the lamp outside our homes, but the light of understanding within our hearts. May Mā Kālī give us the courage to let go, Śrī Kṛṣṇa the wisdom to see, and the Divine Mother the grace to awaken. May we move beyond ego towards humility, beyond fear towards faith, and beyond attachment towards divine love. When the night passes, our outer circumstances may remain unchanged. But if even one attachment loosens, one moment of anger gives way to compassion, or one thought turns sincerely towards God, the night has fulfilled its purpose.
May our lives become a continuous offering at the feet of the Divine.
