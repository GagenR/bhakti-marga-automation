Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 20 november 2026, 12:11
Sant Tukaram Maharaj’s longing for his beloved Vitthal reveals one of the deepest mysteries of Prema Bhakti. Though he lived in constant remembrance of Bhagavan and experienced His divine grace, his thirst for Him only became more intense. The more he tasted the sweetness of divine love, the more his heart yearned for the presence of his beloved Lord. This is the sacred hunger of the atma, a longing that no worldly happiness, pleasure, or achievement can ever satisfy, because the soul belongs eternally to Bhagavan.
In Viraha Bhakti, even the pain of separation becomes a sacred fire that purifies the heart, dissolving pride, selfish desires, and worldly attachments. The soul begins to realise that its greatest poverty is not the absence of material possessions, but the absence of conscious communion with its beloved Lord. Nothing in creation can replace the sweetness of His presence. Vishwananda teaches, the true purpose of life is to awaken our eternal loving relationship with Bhagavan. Through the grace of the SatGuru, this divine longing is awakened within the heart, transforming ordinary devotion into complete surrender. The SatGuru guides the atma beyond superficial worship, revealing that true Bhakti is not merely performing rituals or repeating prayers, but allowing divine love to become the very essence of our existence.The closer the devotee comes to Bhagavan, the more deeply he recognises the greatness of His compassion and the limitations of his own love. He approaches the Lord with humility, longing only to rest his head upon His Lotus Feet. He no longer asks for worldly blessings, spiritual powers, or even liberation. His only prayer becomes, “My Lord, let me never forget You. Let me belong entirely to You.” Bhagavan does not demand perfection before accepting us. He knows our weaknesses, mistakes, ignorance, and countless imperfections, yet His compassion remains greater than our shortcomings. Through the loving guidance of the SatGuru, we learn that surrender is not about becoming worthy through our own efforts, but about opening our hearts to the grace that has always been waiting for us.This is the beauty of Tukaram Maharaj’s longing. His hunger for Vitthal was not born from a lack of faith, but from the overwhelming intensity of divine love. Even the remembrance of Bhagavan became nourishment for his soul, while the desire to see Him grew stronger with every breath.Blessed is the atma that experiences even a glimpse of this sacred yearning. For when His Name becomes our nourishment, His remembrance our breath, His love our greatest treasure, and His Lotus Feet our eternal refuge, we begin to understand the true meaning of Prema Bhakti. As Vishwananda continually emphasises, our deepest purpose is to realise this eternal bond of love with Bhagavan, until nothing remains in the heart but the desire to love Him, serve Him, and belong to Him forever.
