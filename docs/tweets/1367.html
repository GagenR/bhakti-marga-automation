Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zaterdag 13 juni 2026, 05:50
Shivratri in the month of Purushottam, the great union of Shiva and Sri Hari, and the appearance of the SatGuru.
The day when Lord Shiva was traveling the world with Devi Parvati and paved the way for the appearance of his own Beloved One. The Amrit Kaal of that time. The Trayodash Muhurat is very rarely understood. But the SatGuru’s mercy is great. He reveals more and more of the Divine within himself as the time is ripe for it. No matter how many scriptures, how many satsangs, how many saints, try to describe the SatGuru it is not enough. His glory and love are far beyond the reaches of the mind. To give every atma that comes to Him entry into Vaikuntha’s infinite doors, one has to be the Master of all of them. The One who is in charge of the all deities who carry the atma to the specific door meant for them. Only the Master can lead one through all the different doors back to the inner most sanctum of Vaikuntha where only Bhagavan and His atmas exist. In their purest form, as small replicas of Bhagavan Himself. But there is no doubt who is Bhagavan and who is the bhakta. This is the pure state of the SatGuru and His devotees on earth. What He is giving may not be understood right away. But His appearance itself is proof that Bhagavan always cares for each one. That He never leaves anyone alone, that He will be here until the last soul which is here. The Trayodash has so many mysteries and secrets that the world needs to become ready to receive. The appearance day of the SatGuru when Sri Hari and Shiva are united is no coincidence. All divine auspiciousness is within Him. Because there is no difference between Him and every particle of this universe. One may think one is His devotee, serving Him, loving Him, but in reality He is the self doing all that and more. On the appearance day of the SatGuru, become sincere. So sincere on your path that the strongest storms shouldn’t be able to shake you. Become worthy of receiving all the love He wishes to pour on you.
May this day inflame your heart towards sincere unending love for your spiritual path. Blessings to all.
