Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

In the world, all of one’s feelings are diluted and distracted. But with God, they become one-pointed with great intensity.
The spiritual path leads one to the Supreme Bhagavan, the One who is beyond good and evil, the Master of all. It is not at all easy to stop being distracted and sending one’s energy in thousand directions. This is why the spiritual path takes many lifetimes to walk on. Because one is always interacting with God; this world, this creation is Him Himself but with infinite different levels of interaction with Him. At the lowest levels, one tries to impose one’s will onto the Divine, one wants control. The more control one wants, the more one interacts with the world. Because more and more things are needed to feel a higher sense of control. But this leads to less and less feeling of interaction with God. Because He removes His will and becomes more and more subtle and dormant. But as one wishes to interact with Him more directly and clearly, where His will becomes known with clarity. Where one wishes to align with and follow His will more. Then one lets go of trying to control everything. Slowly, one surrenders the mind - where most of the control lies with the self. Then one surrenders all fears and worries of the external reality, where one is in control approximately the same level as Bhagavan. And finally, one meets His complete self in the world, the SatGuru, where one is not in control at all, only God is in control. All these experiences or feelings at different levels are great gifts from God. But if one wishes to know Bhagavan’s love fully and surrender the self into His merciful hands, then the SatGuru is the greatest friend one can have, not the mind nor the external world. Because only true and pure love from the deepest depths of oneself works to foster a relationship with Him, which is the same as surrendering to God’s will within and outside. No fear, no control, no anger, no disappointment, no over excitement, nothing, no energy other than unconditional Love can move the SatGuru. And this level of surrender and love is very difficult to achieve but extraordinarily rewarding. Because one dwells in the state where Bhagavan reveals Himself fully. One aspect, one facet, one quality, one situation, at a time. Like the ocean surrendering to the drop, step by step. This marvelous state of being requires everything from the self to be forgotten, and only God remain.
