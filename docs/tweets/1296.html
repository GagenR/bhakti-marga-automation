Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 26 maart 2026, 13:20
Navami tithi has started. The number for completion, nine. Purnatva.
Sri Ram is that Purna Tattva. The birth of the Avatar is not bound by anything external. The scriptures note that Sri Ram was born when the sun was at its peak in the sky on Chaitra Navami tithi. Some say Satyayug, some say Tretayug.
Some say that it wasn’t in this Mahayug but an earlier one. Some say that it happens in every Mahayug. Who is right? Who is wrong? Sri Ram is born in the heart. For some, He is bonded more and more firmly to their atma in every moment of their life. For others, it takes hundreds of births to care about Him for a single moment. Then when is His true birth date, time, place, and occasion? Was He born in your heart when you went to search for Him in the Mandir? Were you overwhelmed by His love when you bowed to his Vigraha? Did your atma feel like escaping every clutch and shackle to return to His side fully? The outside celebration is a beautiful reminder. A reminder that if you aren’t moved by Ram in your heart, then you cannot see Him move from just being a Murti to the eternal, living, breathing Sri Ram who is walking this earth even today. Bhagavan always keeps things fresh for the mind. Hence, the need to continuously breathe for Him. If you have settled into complacency, you won’t understand His leelas.
This year, Bhagavan’s date and time of appearance are a great mystery. He is beyond the control of the planets. Beyond the movement of the stars. He can make the celestial phenomena for His advent come together in an instant, and also wait for endless yugas for the perfect alignment of all things. Bind your mind to His feet and you will see that He is the unbound, unknown, dearest of dear Great Cause of this world.
