Shubh Akshaya Tritiya.
Akshaya means never-diminishing, everlasting. It is one of the days in the Hindu calendar that is a complete Muhurat, which means that every second of this tithi is auspicious to start new ventures.
In India, people often wait for this day to buy auspicious things, especially gold and other precious items. Until one comes to the inner realization and complete knowing that every moment is an auspicious blessing from God, till then such dates are important reminders for doing good deeds.
On this day, Sudama’s poverty was completely removed by Bhagavan Krishna. Bhagavan Krishna also gave the Akshaya Patra, the pot of never-ending food, to Draupadi. Devi Parvati appeared in her form as Annapurna Ma, the one who makes sure that all her children have enough and the right food to eat. The sixth Avatar, Bhagavan Parashuram, appeared on earth on this day. There are endless more auspicious events that have taken place on Akshaya Tritiya throughout the ages. It is very important to do charity, distribute food, clothes, and water to the needy. Plant trees and protect the environment on this day. Help spread the awareness of vegetarianism and love for all beings. Make sure to remember Bhagavan Sri Hari and thank Him for the blessings in your life. But most of all, learn to love. Love from the bottom of your heart so that the true Akshaya Patra, the never-ending pot of unconditional love within your heart, is constantly overflowing. Your joy, gratitude, and love should be felt to their fullest and more within you, so that they can naturally overflow to others. The world itself is benefited by the presence of people who are genuinely happy and grateful for everything in their lives.
On Akshaya Tritiya, remember the lotus feet of the satguru and make the strong determination to reach the ultimate goal of life in this lifetime itself. Offer the golden energy of that determination to the SatGuru’s feet and know that he is always helping you. This is the real gold that one should buy on this day. The gold of one’s absolute determination to transform everything in one’s life into unconditional love.
Blessings to everyone.
