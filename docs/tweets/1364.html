Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 11 juni 2026, 13:48
As the Antar Guru awakens more and more, the brilliance of Surya Narayan and Devi radiate outward.
Light and heat, Surya dev and Divine Mother. They are both born from the Hiranyagarbha, which is the SatGuru, the golden womb of Bhagavan. The SatGuru is the Hiranyagarbha, always holding Bhagavan in the purest form; capable of bringing Him into the manifest world and making His divine potencies dormant where the ego rises. Surya dev and Divine Mother are both part incarnations of Bhagavan himself. These two Shaktis emanating from the SatGuru’s heart help creation to continue. It is only through the right balance of heat and light that life can be properly maintained. They are two sides of the same coin, always helping each other. For example, Chhathi Maiya, who is an aspect of Devi (Katyayani/Shashthi), is revered as the spiritual sister of Surya dev. Or, worshipping the Gayatri Mantra honors the illumination of Surya Narayan and the wisdom of the Divine Mother simultaneously. After the divine roar that destroys all ego and the great joy of seeing the Antaryami SatGuru manifest, His brilliance starts to shine through the bhakta. The light of wisdom and the warmth of compassion radiate through the bhakta. The less the bhakta is present, the more the SatGuru is present. If one wrestles for control or glory, He starts to hide again. His Prem is manifest when the fully illumined atma humbly lowers itself in Sharanagati to the lotus feet of the SatGuru. Enlightenment and Sharanagati are both gradual processes.
What inner understanding has unfolded deeper love in your heart on this day? What will be your step towards Sharanagati today?
