Today is also Mahakali Jayanti.
The twin energies of the universe, Bhagavan and Divine Mother, both entered the world at exactly midnight on this tithi. Both always come and go together. There is no Sadhana that is complete without both of them being loved and worshiped. They are One. She came to protect the innocent and destroy the evil. He came to light the way and illuminate divine love within all. When the world becomes filled with Adharma, one of them works behind the scenes and one works for all to see, to clean up lower energies and bring everything back to its highest expression of unconditional love. Ma Kali appeared in Gokul as the daughter of Yashoda Maiya and Nand Baba. Sri Krishna appeared in the jail of Mathura as the son of Maharani Devaki and Maharaj Vasudev. Then they switched places and created the most divine net of love around the innocents who knew only to love and love endlessly. Sri Krishna could have easily taken birth in Gokul directly, and Ma Kali in the prison to destroy Kamsa’s tyranny. But they didn't do that. They showed the world that love holds no barriers. It doesn’t matter where or how one takes birth, love brings one to the correct place to perform one’s highest Dharma. Divinity is present everywhere. Their plan was perfect. They answered every call of the bhaktas and showed the glory of divine love. Ma Kali appeared and disappeared within the same night. But She remained in Her invisible form around Vraj. Protecting Sri Krishna and all the residents. Even when hiding so perfectly, She was still seen by the gopis and worshiped as the one who could grant their wish to never be separated from Sri Krishna. Eyes of love can see the Divine even when it tries to hide. Shubh Ma Kali Jayanti to all.
