Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 4 september 2026, 12:10
Th day when we celebrate one of the most beautiful lives ever lived. Shubh Sri Krishna Janmashtami.
The one who gave the world timeless words of love and wisdom throughout His life. The one who fulfilled His eternal promise of being close to those who love Him, all throughout the world in all ages. The one who is the best part of every heart. Who was a friend to the weak, a champion of Dharma, a joyful companion, an ever-loving brother. Words are never enough to describe the glorious life of Vasudev Sri Krishna. His very name translates to ‘the all-attractive one’. Not only is he Shyamsundar of extraordinary beauty but He is also Atma Ramana, the one who abides in the Self and gives endless bliss to the atma. The one who incarnated more than five thousand years ago and the one who is ever-present in this world. He is just as present for His devotees now as He was then. One just has to search for Him in the right way, in the right place. Search with love within the heart and one will always find Him. Born at midnight when all was dark, Sri Krishna became the light of the world. When heaviness spread across the land and the flames of war were ignited by evil-minded ones, Sri Krishna became the beacon of support and love for all. Yet for Himself, he never asked for anything, never worked for himself, keeping always others in His merciful heart. He is the innate positivity that attracts all other positive qualities. He is the in-dweller of the soul, the cosmic being that created and encompasses everything, and the one who appears beside His bhaktas when they sincerely call for Him. Every precious drop of Bhakti is Sri Krishna himself. Each moment of His life should be meditated upon.
