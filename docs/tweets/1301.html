Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 27 maart 2026, 14:29
A new sun has awoken with the arrival of Sri Ram. Shubh Ram Navami.
Sri Ram’s advent in this world and Ma Siddhidatri’s endless blessings come together. Just gazing upon the face of the newborn Sri Ram, all the citizens of Ayodhya experienced the ending of all their thoughts, all emotions became concentrated on that tiny form, all desires got attached to this one small life smiling at them from his cradle. People often wonder what the Asht Siddhis and Nav Nidhis are. How to obtain these spiritual attainments. But the citizens of Ayodhya received them spontaneously. Because Bhagavan came with his entire divinity fully and openly in Darshan. The Asht Siddhis and Nav Nidhis were naturally pouring out from his tiny body. Slowly over time these manifestations would become more and more subtle. He would hide his true self more and more. Only those who truly wanted him, with maddening love, with complete abandon, only they would see his majestic divinity. When Ma Sita, Siddhidatri Devi, Kali Ma, fused as one, She bestowed on Hanuman the same completeness that was so openly manifest at the time of Ram Lalla’s birth, purely because of Hanuman’s endless devotion to Sri Ram. Your life too should become a Gatha of bhakti, a beautiful story of bhakti. Take inspiration from the saints, ask Hanumanji for help, plead with Bhagavan that He is born in your heart. Make sure your heart holds the newborn Sri Ram with the greatest care and with complete attention on Him. To be born as a human celebrating the birth of Bhagavan upon this earth. Understand how exceptional this life is. Your life has been given to you to wake up into complete unconditional love. As the sun performs Tilak on Sri Ram Lalla’s forehead in Ayodhya, make sure the sun of your bhakti is shining just as brightly. Blessings to all.
