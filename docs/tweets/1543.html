Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

dinsdag 3 november 2026, 19:41
O mind, why do you allow yourself to be deceived by the passing attractions of this world? Why wander endlessly in pursuit of things that bring only momentary pleasure, when the time will soon come for you to leave everything behind? This human birth is precious, yet its duration is uncertain. Every passing moment brings us closer to that inevitable hour when we must depart from this earthly existence. Why, then, gamble away such a sacred opportunity on trivial pursuits, worldly ambitions, pride, possessions, and fleeting pleasures that cannot accompany you beyond the boundaries of this life? What lasting happiness can the world offer when everything it gives is destined to pass away? Let your life acquire its true meaning by living every moment in the remembrance of Sri Hari, chanting His holy Name, glorifying His divine virtues, and keeping His sacred presence ever alive within your heart. Let every breath become a prayer, every action an offering, every thought an act of devotion, and every day an opportunity to serve SadGuru and Sri Hari. Prepare your heart so lovingly and sincerely that when the final hour arrives, you may depart not with fear, regret, or attachment to the world, but with the holy Name of the Lord upon your lips and His divine presence within your soul. May the Lord of Love Himself come to hold your hand, guide your soul, and lead you towards His eternal abode. Do not merely desire an ordinary departure from this world; aspire to make the final moment of your earthly journey sacred through a lifetime of devotion, surrender, and loving service. What greater fulfilment could there be than to leave this world in the loving remembrance of Sri Hari, having entrusted your entire existence to His divine care? The true extraordinary blessing is not in the manner in which the body departs, but in the grace-filled state of the soul, whose consciousness remains absorbed in Guru and God. Those who have sincerely taken refuge at the lotus feet of SadGuru and dedicated their lives to His commands are blessed with the greatest treasure: the remembrance of Sri Hari, which no worldly circumstance can take away. Vishwananda has witnessed this profound truth time and again when a devoted soul departs from this earthly existence. There are moments when the atmosphere itself seems filled with an indescribable tenderness, love, and peace. A deep spiritual comfort descends upon the hearts of those gathered, especially upon those whose minds remain attached to the sacred feet of SadGuru and Sri Hari. Such moments remind us that divine love is far greater than the limitations of our worldly understanding, and that the holy Name, when cherished with sincerity, becomes the eternal refuge of the soul. Yet let no one imagine that such grace is the achievement of personal merit or spiritual greatness. Whatever peace, devotion, understanding, and liberation a soul may receive is entirely through the boundless compassion of Sri Hari and the grace of SadGuru. Those who are protected by the remembrance of His holy Name are blessed not by their own strength, but by His infinite mercy. Why, then, O mind, do you allow pride and ego to rise within you? Why make a vain display of accomplishments, knowledge, devotion, or spiritual attainment, as though anything truly belonged to you? Everything that appears in this earthly reality is temporary and destined to vanish. The body will perish, possessions will be left behind, worldly recognition will fade, and the praises of people will eventually be forgotten. Only the love of God, the grace of SadGuru, and the devotion cultivated in the depths of the heart can give lasting meaning to this precious human birth. Therefore, abandon the burden of pride, surrender the illusion of self-importance, and cease measuring your life by what you possess or what others think of you. Instead, measure it by how sincerely you have loved, how selflessly you have served, how faithfully you have remembered the Lord.
