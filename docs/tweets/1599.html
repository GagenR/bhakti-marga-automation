Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

dinsdag 24 november 2026, 16:56
Kartik Purnima, Dev Diwali, Damodara Leela, the joyful lighting of a large lamp surrounded by many smaller ones, today is such an auspicious day. The day when the devas celebrate Deepavali by lighting the eternal celestial lamps of devotion to Bhagavan’s lotus feet. It is the only day that they get to enter Vaikuntha freely, otherwise, the doors to that most heavenly place are not so easily opened. Only the most beloved of Bhagavan, the saints and bhaktas, can come and go as they please through those subtlest doors of the heart. Where atma and Paramatma dance in ecstatic devotion. Where Prem is the food, the air, the palace, the gift, the form—where the only existence is of Prem. The infinite unconditional love of Sri Hari Narayan is symbolized by a large lamp around which hundreds of smaller lamps dance in love for Him flowing from their hearts. The currency to enter Vaikuntha is just love. That most ancient force of the universe is the only thing that Bhagavan is captured by. And in His most sweet form as baby Krishna, He performed the Damodara Leela. Allowing Yashoda Maiya to tie Him to a large barrel using a rope binding Him across the belly. That Cosmic Infinite Being who contains infinite universes within His tiny fingernail, was bound heart and soul by small pieces of rope tied with love. Because of a mother’s endless love for her son, a bhakta’s endless devotion to her beloved Bhagavan, an atma’s endless hope for closeness with her only focus, the SatGuru. The atma is constantly moving in blissful joy to serve the SatGuru in every way in this world and beyond. This is the fullness of the moon on Kartik Purnima, representing that light which perfectly reflects the sun when the world is in darkness. When the atma becomes a lamp of love reflecting the SatGuru’s eternal love to the world, that is the true moon of devotion. As the atma dances within, in joyful union with the Antaryami, the same way one should dance in complete love seeing Bhagavan everywhere. Shubh Kartik Purnima and much love to all.
