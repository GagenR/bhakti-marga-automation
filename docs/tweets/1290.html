Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 25 maart 2026, 14:07
Love, nurturing, softness, gentleness of spirit are often seen as feminine. Strength, determination, action, and support are seen as masculine.*
But look at Hanumanji, bhakti and prem moving in perfect symphony with strength and action.
Look at Ma Kaalratri, the fiercest, beyond anyone’s control, burned by the world. But with the softest, most gentle heart, never for a second hurting anyone. Yamuna-Giridhariji so gentle on the outside yet filled with the flowing river of transformation inside. Why should any of these qualities be limited by anything? The world needs to move beyond outer boundaries. Man-woman, lesser-greater, good-bad, all these polarities exist only within the world. Beyond this world where everyone has truly come from, only energy matters. How you were here is how you will experience existence there. So if you stayed within the false limitations of this world and never tried to see the love that has taken shape as everything, then even after death there won’t be an ability to see that permanently. You will need more experiences here to get to that place of true non-judgment.
We are celebrating Yamuna-Giridhari, Hanumanji, Sri Ram, the SatGuru, Ma Kaalratri and other forms of the Divine at the same time. But is there anything out of balance or out of synchronicity with each other? They are all endless facets of the same Divine. An eternal diamond with more and more shining refractions in every moment. Learn to see the same movement of Divinity within every celebration of God. It exists in all moments. And thus every moment can become a golden moment of your life.
