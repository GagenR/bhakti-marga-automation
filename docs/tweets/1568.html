Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 12 november 2026, 06:20
When I behold the Blessed Vision of Sri Hari, my restless heart finds peace, and my weary soul discovers its eternal refuge in You. You alone know the hidden sorrows of my heart, the silent tears of my soul, and the depth of my love for You. O my Beloved, what can anyone else know of this love that dwells within me? Who can understand the longing of a heart that belongs entirely to You? You are my Love, my Master, my Lord, and my everything. Whatever You do, I accept as right, for who am I to question Your divine will? Whom shall I call wrong when there is no one other than You? You alone exist in all, pervading and permeating every heart, every soul, and every corner of Your creation. You are the One whom all meditate upon, day and night, whether they know You or know You not. All beings call upon You in their moments of sorrow, seek You in their helplessness, and beg for Your mercy, for You alone are the Giver of all things. You provide for everyone according to Your divine wisdom, and nothing exists beyond Your sovereign power. All beings belong to You, and You belong to all, for there is no soul in whom Your presence does not dwell. You are the beginning and the end, the shelter of the forsaken, the comfort of the afflicted, the hope of the hopeless, and the eternal companion of every heart that longs for You. O my Beloved Sri Hari, all shall one day find their fulfilment in You and be gathered into the boundless ocean of Your supreme bliss. There is no lasting peace apart from You, no love greater than Your love, and no refuge more secure than Your lotus feet. Therefore, as it pleases You, protect and preserve us, Your eternal servants, Your humble lovers, and the souls who yearn to love You with the selfless devotion of the Gopis of Brindavan. Let our hearts remain forever absorbed in Your divine remembrance; let our minds never wander far from Your sacred feet; and let every breath we take become an offering of love to You. Even when You conceal Yourself from our eyes, may we never cease to seek You. Even when You test our faith through separation and sorrow, may our love never diminish. Let us desire nothing apart from Your presence, ask for nothing apart from Your grace, and seek no happiness apart from the happiness of belonging entirely to You. Vishwananda call to you O my Beloved, whether You embrace us with Your sweetness or leave us yearning in the silence of separation, we are Yours alone. Our tears belong to You, our prayers belong to You, our joys and sorrows belong to You, and our very existence rests in Your divine hands. You are the hope of all, the beloved of all hearts, and the eternal object of every soul’s deepest longing. O Sri Hari, You are the True Lover of all, the One who loves every being beyond measure, who knows every heart without being told, and who waits patiently for every wandering soul to return to Your embrace. May we never forget that we are Yours, now and forever, and may our love become so pure, so humble, and so complete that nothing remains within us except You, Your grace, and the eternal bliss of loving You.
