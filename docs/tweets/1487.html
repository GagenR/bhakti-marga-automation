Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 4 oktober 2026, 02:03
The quiet strength that you discover deep within yourself after a period of struggle was there all along; you simply had not noticed it. Sometimes life takes us through difficulties, disappointments, and moments when we feel weak or lost. Yet these very experiences can turn us inward and help us discover a depth within ourselves that we had never seen before. We often long to reach that inner space of peace, where there is no restlessness and where we feel complete. We know that the happiness we find in the outside world is temporary, and something within us longs for something deeper. To reach that depth, we must grow, become more aware, and continue inward even when there seems to be no reward for doing so. But the strength we discover there is not simply the result of our struggles. It is something that was already alive within us. It is the spark of Bhagavan, quietly present within the heart. It does not come with noise or force. It is gentle, innocent, and almost shy. It does not arise to destroy who we are, but to transform us, to bring back our innocence, to awaken wonder, and to help us see the beauty that surrounds us. The Atma is constantly speaking from within, but we are often too occupied with the noise of life to listen. Paramatma is present within us, waiting to be recognized, yet our distractions keep us looking outward. When we begin to turn inward, we slowly discover that the peace and love we were searching for were never truly absent. The SatGuru helps the jeevatma understand this and shows the way inward. The SatGuru does not simply give something from outside; the SatGuru awakens us to what is already present within and guides us toward its realization. Step by step, the jeevatma moves beyond its distractions, fears, and sense of separation and enters that deeper space where Atma and Paramatma are no longer experienced as separate. Then the heart begins to experience a joy that cannot be found in the outer world, a joy of recognition, as though the Atma has finally remembered its true home and can once again dance in the presence of Paramatma.
