The beauty of Hanuman.
We have heard so many stories of Hanumanji, can learn endless wisdom from him. But people often create a distance in their mind that they can never learn the qualities that the great bhaktas learned. But why this weakness? Bhagavan openly distributes grace to all. It all depends on how one receives life from Him. Remember the story of when Narad muni got a monkey face from Bhagavan Sri Hari. People often take it as a prank that the Lord played on Narad muni to reduce his ego because everyone laughed heartily looking at Narad muni’s monkey face. Narad muni was exceedingly hurt and got very angry but such a sweet revelation and blessing was hidden in that by the Lord. The face of Hari, the face of a monkey. The face of Hanuman is the face of Sri Hari and that was given to Narad. For just a little while. But blessings often don’t come with a signpost that says, “This is a blessing, be grateful, enjoy it.” Often they come disguised as problems, beatings from life, and difficult lessons. Did Narad muni understand the blessing then? No. He too got upset. Felt humiliated. Felt cast aside and striped of his dignity. But to receive anything of Hanuman for even a moment, if he was asked in that manner, which bhakta would refuse? Every story of God has deeper and deeper revelations. Every word of the SatGuru has deeper and deeper blessings within them. On Hanuman Jayanti, take heart and go with full force on the path, like Hanumanji does to fulfill the work of Sri Ram. Know that through the Name, the Mantra given to you, the SatGuru is always with you, the way Hanuman constantly holds his beloved Sri Ram close in his heart. Learn to look at the blessing in everything.
Jai Sri Ram, Jai Hanuman.
