Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 2 oktober 2026, 03:55
It is a common complaint of spiritual people: “The SatGuru is giving contradictory Adesh, or He Himself is behaving in contradictory ways.”
The problem is that people love to get stuck in the past so much that one cannot see the present moment at all. One must keep the SatGuru’s every Adesh constantly in mind to work on it, but that does not mean to become blind to the present moment. The needs of the present may require a new learning to emerge from the same grace of the Adesh. The SatGuru’s one guidance can bring one to newer and greater heights of spirituality continuously. One adesh given years ago is still just as fresh today as it was then. One divine experience with Him decades ago is just as complete with grace today as it was then. But only if one keeps working on it diligently and happily within the present moment.
After when one has truly done that, when the time is right, the SatGuru will also pull the rope in the opposite direction so that one can be firmly anchored on the spiritual path. And here, instead of seeing His great mercy, people complain and hold onto the past. He is pulling the string of the bow. The more firmly the string is anchored to both sides of the bow and the harder the string is pulled in the opposite direction, the faster and more precisely the arrow can fly towards the goal. “The mind is the string. The atma is the arrow. The SatGuru is the expert Archer.” When the mind is kept neutral, filled with love, serving in every moment, then it can handle being pulled in any direction without breaking. The atma is full of potential waiting to launch itself towards the Ultimate Goal; it keeps preparing itself for the Archer to place His hands of blessing on its head. The SatGuru is guiding the string of the mind to be completely relaxed and flexible as well as stronger than the toughest diamond. “The lotus feet of the SatGuru are two sides of the bow the seeming ‘contradiction’. “When the mind wraps itself around any Adesh of the SatGuru with ease, instant action, gratitude, and humility, then the mind is strung onto one side of the bow perfectly. But the other side, the opposite side, also needs to be roped in for the mind to be completely anchored to the SatGuru’s lotus feet. This way the SatGuru gives the seemingly ‘contradictory’ Adesh or behavior. But there are two sides of the same coin. Perfectly logical and aligned to the Highest. “The SatGuru’s eternal heart is Vaikuntha.” The Ultimate Goal where the SatGuru points the bow is His own infinite love. When He sees that the atma is ready to fly towards that innermost space of Vaikuntha, He does everything to send the atma straight into the bull’s eye, His own heart.
Tasmai Shri Gurave Namaha
