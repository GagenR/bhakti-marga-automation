Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 11 november 2026, 13:27
When the Lord extends His protection to a jeevatma like that of a loving brother, He does not merely stand beside them to shield them from the storms of life; He awakens within them the strength to walk through every storm without losing faith. His protection is not always the removal of difficulties, but the grace to transform every difficulty into an opportunity for inner growth. He breaks the chains of weakness, burns away the tendencies that keep the soul bound to its limitations, and dissolves the fears that prevent it from recognising its own divine potential. This is the extraordinary gift of Bhai Dooj that Sri Hari Vishnu, the SatGuru, bestowed upon Parvati Devi, Draupadi, Subhadra and all the divine manifestations of Ma Bhagavati who came as His sisters, just as He extends this eternal bond to every jeevatma. His brotherhood is not confined to blood, time, relationships or a single lifetime. It is a sacred promise of protection that follows the soul throughout its journey towards the Divine. On this Bhai Dooj, let us therefore celebrate not only the relationships that have shaped our lives, but also the eternal relationship we share with Bhagavan, who remains the truest brother and guardian of every soul. The SatGuru’s work is perhaps the most difficult expression of divine love. He does not simply comfort the devotee or fulfil every desire; He lovingly removes everything that stands between the soul and its highest truth. Sometimes His grace comes through tenderness, and sometimes through challenges that reveal strengths we never knew we possessed. He allows us to face what we must overcome, not because He wishes to see us struggle, but because He sees the extraordinary light that we are capable of becoming. To guide someone through the fire of transformation, while remaining their unwavering refuge, requires a love that is beyond human understanding. The SatGuru asks for nothing in return, keeps no account of what He gives and never measures His love by the gratitude He receives. He simply continues to uplift, purify and guide those who surrender at His lotus feet. This is Prem in its purest form: a love that does not possess, imprison or weaken, but liberates, strengthens and brings the soul closer to Bhagavan.
May this Bhai Dooj awaken within every heart the courage to trust the SatGuru’s grace, the humility to accept His guidance and the strength to embrace every transformation He brings. Vishwananda pray May we become instruments of His divine love, shining with faith, fearlessness and compassion, and may our lives become a blessing to all those we encounter. Bhagavan is always our protector, our strength and our eternal brother. Jai Sri Hari! Blessings to all.
