Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 18 juni 2026, 15:19
The passing of a devotee is an exceptional outpouring of grace.
The Bhakti Marga family has lost a dear member today. Madhavadas former swami left his body a few hours ago and has gone to the eternal abode of the lotus feet of Sriman Narayana which is the real Vaikuntha. His service was amazing. For 27 years, in any situation, he stood by me. He was the founding members of Shree Peetha Nilaya ashram and he helped in building it from scratch. He also led two more ashrams to be built, Sri Vithala Dham in Germany and Adhokshadham in Austria. When a devotee leaves their body, the universe takes a deep breath of silence. A beloved atma is on its way back to Paramatma. And there will never be a life like that again in this world. That particular personality, those set of circumstances, that way of loving Bhagavan will never come back. This is why every life is so precious to the SatGuru. He knows how short time is here. One comes for just a few days. Take inspiration from your spiritual family, take inspiration from the devotees. They spend their lives serving something greater than themselves and are freed forever from the bonds of this world. The true devotees never need to come back to this world because all their karma is taken care of by the SatGuru. He puts His complete force and love behind the cleaning of the jeevatma, returning them to Bhagavan in their pure state. But only a few understand and let their lives become an outpouring of Bhagavan’s mercy. They become an instrument in His hands. Till the last moment, Madhavadas served me and has claimed his eternal place at the lotus feet of Sri Hari. To say goodbye to a devotee is tough also for the SatGuru, but when one does everything possible to love and serve Bhagavan in all, the SatGuru finds solace in that. Sadgati Prapti Rastu, Om Shanti. Blessings to the departed soul on their way to Bhagavan, may everyone find true peace.
