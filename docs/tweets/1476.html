Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 27 september 2026, 03:35
Sometimes, the greatest blessings in our lives are the ones we never thought to ask for. A door opened when we thought everything was closing.
A person came into our life when we needed them most.
A path changed when we were certain we knew where we were going.
A prayer was answered in a way we never expected. That is why being grateful is more than simply saying “thank you.” It is trusting Satguru wisdom even when we cannot understand the journey.
We often ask for what we think will make us happy, but SadGuru knows what our soul truly needs. Some blessings arrive as opportunities. Some arrive as people. Some arrive as protection. And sometimes, a blessing is simply being kept away from something we desperately wanted.
So today, pause for a moment.
Be grateful for the blessings you recognize. Be grateful for the blessings you haven't recognized yet.
Be grateful for the doors that opened.
And be grateful for the doors Sri Hari chose to keep closed. Because what feels like a delay may be divine timing.
What feels like a loss may be protection. What feels like an unanswered prayer may be an answer we are not ready to understand.
Grateful and thankfulness, SriHari SadGuru. For every blessing I asked for, and especially for the ones I never knew I needed. Success can build your name, but Seva builds your character.
We live in a world that constantly tells us to achieve more, earn more, own more, and become more. But Satguru reminds us that the true measure of a life is not only what we achieve for ourselves, it is what we do for others. Your SUCCESS isn’t meaningful without SEVA. Because success becomes beautiful when it gives you the ability to lift someone else.
Your WEALTH isn’t blessed without giving a part to service. Because what SriHari has given us is not meant to become a reason for attachment. Sharing what we have turns abundance into blessing. Your SERVICE isn’t Seva without LOVE. Seva is not about being seen. It is not about praise, recognition, or proving that we are better than others. True Seva comes from a heart that genuinely wants to help. Your VOICE isn’t powerful without TRUTH. Words can influence people, but truth gives those words meaning. Speak with courage, but also with humility and compassion and don’t use your word in any to hurt anyone. Your KNOWLEDGE isn’t wisdom without the true word and knowledge of the Satguru. Knowing more does not automatically make us wiser. Wisdom is learning how to live what we know with humility, compassion, patience, and remembrance of Satguru.
At the end of the day, it isn't about how much you collected.
It is about how much you shared.
It isn't about how many people know your name. It is about how many lives became a little better because you were there. It isn't about looking successful. It is about becoming useful.
Earn, but remember to share.
Grow, but remember to serve.
Speak, but remember the truth.
Learn, but remember humility.
Live, but remember Sri Hari.
Because a meaningful life isn't measured only by what you have.
It is measured by what you give.
Seva. Constance remembering of God name. Truth. Humility.
