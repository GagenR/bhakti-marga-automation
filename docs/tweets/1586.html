Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 22 november 2026, 13:49
My beloved Lord Sri Hari alone pervades and permeates all existence. Beyond the limitations of time, space and form, He eternally abides in His own fullness. My Vithala is the divine trader of this world, and Giridhari is the true banker in whose treasury lies the inexhaustible wealth of devotion. His Divine Name is the eternal currency, His Grace the true fortune, and loving surrender unto His Lotus Feet the greatest gain. O mind, abandon your attachment to the fleeting treasures of this world and immerse yourself in the remembrance of Sri Vithala, Giridhari, Hari and Ram. Let every breath become an offering, every heartbeat a remembrance, and every thought a silent prayer unto Him. By the boundless Grace of the SadGuru, the inaccessible becomes accessible, the unfathomable reveals Himself, and the hidden Beloved awakens within the sanctuary of the heart. Sri Hari witnesses all through countless eyes, hears through countless ears and speaks through the mouths of all beings. Even in the wilderness of uncertainty, He secretly guides our footsteps, transforming our struggles into wisdom and our suffering into opportunities for surrender. Nothing unfolds beyond His awareness, for all existence moves within His divine will. From Himself emerges the entire universe, within Himself it is sustained, and unto Himself it ultimately returns. Yet He remains untouched, eternally free, immersed in His own divine bliss. Sri Hari is the endless ocean of existence, the sacred vessel of devotion and the compassionate SadGuru who steers the soul beyond the currents of Maya. His Grace is the favourable wind, faith is the sail, and His Divine Name is the anchor that steadies the heart amidst the storms of worldly life. When the illusion of separation dissolves, one discovers that the seeker, the seeking and the One being sought have always rested within Him. What is there to possess when everything belongs to the Beloved? What is there to fear when His presence embraces all? Blessed is the soul that recognises His hand in every circumstance and beholds His wondrous Lila in all creation. Giridhari, the infinitely merciful Master, manifests His compassion through His servant Vishwananda, dispelling the darkness of ignorance, forgiving our shortcomings and guiding the wandering soul towards loving union with Himself. O my beloved Sri Hari, let there remain no desire apart from the desire to serve You, no remembrance apart from Your Divine Name, and no longing apart from the longing for Your Lotus Feet. May this little self dissolve in the vastness of Your love, until only You remain, eternally radiant, eternally blissful, eternally One without a second. Sri Hari alone is, Sri Hari alone was, and Sri Hari alone shall ever be.
