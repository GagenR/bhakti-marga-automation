Today is also Father’s Day.
Often we speak of a mother’s care and love. When we think of a child, it is more automatically linked to the mother. The expression of a father’s love is more subtle to understand yet just as important as the role of the mother. The true depth of a father’s heart is often not seen clearly. Not because it is any less than that of a mother. But because it is a subtler form of love. Many times it is shown through action rather than an expression of feelings. Protection, providing for the child’s needs, guidance for growth, and setting an example for right thought and behavior. Fathers are so important to celebrate. To open the heart and allow the mercy of the Divine Father to enter and surround one’s existence; one needs to first deeply thank the father one has been given in the world. Any father figure who has been a guiding light in your life, reach out to them today. Express your joy in their hidden love for you. Love when expressed with gratitude and openness has the power to change the world.
To all the fathers out there, may this day show you the endless deepness of love within your heart.
May Bhagavan brighten that flame so that your light can spread to more children in need of a strong, loving, and kind parent. Blessings to all.
