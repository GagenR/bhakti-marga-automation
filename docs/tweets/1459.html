The SatGuru keeps empowering the jeevatma to reach Him.
But it is extremely difficult to reach the place of the atma that can endlessly and eternally converse with the Paramatma. It is so difficult because one has put so many veils around oneself to strengthen the ego instead of the divine self. Many people on the spiritual path think that they would love to endlessly converse with Bhagavan, with the SatGuru. But is one truly ready for such an opportunity? What conversation does one hold with the Antaryami within one’s own heart? A conversation of complaints? doubts? anger? jealousy? telling Him the story of one being a helpless victim? Or, is one’s conversation with Him in the heart one of gratitude? joy? love? wonder? The type of conversation one has with the Antaryami within the heart is heard by the Paramatma. It is heard by the SatGuru, who may be thousands of miles away. It is heard by the cosmic eternal Shakti of Bhagavan, of Sri Hari, who is everywhere. What conversation do you hold with the SatGuru when you meet Him? What type of conversation would you hold with Bhagavan if He appeared in His chaturbhuj form? If one has no idea, or the idea of endless conversation from the atma with the Antaryami sounds scary or impossible - then one should ask oneself: is one ready for greater closeness with Bhagavan, with the SatGuru? Or, does one simply like the idea of it in one’s mind? And if one truly wants that greater closeness, then how has one made oneself ready for it? Has one truly tried to embody the SatGuru’s quality and teaching of unconditional love?
