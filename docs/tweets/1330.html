Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

Right will always remain right, regardless of the number of people who disregard it. Similarly, wrong will always be wrong, even if the entire world embraces it. Sikhi encourages us to remain steadfast in truth, rather than following trends.
What is right; what is wrong? What is judgement; what is discernment? Spiritual seekers keep fluctuating between the understanding of these truths. But often one simply complicates things and doesn’t actually deeply understand anything.
The discernment of what is the right way forward is determined by 4 simultaneous points.
(1) Your own deep inner intuition.
(2) The way opened up by life, others, destiny, the universe.
(3) The instruction and guidance given by the SatGuru for everyone.
(4) The personal Adesh given by the SatGuru for you alone.
Each and every one of the four points has its own way of revealing itself and blossoming to its fullest potential. In this way, all four points hand-in-hand, illuminate what’s right and what’s wrong. The SatGuru alone knows the complete truth; every jeeva has to discern the truth by working on one’s life to align these four ways. Hence, what is really true will always remain true. What is false will always be false. The way to approach the realization of it in one’s life has to be worked on with sincerity and single-pointed determination. However, there is one secret to a completely successful life that one must understand. The third and especially fourth point, the SatGuru’s subtle and direct blessing in one’s life, they are what one should focus on completely. They are what lead to the polishing and revelation of the true first and second points. Never go into weakness even if you fall or fail. Focus on the SatGuru’s love, pick yourself back up, and continue walking. Life is meant to be lived with fearless joy. Life is fearless joy. Learn that deep truth and walk with God everywhere you go.
