Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 26 november 2026, 11:07
Sri Hari is the eternal sanctuary of every soul, the boundless ocean of divine compassion, and the supreme reality beyond all worldly limitations. At His sacred feet’s , the burdens of the heart dissolve, the darkness of ignorance fades, and the restless mind discovers the serenity of divine surrender. His lotus feet are the sacred refuge where humility blossoms into wisdom, faith matures into realization, and Bhakti becomes the purest expression of unconditional love. Through the infinite grace of the SadGuru, the soul awakens to the understanding that true liberation is attained not through worldly possessions or fleeting pleasures, but through purity of heart, selfless service, and complete surrender to the Divine Will. Every difficulty becomes an opportunity for spiritual growth, every experience a lesson in detachment, and every breath a sacred remembrance of His eternal presence. Sri Hari dwells within the hearts of all beings, sustaining creation through His divine love while silently guiding every soul beyond the illusions of Maya towards the realization of eternal truth. Those who seek shelter of his lotus feet gradually transcend pride, selfish desires, and attachment, discovering the everlasting joy that arises from unwavering devotion. The sacred Name of Sri Hari becomes the light that illuminates the inward journey, while the blessings of the SadGuru awaken the wisdom to recognize His divine presence everywhere. Vishwananda humbly prays that the holy lotus feet’s of his beloved Sri Hari remove all negativity, ignorance, jealousy, anger, and suffering from every hearts and minds. May His boundless mercy awaken compassion, forgiveness, divine wisdom, and the deepest longing for pure Bhakti within all beings. May every thought be sanctified by His remembrance, every word reflect kindness, and every action become an offering of love and service. May His grace transform fear into faith, restlessness into peace, and the illusion of separation into the realization of divine oneness. May all humanity walk the eternal path of Dharma with humility, righteousness, and unwavering devotion, recognizing the sacred presence of Sri Hari in every living being. May His lotus feet remain our everlasting refuge, our guiding light through the ocean of worldly existence, and our ultimate destination beyond birth and death. May all souls attain inner purity, spiritual awakening, and the supreme bliss of Moksha through His infinite compassion and the blessings of the SadGuru.
