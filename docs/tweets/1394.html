Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

On the spiritual path, the mind is often washed like a cloth by the SatGuru.
The mind takes on the colors that you most feed it. So it becomes a certain way after you have fed it the same stories, the same emotions, the same opinions again and again. And often then one looks outward and blames the world, blames God and Guru. “God, why do you send me so much pain? Guruji, why do you test me all the time?” But that has simply become your mind’s way of thinking, hence you see things that way. If you change the pattern of your thoughts, if you change your perspective, if you change the way you look at things, everything changes. How you take things, how you handle things matters the most to Bhagavan. But the SatGuru is the kindest of kind. He doesn’t just wait for you to keep falling and learn in your own time over many lifetimes. He takes the dirty cloth of the bhakta’s mind and with all His energy washes it and makes it clean. The process may seem like a heavy beating. But is it really? Not at all. Is the mind, is the person losing anything really? No. The pain is a lower illusory reality. In actuality, one is being cleaned thoroughly. All the negativity is getting washed away. One is being made new. On the path, many times it may seem like the mind is spinning out of control. But as a bhakta one should never fear or worry. In such moments, offer the mind more strongly into the SatGuru’s capable hands. Offer the mind Bhagavan’s name to calm it down. Don’t let go of who is helping you. Only God and Guru have the permanent answer to the problem, only they have endless unconditional love which is the true solution to every problem. Feed the mind with Vitthal Giridhari’s name more and more lovingly when the mind feels like it is spiraling. Then you can experience the flow of Bhagavan’s sweet mercy within your heart once again.
