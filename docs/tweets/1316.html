Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

Accepting Hukam is not always easy, as life may present unexpected turns. However, Sikhi reminds us that Waheguru's plan surpasses our own, and trusting in Hukam can bring peace even amidst challenges.
All spiritual traditions talk about the will, the command, the instruction, adesh, or hukam of the Divine, of the SatGuru.
And often seekers of God discuss this, but how much one understands about it remains to be seen. One’s plan is always just a tiny portion of God’s plan. One cannot see the entire picture. But there is a being who can see the complete plan. There is someone who takes form and incarnates into this world who is that eternal one himself. It is this one who leads all from darkness into the light, the Sat Gu-Ru, the true eternal teacher, the eternal master. And it is only from the satguru that one can take instruction at every turn. His every word is a command to the universe. His will is what the universe waits for to act. The silent breath between two thoughts, between feeling and action, that is where the SatGuru places his command. His Adesh is the movement of energy. Every person has been given the ability to exert their willpower and see actions manifest into reality. And for the satguru, that ability encompasses all beings, all times, and all things. But this power, this complete command over the universe is not something one receives or grows into. It is something that the satguru already is. He alone is that. So one must understand how extraordinary it is when the satguru gives an instruction for one’s life. He is bringing the silence of God’s will into loving manifestation for the bhakta. And then how sad it would be to not value his effort, to not give one’s all into making his command a reality. His plan is always better than the best thing one can dream of. Trust in that universal love, trust in his eternal presence with you. Whether you flow with His word which becomes the boat of your life and He becomes your captain. Or, you fight His word and keep dashing against rocks, getting hurt, and drowning. No matter how difficult, strange, or unpleasant His word may seem on the surface, know that it is the absolute best and strongest boat to get you to the end of the river which submerges one fully in God. Never judge his word. Never set it aside for something else. Understand why the saints kept the word spoken by the Guru higher than even the saakshat darshan of Bhagavan. This one deep understanding, to follow the instruction of the SatGuru, will give you everything.
