As long as one has a ‘rescue fantasy’, one’s experience of SatGuru and God will be very limited.
A being who can rescue you from misery. Who can protect you when you feel unsafe. Someone who will love when you can’t love yourself. God and Satguru is and constantly gives all these things and infinite more. But He will first teach one to become so strong to be one’s own savior. So loving to rescue oneself from misery. So humble to protect oneself from all harm. So confident to be one’s own best friend. Become so truly loving to yourself that you can correct your mistakes without harsh self-criticism or arrogant ego. Simply with wonder at Bhagavan’s immense love and SatGuru Grace . This is the experience of God in your heart. He will free you fully from inside. And hold onto that experience, the values, qualities, and traits that led you to experiencing His unshakeable peace in your heart. Don’t let go of the inner qualities to now start experiencing God externally. The One who is everywhere infinite in quantity, who is the soul of your soul, who is present with form, name, and personality of His own. That One will also reveal Himself externally fully. Don’t ever doubt that. But don’t let go of the inner experience to hunt for the external, that will not work. Without the inner loving qualities, Bhagavan cannot reveal Himself in any way.
