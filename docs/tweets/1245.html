Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

The third Prahar of Maha Shivratri is from 12am-3am.
During this time, Chakreshwar Mahadev is worshipped. Lord Shiva had formed the Sudarshan Charka with his intense penance and devotion to offer to his beloved Sri Hari. And in turn, Bhagavan Sri Hari formed the Trishul Vijay with His immense love for Lord Shiva and gave it to him. Enclosing each other with their complete protection and single-pointed focus. When Sri Krishna was on Earth, Indra due to his ego, sent heavy rain in great disrespect to Bhagavan. For seven days and seven nights, Bhagavan held the Govardhan Parvat on His little finger to protect the land. During this time, the Sudarshan Chakra was helping Bhagavan to absorb the excess water. But the Sudarshan became haughty and arrogant about being able to help Bhagavan. To show Sudarshan that true bhakti and service have nothing to do with the self, Bhagavan called Lord Shiva and asked him to help. Lord Shiva happily agreed and took all the flood water into his hair, where the sacred River Ganga already resides. Within moments, the excess water disappeared from Vraj, and the Sudarshan Chakra was entirely humbled. This is why Lord Shiva at Govardhan is known as Chakreshwar Mahadev. In this way, Bhagavan once again established Lord Shiva’s authority as the Lord of the Chakra, Chakreshwar.
This Prahar also celebrates the emergence of the Jyotirlingam. Pristinely pure, divine, light. This connects with the Dakshinamurti, who is the Eternal SatGuru. When one moves out of sense enjoyment and worldly attachments, one enters Vanprasth Ashram. The time of life when one’s true inner nature is reawakened. One starts giving importance to the atma and searches earnestly for the right teacher. Like a blazing pillar of light that illuminates the bhakta’s mind, life, and spiritual path back to God, the SatGuru enters one’s life.
