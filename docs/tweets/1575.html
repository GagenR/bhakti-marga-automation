Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 19 november 2026, 11:11
The joy, love, blissfulness, and profound feeling of returning home to your true Self are not merely passing experiences when they arise from genuine spiritual awakening. What is discovered in the depths of your being cannot be taken away by distance, time, or changing circumstances. Truth is not something that belongs to a particular place or person; it is the eternal reality within you, waiting to be recognized. Moving away from the physical presence of the Sadguru is therefore not a separation from grace, but an opportunity to discover how deeply His teachings have become part of your consciousness. When the peace awakened in His presence continues to shine within you amid the challenges of everyday life, devotion begins to mature into realization. Through the boundless grace of Sri Hari and the Sadguru, the Bhakta gradually understands that the purpose of spiritual awakening is not to escape the world, but to live within it with a transformed heart. True spirituality must express itself through the way we think, speak, act, and relate to others. Carry the fragrance of devotion wherever life takes you. Let there be songs of gratitude in your heart, the dance of divine joy in your being, and compassion in every encounter. Do not measure your love according to another person’s worthiness, beliefs, behavior, or circumstances. Divine love is not a reward given to the deserving; it is a blessing that flows naturally from a heart awakened to the presence of Sri Hari in all beings. When love is free from expectation, judgment, and the desire for recognition, giving itself becomes a sacred expression of devotion. The more deeply you recognize the Divine within yourself, the more naturally you begin to recognize that same Divine presence in others. This is the flowering of true Bhakti, where prayer becomes compassion, meditation becomes awareness, surrender becomes inner freedom, and devotion becomes a living blessing. The Sadguru does not awaken the light within the Bhakta merely for personal happiness, but so that this light may illuminate the lives of others. Let your life become a silent testimony to His grace, not through grand declarations, but through humility, kindness, patience, forgiveness, and unconditional love. Ultimately, the deepest gratitude we can offer to Sri Hari and the Sadguru is to embody the love they have awakened within us. When the Bhakta becomes an instrument of divine compassion, the blessings received in the presence of the Sadguru begin to flow into the world. This is the true beauty of spiritual awakening: the journey that begins with seeking the Divine within ourselves blossoms into seeing, serving, and loving the Divine in everyone.
