Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 22 juli 2026, 09:38
Absolute joy is your natural state of being.
What stops one from experiencing this endless bliss is the fear of the mind. The fear of letting go. The fear of holding on. The fear of failure. The fear of pain. The fear of rejection. The fear of humiliation. The fear of mockery. There can be endless fears. But there can also be endless happiness. Which one you focus on becomes your experience. Life is so simple, but it’s not easy to love its simplicity. We think happiness is very far away, so we complicate the path to ‘get there’. But it is closer than close. It is you yourself. Happiness is your true state. This radiant place where the light is forever shining. Where joy is so abundant that you feel like you are bursting with it. This is the truth of your atma with Paramatma. When the atma has enjoyed its play of acting as if far from Bhagavan, it will start removing the veils that it has put between itself and Him. Then you will know. You will experience the bliss of the atma meeting Paramatma again and again and again. That He was closer to you than your own self. He is that inner self you were disconnected from. He is the cosmic entity in control of all. He is the SatGuru who embodies this divine play. One is never separate from Him. Be curious about Him. Crave for Him. In wanting Him, you will have Him. He is the only one with whom that paradox works. With everything else, when you want it, it runs away from you. Wanting God above all else, is the pinnacle of success.
