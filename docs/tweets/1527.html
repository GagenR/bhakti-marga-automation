Jai Ma Siddhidatri.
Today, on Mahanavami, the energy becomes fully rooted at the Ajna Chakra—the Seat of Divine Command of the SatGuru—to behold the beauty of Maa Siddhidatri, who is the manifestation of every fulfillment, joy, surrender, and devotion. She is the aspect within the Bindu of the Sri Yantra. She is the complete awakening of the infinite connection to the SatGuru’s presence within the atma and everywhere. She is Mahalakshmi, Mahasaraswati, and Mahadurga. She carries the Shanka-Chakra-Gada-Padma, all the symbols of Mahavishnu Himself. Maa Siddhidatri is the one whom Lord Shiva himself worshipped to attain his Ardhanarishwara form. She is greatness itself and can give everything, but Her heart is devotion. To ask Her for devotion to the SatGuru’s lotus feet is the greatest prayer that one can make to Ma Bhagavati. At the Ajna Chakra, the three major energy streams within the body—Ida (mind), Pingala (prana), and Sushumna (spirit)—converge into single-pointed devotional service to the SatGuru’s eternal presence everywhere. Maa Siddhidatri is commonly misunderstood as merely the giver of magical powers. No, Maa Siddhidatri is the giver of perfection—to have Siddhi means to completely attain and dissolve one’s individual identity into one’s aim. One cannot reach the truth of the Ajna Chakra no matter how much one tries to meditate on the third eye, unless it is the SatGuru’s will that one reaches Him. And He is looking only for unconditional love. To help all move towards the Unattainable One, through these Nine Divine Nights of Ma Bhagavati, She bestows 9 qualities of perfection: (1) Intense Introspection (2) Fierce Focus (3) Radiant Rebirth (4) Inner Illumination (5) Selfless Sacrifice (6) Constant Closeness (7) Keen Kindness (8) Devoted Detachment (9) All-Encompassing Abundance. May Maa Siddhidatri grant everyone the supreme achievement of abiding permanently in the grace of the SatGuru’s lotus feet.| Om Aim Ham Shreem Klim Hreem Haum Siddhidatryai Namah ||
