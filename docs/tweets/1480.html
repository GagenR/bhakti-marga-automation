Remembering Karna as Pitru Paksh begins.
One of the most difficult lives, yet most beautiful inner character. Stuck in the middle of adharma due to destiny, yet so close to Sri Krishna’s heart. Having nothing, receiving only thorns from the world, yet giving the very best of himself away in selfless charity to everyone. Karna forgot about himself completely in compassion for all. The very few times Sri Krishna truly cried in His life, one of them was when Karna was dying. Such an extraordinary attainment of being so close to Bhagavan’s heart, for someone who was only tormented his entire life. To remove Karna’s pain of being unwanted and unloved, Sri Krishna showed him the endless closeness they had always had. Even in past lives when Karna (Sahastrakavach) had alone fought against Sri Krishna (Narayan) and Arjun (Nara), his true self was dearest to the merciful heart of Bhagavan. And during the Mahabharat war, this was the last time they would stand against each other. After this life they would always stand together. As he was dying, Sri Krishna promised Karna the end of all feelings of separation from His heart, eternal dearness and sweetness. Karna was one of the very rare people who received an Adesh from Sri Krishna as he was dying. The other was Hanuman. Sri Ram gave His final Adesh to Hanuman as Sri Ram was leaving the world and Hanuman ji is fulfilling it till today. And on the other hand, Sri Krishna gave His final Adesh to karna as karna was dying. Just imagining how much trust and love Bhagavan must have had in him touches the heart so deeply. And Karna has lived up to that trust and love fully.
Every year during Pitru Paksha, Karna comes from Vaikuntha and offers food for all lineages, to all ancestors. During his life, he could never remember or offer food for his ancestors because he never knew what his true lineage was. But that which was Karna’s greatest pain, of being so unloved and unwanted, Sri Krishna gave him complete mastery over it—coming every year to be the one loving and feeding all those who have left their body. To share Sri Krishna’s infinite love with them through the performance of Shraddha. During the days of Pitru Paksh, remember to give thanks for everything that the SatGuru, Bhagavan, endlessly gives, and offer food in charity as much as possible. Blessings.
