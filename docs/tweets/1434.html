Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 28 augustus 2026, 12:11
On this day of Raksha Bandhan, let us keep in mind the connections of love that make this entire world one family.
So many places are facing crisis situations. Floods, earthquakes, wildfires, war, to mention a few. On the spiritual path, it is essential to see oneness everywhere. When any part is suffering, the Whole cannot be at ease. This entire creation is one family. It is time to truly learn the principle of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam and start to grow this energy of unconditional love for all. On this day, we celebrate Raksha Bandhan, the divine connection between a brother and sister. The Rakhi is a symbol of love and protection worn on the wrist, a constant reminder to keep love for others as the priority of life, and transform any self-centeredness. To see the same self shining in all and work hard to uplift the world with one’s light and compassion. This Raksha Bandhan, as Shreeman Narayan and Devi Parvati helped each other in their most difficult times, let us remember to offer that same hope, help, and love to everyone. Not just today, but every day. Let us hold a moment of silence remembering all those affected by the floods in Nepal and many other places that are facing an hour of darkness. We dedicate our prayers to them today, asking Bhagavan to help the light to shine through this very tough time for them. May He help everyone to come through this with His strength and grace. Shubh Raksha Bandhan. Lokah Samastah Sukhino Bhavantu, Om Shanti Shanti Shantihi, Shri Gurubhyo Namah Hari Om.
