A very blessed Gudi Padwa and Ugadi.
Gudi Padwa carries deep significance. It is said to be the day Lord Ram was crowned, and homes and temples raise the Gudi, a bright red flag representing prosperity and the triumph of righteousness. This is a symbol of His royal banner.
Tradition also holds that this is the very day Brahma set creation in motion, making it a beloved occasion for new beginnings.
What I find most beautiful about this festival is the food. The dishes prepared today aren't just delicious. They also carry a message. Maharashtrians share jaggery and neem leaves together, sweet and bitter in the same mouthful, an honest acknowledgment that life is both.
In the Ugadi tradition, Pachadi brings together six distinct flavors: neem flowers, raw mango, jaggery, tamarind, pepper, and salt. Each of these represent a different shade of human experience.
The invitation, it seems, is to embrace all of it, joy and sorrow, sweetness and bitterness, with an open and steady heart. It is a reminder not to avoid difficulty. It is about finding peace within ourselves that no circumstance can shake, rooted in gratitude, grace and the quiet offering of our actions to something greater than ourselves.
Happy Gudi Padwa and Ugadi
