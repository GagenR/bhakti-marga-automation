Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

It is an enormous grace from the SatGuru if you can remember Him.
People think, “Oh, it is just a thought, what’s the big deal? He isn’t really there, I’m just making everything up in my mind.” But it is the Lord Himself who can allow you to think about Him. You cannot do so otherwise. Not a single thought of Him is random. Not a single longing to love Him is random. People waste their time feeling that remembering Bhagavan is just a thought like any other. But it is the single most incredible thing in the universe; being able to think of Bhagavan. Everything starts from there. All gyaan, all dhyaan, all karma yoga, all bhakti. Everything starts with a thought about the most beloved Lord. He is the Lord of Love. He is the Lord of All. Yet one spends so much time thinking about the ego, thinking about what keeps us separate from Him. But one needs to start thinking about what one wants, not about what one doesn’t want. Think of the Lord. Think about what will bring you to Him. Think about how to love Him more and more freely, happily, and sincerely. Just love; this is so difficult for people to focus on, for their mind to make it their only goal. But just love holds the essence of everything in it. The SatGuru’s word is simple. Yet He sometimes needs to complicate it for people to appreciate ‘the depth of knowledge’. But this is not wisdom. The wise are always simple. Always loving. Always happy. Always in action and surrender to Bhagavan at the same time. They are never complicated in their mind. Give love everything you have and it will return everything million times better than before, all that you can never even dream of. Your thoughts are extremely powerful, make them positive. Give them a good direction to flow, let them keep running to God. To think of Him with love a single time is such a special grace. If only the jeevatmas knew how much the cosmic realm desperately yearns to be given that grace. The grace to think of Him. Not everyone is able to keep their mind centered on God. Just observe and you shall see. Focus your mind on Him. And just love.
