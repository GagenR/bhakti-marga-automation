Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 11 oktober 2026, 13:55
Kalyāṇamayī Navarātriḥ Sarvadā Bhavatām Bhavatu (May Navaratri uplift all of your lives into becoming auspicious and inspiring for all). Navaratri is a time of year that is filled with joy. Slowly reawakening the world from its state of inward reflection on one’s gratitude towards Bhagavan during Pitru Paksh into a joyous outward manifestation of one’s love for Bhagavan. Gratitude and love always build each other up.
The first day of Navaratri starts with Ma Shailputri. Her name itself gives us so many lessons. The daughter of the Himalayas. Her own identity is unknown at this point, she is known by someone else’s identity. Often this is the case for many of us. One’s inner identity remains hidden although one’s life keeps moving forward. That inner truth of the atma isn’t brought forward by the person. One remains stuck in family bonds, social identity, status, and other things. This does not mean to undervalue anything or anyone that Bhagavan has given to you as a loving gift in this world. It means to find yourself in the midst of a busy world. “Who am I who is having these various relationships with different members of my family? Who am I who is known to others in a particular way? Who am I that is receiving what I am from the world? Who am I? Who is this “I” that is experiencing the world?” Ma Shailputri introspected, contemplated, she thought about what is her core truth that she has come here to grow into and blossom towards the outside. It wasn’t enough to be known as the daughter of a great and pious king. It wasn’t enough to be respected because of her parents’ good deeds. It wasn’t enough to be a princess who would get married one day and start her own family. It was burningly important to find herself. A small seed has to fall slightly far from its parent tree to grow into an enormous tree itself. Directly under the shade of another tree, a new seed cannot manifest its full potential. The same way, one has to take oneself out of one’s comfort zone to find oneself. Circling within the same thoughts again and again one will not reach anywhere. One has to learn true Karma Yoga how to maintain and serve one’s outer world while continuously elevating oneself towards loving detachment internally.
May Ma Shailputri guide all to take this first step towards living from the true spirit of oneself. | Shubh Shailputri Maa Parvah (Blessings on this auspicious festival day of Maa Shailputri)
