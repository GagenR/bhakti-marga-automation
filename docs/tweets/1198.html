Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

Rath Saptami is the third celebration this month for the arrival of spring.
Continuous joy. Constant reminders of the Divine. And sharing one’s love with all. This is the sweetness of the Sanatan Dharma. Ratha Saptami is the great celebration of the Sun (Surya). It is also known as Surya Jayanti - the day when the Sun, the source of energy for all life on Earth, changes direction, moving from southeast to northeast. Surya Dev’s seven-horse chariot marked by the seven colors of the rainbow delivers joy, abundance, good health, prosperity, wealth, compassion, and spiritual blessings to all. Hence, this day is also known as Arogya (health) day. Those who pray to Bhagavan from their heart today receive blessings as radiant as the Sun, just like Hanuman Ji did. Hanuman Ji’s SatGuru is Surya Dev, who illuminated Hanuman’s life purpose as an eternal Ram Bhakt and gave him the Mantra “Sri Ram Jai Ram Jai Jai Ram”. This Guru mantra with the Ashirwad of the SatGuru gave Hanuman Ji everything and more. His eternal place at the Lotus Feet of Bhagavan through Ananya Bhakti that every bhakta aims to inculcate. No Temple of Sri Ram can be complete without Hanuman Ji’s presence.
Surya Narayan is also the first progenitor of the Surya Vanshi clan, the Ikshvaku dynasty, bringing endless blessings into the world - The dynasty includes great kings like Mandhata, Harishchandra, Bhagirath, Raghu, Dasharath, and Bhagavan Sri Ram.
How endless are the ways that Bhagavan blesses everyone. How endless is the joy when one turns to Him fully. Just like the sun is too radiant to be looked at directly, in that same way Bhagavan’s love is too enormous to be understood fully. But on Rath Saptami, wake up with the first rays of the sun, and gaze towards Bhagavan’s abode in the sky reflecting His beautiful colors all over the world.
May He illuminate your heart with bhakti and prem.
Shubh Ratha Saptami to all.
