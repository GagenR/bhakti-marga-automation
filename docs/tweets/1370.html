When someone is on their way to Bhagavan’s abode, keep chanting for them.
In life and death, the Name of Bhagavan is the way to salvation. Keep chanting Sri Vithala Giridhari Parabrahmane Namaha. When the memories of someone who has departed come, allow those beautiful moments to remind you of Bhagavan’s love and mercy. One misses loved ones, that is natural. It is normal to feel the pain of their physical separation. But don’t become stuck in sorrow, be joyful and grateful that they lived well and have gone beyond the veil of sorrows. Remember that they are never truly gone. Bhagavan makes sure that the divine relationship with Him is eternal. Pray for them that they always love and serve Him. That they choose to live in His care and shelter. Bhagavan’s name is the greatest comfort for the soul. May the devotees across the world keep chanting the Name of Bhagavan more and more. Offer that beautiful service to all the departed souls and to everyone in the world. The mantra given by the SatGuru has the ability to fill the pot of love and happiness within you and overflow endlessly to others. You will never lack anything by offering all the energy generated by your Japam back to Bhagavan’s lotus feet. He will amplify that drop of love from you thousand times and give it back. Keep chanting the Divine Name as someone leaves the body. This is the greatest offering of love and divinity because it frees everyone from all pain.
