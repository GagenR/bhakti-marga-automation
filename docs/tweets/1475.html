Divine Sangat.
Sat sangat — the association of people focused on the Ultimate Truth (Sat), where Bhagavan’s Name is remembered and loved.
Sadhu sangat — the association of saints and spiritually elevated teachers, where one can grow because of their teachings, the mind can be purified to some extent with their blessing.
Bhagavad Sangat — the association of Bhagavan within the heart and the cosmic entity in control of everything, present everywhere. This was given to the rarest of saints. Not all saints were also qualified for this experience.
Guru Sangat — even rarer were the saints who realized the importance of the Guru. Who gave their all to Guru Seva, who became Gurmukh, Guru Bhaktas. Like Sant Kabir Dev ji, who immediately and forever chose the Guru’s feet even above Bhagavan’s.
But if one is fortunate to receive SatGuru Sangat, then one has received everything. The company of the SatGuru is the most rarely obtained jewel. At His feet, is sat sangat. At His feet, is sadhu sangat. At His feet, is Bhagavad sangat. At His feet, reside all Gurus. One doesn’t have to go anywhere else, to anyone else. The Name of the SatGuru, His lotus feet, His acceptance of oneself in His company. This is the atma’s Purna Vishram.
