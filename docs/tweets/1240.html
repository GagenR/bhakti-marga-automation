Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

The second Prahar of Maha Shivratri is from 9pm-12am.
This is the second watch of the night. Bhooteshwar Mahadev is worshiped during this time. Lord Krishna, after slaying Kansa, appointed Lord Shiva in his form of Bhooteshwar as the guardian of Vraj to protect the devotees and the holy city. Bhooteshwar also means the one who is the Lord of all ghostly beings, ganas, and other creatures that are reviled by common folk. Lord Shiva lovingly gave them all a place in his Baarat, the procession that follows the bridegroom as he goes to his wife’s house to marry her. This divine Baarat of Lord Shiva spanned all three worlds.
Hence, we also celebrate the second Varnashram; Grihasta Ashram as Lord Shiva shows through his divine marriage to Devi Parvati. Both of them are the highest examples of samadhi, penance, prayers, single-pointed focus, and Bhakti. When Grihasta Ashram is undertaken properly, keeping God at the center of one’s family, it becomes an extraordinary way to reach the Divine. Not everyone is supposed to take up Akhanda Brahmacharya vows; it is not for everyone. Creation continues through Divine Will, and some atmas are guided to help raise families that can become pillars of Bhakti in this world.
As Sundareshwar, Lord Shiva dresses up as a bridegroom waiting to marry his dear wife, Devi Parvati. Their marriage is still celebrated in all three worlds with great happiness. As Nataraj, Lord Shiva shows the joy of Prem. The immense happiness the atma feels when the lost memories of one’s Beloved are retrieved. When the atma is anticipating reunion with the Beloved. When everything becomes a dance of love, a celebration of life itself. Because Love has returned. During the Raas with Sri Krishna, Lord Shiva danced the same way as his Nataraj aspect when he won Devi Parvati’s hand in marriage. With complete abandon and ecstatic bliss. The Tandav, Lord Shiva’s unique dance, is known only to him and Bhagavan Sri Hari. When Sri Krishna danced the Tandav with Lord Shiva during the Raas, simultaneous actions of Creation and Destruction took place. It was a moment immortalized in time like no other.
