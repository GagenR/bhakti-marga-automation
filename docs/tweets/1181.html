Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 2 januari 2026, 14:33
Happy new year everyone. May all create a wonderful 2026 for themselves and everyone else.
Life is always a dialogue between the atma and Paramatma. A constant creation of love between the two. No destiny, no fate, is written in stone unable to be changed. Everything depends on the grace of the SatGuru and the atma’s own efforts. When you walk on the spiritual path, everything can be transformed. Even the most difficult karmas to crossover can be easily transcended by the joint efforts of the atma and the SatGuru. Remember you are the creation of the Great Creator Himself. You are loved and have miracles in your life beyond measure. Be rooted in the present moment but look forward with joy and positivity. Although it is a simple date change on the calendar, what is important is that one is reminded of a new beginning. Life is always new in all moments, but the mind needs reminders to become aware of this beauty.
Make a strong determination to go deep into your Sadhana this year. Go with full force on the spiritual path. Don’t let anything hold you back from attaining your goal. God is always with you. Make Him your best friend. Blessings to everyone.
