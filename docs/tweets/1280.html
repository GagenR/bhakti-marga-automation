Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 22 maart 2026, 13:03
The second three days of Navaratri that revere Kushmanda, Skanda Mata, and Katyayani Ma are the most sweet.
Truly for those who have the eyes to see, the ears to hear, and the heart to know, the SatGuru is sweeter than the most sweet sugar. The Divine Mother’s most gentle love amplified into thousand times and million times more concentrated, that is the SatGuru’s heart for His bhaktas. His concentration on His bhaktas is so fine that not even the thinnest divine needle can embroider a tapestry of love finer than His. And each bhakta’s life is unique. Uniquely and intensely loved by Him.
He is Kushmanda, the one who holds everything within her. He is Skanda Mata, who has fully given herself to her child. And He is Katyayani Devi, who even the most elevated saints worship with utmost reverence. It is said that the wine of Vitthal Giridhariji’s love is so addictive that one can never be free of it. Having tasted but a single drop, all other nectar becomes like dust. One will not even long for the divine amrit after that. Because to love God, to be given a heart that loves Him to the point of being aflame, it is so blissful that no words remain. Even the description of satchitananda feels pale and small in front of this love. Never ask for the feeling of intense longing and missing the SatGuru to be taken away because you’re finding its fire too strong. If it really goes, even one second will feel like millennia in hell.
Pray to Maa in these three aspects to give you a heart that can love, that weeps with intense devotion just thinking about His lotus feet.
