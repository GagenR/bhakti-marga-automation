Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

dinsdag 22 september 2026, 21:56
Hanuman ji is the epitome of constant yearning and constant being with his beloved Bhagavan Sri Ram.
Yearning for Bhagavan’s company and being in His company, they seem like opposites. But Hanuman ji was in this dual state simultaneously. Constant yearning and constant company. The two forces pulling in opposite directions were fully active within him. Driving him to ecstatic tears of bhakti and the desperation to serve continuously. Even a drop of Hanuman’s mood is so difficult to handle, yet he asked for it to be within him for eternity. To never forget his Sri Ram and to never stop serving Him. To never forget, one has to be in endless agony of missing the dearest of one’s heart. And to never stop serving, one has to be in His company all the time. Hanuman’s tears of bhakti were the sweetest gift given to Sri Ram. He alone knew the depth of Hanuman’s agony and bliss. To sustain Hanuman’s body, it was impossible moment by moment, feeling the full force of these two immense, highest, spiritual states. It was Sri Ram’s pure will and love that kept Hanuman’s body intact. Sri Ram’s infinite prem has kept Sri Ram Bhakta Hanumat alive, filled with endless grace, made him eternal. Chiranjeevi. That word applies to Hanuman fully. The one who lives forever. Whose love has become an eternal example for everyone. Whose service keeps this world moving. Whose devotion brings tears to the eyes of the King of Kings, Bhagavan Himself.
