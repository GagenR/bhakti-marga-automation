Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

dinsdag 20 oktober 2026, 19:30
Today is Dussehra or Vijay Dashami, the day that we celebrate the Nine Victories and bring them back into daily life as the Tenth Victory. On this sacred day, we celebrate Ma Bhagavati’s complete victory over all asuras and negative forces. Sri Ram’s eternal victory over Ravan. The Pandavas retrieving their weapons and getting ready for war against the evil-minded Kauravas. The Nine Victories during Navaratri are victories over the nine gates of the body that keep one bound to the material world. And Vijay Dashami or Dussehra brings one the complete experience and surrender to Bhagavan inside and outside. All is handed over to Him and dissolves into His Anubhuti. The SatGuru is the embodiment of that Anubhuti, His presence in this world is living proof of Bhagavan’s love for all. On Vijay Dashami, we not only celebrate the victories we have already achieved, rather today is about ascending to the topmost heights of spirituality and bringing that awareness back down to change the grounded reality of the world around us. Bringing our spiritual lessons into actions inspired by love and humility to uplift everyone. The final attainment isn’t for oneself alone, it is for everyone. To uplift the entire world. The SatGuru’s grace is to be shared with all. Like a candle can light infinite other candles without losing anything. Its own light is not reduced at all. In fact, the brightness it experiences can be thousandfold increased by lighting others’ paths. This is the sweet and simple learning that arises from within when one has surrendered everything to Bhagavan Sri Hari. That all possessions are His, all creatures are His, even oneself and everything one seems to possess is His own. Then one’s prayer changes from wanting to have, to wanting to give. Give service to all. Give love to all. Give kindness to all. One brings oneself down the mountain of spirituality not to get reattached to worldliness but to show all what one has received and how to get there themselves. This descent from the Ajna to the Muladhar is equally important. Not forgetting that one’s true home is at the lotus feet of the SatGuru, one brings that same grace everywhere. Never forget what one has received from the Divine. Never forget the closeness one has experienced to the SatGuru’s heart. And bring that hope and light to all. This is Vijay Dashami, the lighting of the lamp of victory in everyone’s heart. Shubh Dussehra to all. | Sri Vitthala Giridhari Parabrahmane Namah ||
