Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 10 mei 2026, 15:43
The Mother is the divine herself through her selfless service to the family.
Her focus and care for everyone. Leaving her birth home and becoming a beacon of joy and love in a new family. Opening her heart so much that she can accommodate all as her own. This is the heart of the divine mother. Each mother is a unique part of the eternal divine mother. Loving, caring, fiercely protective, joyful, innocent, endless facets of Her beauty. Countless forms of the divine mother are present on this planet itself. Beyond the personality of this world, everyone is a spark of the divine. But how many times do we truly see them like that? We’re often stuck in the external and limited; however, this day and all other celebrations of the divine should remind you of that eternal loving reality behind everything. The deep love of a mother gives such a close glimpse of God’s own love. So much so that when Bhagavan, the SatGuru, incarnates in this world, his first connection is also with the mother. That endless divinity also longs for Her unconditional love and care, the innocent and fully offered self.
And when the SatGuru’s divine self is revealed to the world, the verse written by Guru Arjan Dev Ji fits the SatGuru so perfectly. “gurdayv maataa gurdayv pitaa gurdayv suaamee parmaysuraa”. (Meaning- The Divine Guru is my mother, the Divine Guru is my father, the Divine Guru is my Lord and Supreme God.)
May the divine mother shower her blessings on all. May all families, mothers, fathers, and children, be inspired to be with each other with love, care, joy, and peace.
Happy Mother’s Day to everyone.
