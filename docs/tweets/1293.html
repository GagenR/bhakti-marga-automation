Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 26 maart 2026, 13:11
Glories be to Sri Ram, the most compassionate one.
He was known by so many names. Raghunandan. Raghav. Maryada Purushottam. Thousands of names. Yet the single call of Ram has always been enough for His bhaktas to be overcome with love. Look throughout the Dashavatar. The came and left flawlessly. Yet Sri Ram is different. He allowed others to call him flawed. To question his motives. Even till now, millennia later, many people still misunderstand him.
Yet he was never wrong.
Throughout all the births in this world, there have been none like Sri Ram. None even in the cosmic realm. He held himself to the highest standard but allowed others to treat him lower than low if they wished. He allowed nature to take its course in his life but stood like a dam preventing the flow of pain to others. He has silently accepted while history maligned His name. Destroyed his home again and again. Because he didn’t come to receive worship. He came to show that God is all things. Even if you throw His name into the mud. Even if you take apart His life and infiltrate it with lower thoughts. No matter how much you stand against Him. His works slowly reveal the truth. Again and again. Throughout the ages bhaktas have come and gone who have loved Ram beyond everything else. Because they understood the priceless value of a heart that has been pierced with arrows of judgement yet still radiates only the utmost gentle love. Such is the divinity of Ram.
One only needs to turn to Ram once and the heart is shocked by the intensity of His presence and love. Hanumanji has stayed in this world to constantly bring this deep truth outward into the world. The story that Hanumanji personally witnessed and narrated to Goswami Tulsidasji is beyond compare. There were no decisions of injustice. No pain delivered to others. No one who was unhappy because of Ram. But Ram himself silently bore the stones cast in His direction through the long years that have passed since His incarnation. Again look at the Dashavatar, the eternal relationship between Lord Shiva and Bhagavan Sri Hari is most gloriously revealed in the Ram Avatar. Why? Because Bhagavan came so vulnerably.
So complete yet so hidden in His divinity. So much hidden that people still wonder if He hurt his most dear one in the most atrocious way possible.
Ask your heart, “Can my Sri Ram truly be as He has been portrayed by fools? Or, have imperfect minds fabricated a history that can allow Ram to be pulled down from His perfect pedestal?” The one who didn’t seek any pedestals but deserves all of them.
Such is my Sri Ram.
