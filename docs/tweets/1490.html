Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 7 oktober 2026, 03:01
By the grace of the SadGuru, the heart learns where true refuge lies. Whom is there to seek, and whom is there to worship, when all that exists has arisen through Sri Hari? Every form that appears before us is temporary; even those who seem mighty and exalted must one day return to the dust. But the Fearless Lord of true Love, beyond both form and formlessness, remains untouched by time. He is the Destroyer of fear, the giver of peace, and the source from whom every blessing flows. O beloved Sri Hari, Your grace alone is enough; Your gifts fill the heart in a way that nothing of this world can. Why bow inwardly before temporary power, or become dependent upon those who themselves are dependent upon You? When the mind turns toward the Lord with love and remembrance, an unseen abundance begins to unfold. The Lord becomes the sustenance of the seeker, nourishing not only the body but the deepest hunger of the soul. His wealth is not measured in possessions, for the treasure He gives cannot be exhausted. It is the wealth of peace, devotion, wisdom, and freedom from fear. In that remembrance, the heart becomes still, and the soul enters a joy that has no worldly cause. By the compassion of the SadGuru, the seeker is drawn beyond separation and brought into the sacred awareness of Divine Union. There, nothing more is needed, nothing more is sought; one simply rests in Sri Hari, absorbed in His eternal peace.
