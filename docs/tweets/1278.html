Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

The first three days of Navaratri are the most subtle and quietly inward.
Full silence of the mind. How many people would love to achieve this in the world. Mostly everyone. Because the mind is fighting with one constantly.
The first three days that we celebrate, Shailputri, Brahmacharini, and Chandraghanta Ma are the representations of achieving complete silence of mind. Where penance becomes so strong that Japam of Bhagavan’s Name becomes the mind’s natural state, Ajapa Jap. Where purity becomes so resilient that it can defeat the strongest negativity. Where the fight becomes against one’s own asuric tendencies and not with anyone else. One starts to see one’s own self within all aspects of life and every creature. Then who will you hate? You won’t be able to hate anyone. Only love exists then.
The SatGuru continuously talks about Japam. It is only through continuous remembrance of Bhagavan that one can become pure, resilient, and capable. Pure in body and mind. Resiliently anchored in humility. Capable of loving all the time. The saints used to talk about coloring the mind with God’s love. This is the Tyaga of the world, to become so enamored with the Beloved that all self identity vanishes. To honor the first three aspects of Maa, go deep inside your consciousness and find that immovable place within that only loves.
