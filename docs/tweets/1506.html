Jai Ma Kushmanda.
Looking at Devi’s enormous trust in Him, the SatGuru, Bhagavan, also placed enormous trust in her. This is the way He always works, with great love and trust in His bhaktas. And when He sees that someone is truly making themselves ready, He pours everything. Bhagavan established His cosmic presence around Devi Parvati and raised Her consciousness to the Anahat Chakra where one is truly freed from all shackles. It is extremely difficult to reach the Anahat Chakra and establish oneself there, not falling again and again into any lower tendencies. This is where Devi’s fourth form arises from, the heart. Kushmanda. The one who emerges from the golden egg, the golden sphere, which is hidden deep within the heart. When one makes every effort to walk on the spiritual path, the thin outer shell of that golden egg is broken, revealing the vast mysteries of the One hiding within. This is the breaking of all karmic shackles surrounding the atma. The rejoicing of the Atma in facing the Antaryami is felt throughout the universe, hence Devi Kushmanda’s name also means the warmth or radiance of the Brahmand. And, Ku also means tiny. The tiny particle of illumination within the Self that is enough to shake the universe with its power of love. Her name is so beautiful, with so many lessons to give us. Love can shake the Immovable. It can change what is unreal into the Real. The brilliant light of true unconditional love forever removes any trace of darkness from the mind. The mind emerges as a luminous entity, capable of handling Bhagavan’s grace, completely devoid of ego. The same mind which was one’s worst enemy, emerges as one’s best friend. This is the emergence of Devi Kushmanda, may She bless everyone to firmly remain in the consciousness of love and let go of any material attachments.
| Om Aim Hreem Kleem Kushmandayai Namah ||
