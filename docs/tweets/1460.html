Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

To receive Diksha from the SatGuru is totally different from simply learning about the practice or technique.
This universe is a play of energy. A play of movement and stillness. The same one Source of energy that generates the entire Brahmand is the same one that exists within the body and mind, giving them life. The SatGuru enters the world and stays here to uplift all into His space, a state of being that is beyond all dimensions and time. There is a deep difference between having knowledge or information about something and actually embodying that wisdom and being immersed in its state of existence. For example, one can look up information about mantras on the internet, but that doesn't mean one embodies that Sadhana. For one to be immersed into sadhana, the SatGuru opens the portal to that energy. The energy accepts one into itself. Bhagavan accepts one close to His feet. The mercy of the SatGuru is without words. Because with His own efforts, He becomes the bridge between the two worlds, which otherwise would take countless years for the sadhak to open on their own and even then there is no guarantee that one can cross over the ego completely. The SatGuru is the father who protects and carries the child across the swamp of worldliness while allowing attacks upon His on self. He continuously keeps aside his own hurt to bring His child to the ultimate reality. But that Ultimate Reality isn’t foolish. It isn’t callous or indifferent. It cares deeply how one has reached Its doors. If one has simply made use of the immense love of the SatGuru and never been truly grateful and humble, the Ultimate Reality keeps Its doors eternally shut. But when one sees the painstaking efforts of the SatGuru towards one’s wellbeing and develops true wisdom, gratitude, and humility, then that door shows signs of opening. Endless efforts, endless gratitude, endless faith, endless love—the door of Bhagavan’s heart is opened with these. This is what one starts embodying through the SatGuru’s Diksha. Don’t go into the constant chattering of the mind; become still and experience the boundless space of consciousness which has been opened through His love.
