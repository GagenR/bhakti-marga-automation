Jai Ma Katyayani.
From the aspect of Devi Skandamata arises the form of Ma Katyayani. It was She who slayed Mahishasur, the demon representing all the lower tendencies and beastly qualities. She shows us through Devi Skandamata that gentle love for her children is the deepest part of her heart, but her external form often has to look fierce and be strict. Because the world is filled with both dark and light, good as well as bad. If the SatGuru or Ma Bhagavati only showed their sweet forms as the ultimate, many people would take them for granted. Even spiritual people often allow themselves to get inflated with various forms of pride. And this spiritual pride is very hard to uproot because one wants to hold onto it as a ‘divinely inspired’ way of thinking or behaving. Ma Katyayani emerges to protect the Anahat. Many times bhaktas let go of their original intention of walking on the spiritual path. Kindness, sweetness, humility, generosity, equanimity—are all forgotten—and one becomes puffed up with negative qualities and hypocritically sugarcoats the negative as good. This is exactly what Mahishasur tried to do, he wanted to trick Devi by appearing as a sacred bull. But She can never be fooled. She is Yogmaya Herself, appearing in the world through the direct will of Sri Hari Narayan. She is the final barrier to be crossed when one wishes to reach the SatGuru’s sacred lotus feet. But it is for this exact reason that the final crossing cannot happen through one’s effort, it is in Bhagavan’s hands entirely—because one often doesn't know whether one is hypocritically sugarcoating one’s bad behavior or one is genuinely behaving in a good way. That is why Bhagavan chooses when Ma Bhagavati should test one and when She should bring one to Him, for the truth of the inside to be revealed without a trace of hypocrisy. May Ma Katyayani, Yogmaya Devi, help everyone to keep pulling all lower tendencies towards the true unconditional love of the Anahat and fiercely guarding that sweet humble love.
| Om Aim Hreem Kleem Katyayanyai Namah ||
