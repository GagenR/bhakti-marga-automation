Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 21 januari 2026, 22:32
On the spiritual path people often feel frustrated that things are tougher than expected. Have you ever seen a plant growing? It grows its branches, leaves, flowers, fruits and becomes higher and taller. But at the same time its roots also dig into the earth deeper, they sink lower and lower. The more the tree grows higher, the more the roots sink deeper. Do you think this is just a coincidence? It is not. Let’s look at another example. To grow their wings, little birds just jump from their cozy nests. Not knowing what’s underneath. Not caring about predators. Not bothering about how they will find food and shelter in the future. They just jump. And in the middle of falling, they find the strength to lift themselves and rise higher than ever.
Nature teaches everything. We just don’t bother to look. One needs to become more and more grounded in order to reach greater heights. The troubles of the hard earth and sharp rocks have to also be faced by oneself, but the free air and towering shelter to everyone is also one’s own. The danger of falling into the abyss gives one the internal strength to face everything and rise above it all.
This is how the SatGuru trains His bhaktas. Unimaginable divine grace as well as the strongest winds and storms. He is the Great Gardener who creates the most life-giving trees. The Great Mother who teaches Her babies to become completely strong within themselves. He leaves not a trace of weakness inside. Yet fills all with the most gentle, compassionate, loving friendship towards all. Such a paradox of experiences. The SatGuru is the Great Master who dances in the infinite beauty of Prem and Bhakti. When He is always on your side, why let petty frustrations and troubles disturb you so much? Learn to dance to the joy of life.
