Don’t make spirituality so serious.
Spirituality doesn’t have to mean that one stops being fun or enjoying life. See the happiness and humour all around. Life is extraordinary. Live it to the fullest. The best part of life is that it doesn’t last forever. That awareness doesn’t need to make you scared, it can make you enthusiastic. To experience each moment rather than doing ‘time pass’. To let go of grudges rather than holding onto them. To choose love rather than pain. There is so much joy everywhere, so much innocence, so much love. Don’t make spirituality heavy. Spirituality is light. In both ways - where darkness and heaviness is transformed into lightness and light. Enlightenment. Words are not used just like that. See their beauty and understanding their many facets. Enjoy the time you have here. Give so much love that others also enjoy their time here. Being completely attached to Bhagavan, to the SatGuru, is to thoroughly enjoy life. Don’t only keep “working” on your spirituality. Enjoy your spirituality. Let it make your heart leap with joy. Unconditional love is pure joy. SatChitAnanda. Meditate with joy. Contemplate with joy. Transform with joy. Don’t make things too heavy, be a source of innocent joy for yourself and all.
