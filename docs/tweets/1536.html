Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

maandag 26 oktober 2026, 21:39
There is none as helpless as we are, nor any as compassionate as You, O Lord of Love, Sri Hari. How long must our hearts endure this separation? What more remains to be tested in souls that have nothing left to offer but tears, faith, and an unceasing longing for You? May our restless minds find eternal refuge at Your lotus feet, and may the divine Word of the SadGuru illuminate our path. Bless this humble servant with perfect surrender, unwavering devotion, and the grace to desire nothing apart from You. Let our very existence become a sacred sacrifice unto Sri Hari. O Beloved Lord, why does Your silence pierce our hearts so deeply? Across countless incarnations, we have wandered far from You, forgetting our eternal home in Your boundless love. Yet this life, with all its joys and sorrows, we lay entirely at Your feet. We breathe by placing our hopes in You, and we endure by trusting in Your mercy. How long has it been since our eyes were blessed with the sacred vision of Your Darshan? O Hari, You alone know the depths of our longing, the wounds hidden within our hearts, and the silent prayers that rise from our souls when words fail us. We have sought peace in this fleeting world, only to discover that no earthly comfort can replace the sweetness of Your presence. Let every desire dissolve in remembrance of You, every thought become a hymn of praise, and every breath carry the sacred echo of Your divine name. When our faith trembles, hold us close; when our minds wander, lead us back to Your lotus feet; when our strength fails, let Your grace sustain us. May we never forget the SadGuru’s sacred Word, nor turn away from the path that leads us towards You. We ask not for riches, worldly honour, or freedom from every sorrow, but for a heart that remains Yours through every trial. If tears must flow, let them flow in remembrance of You; if we must wait, let us wait with devotion; if we must endure separation, let our longing deepen our surrender. Come, O Beloved Sri Hari, awaken our sleeping souls, dispel the darkness of ignorance, and draw us beneath the shelter of Your infinite compassion. Let us behold Your divine Darshan, rest in the sweetness of Your presence, and find our eternal home in Your love. This life is Yours, our hopes are Yours, our souls are Yours. Until the final breath, may we remain at Your feet, calling Your name with unwavering faith: O Sri Hari, accept us, guide us, and never let our hearts turn away from You.
