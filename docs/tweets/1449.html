Is one able to question one’s thoughts or beliefs?
“What if my perspective isn't the only perspective? What if that which I have heard or even experienced isn't the highest truth of that situation? … What if I am wrong?” Is one truly okay with this type of self-analysis? Or does one stick one’s head in the sand and pretend nothing is happening? One should leave open the possibility of being wrong. Because then one can actually hunt for the truth. If one has already determined what the conclusion is, there is no hunt for truth. Very often, one’s dogmatic thoughts show up in one’s speaking patterns. The words one uses are a great clue to knowing whether one is stuck in a particular belief system or one is truly open-minded. For example, one looks down on someone and thinks of them in a certain way. However, what if one has never seen the true reality? What if one’s perspective was genuinely proven wrong? But in the meantime one has judged and executed them. There isn’t always an easy way back to repair the hurts one causes. And that’s why one often creates many, many future lives for oneself. To recreate the possibility of healing that deep-rooted hurt. When someone is 100% sure of something to the point of not even being able to meaningfully discuss or being open to other points of view, become aware and cautious. There are infinite facets to every subjective truth that should be explored to allow the Universal Truth to emerge from within and outside. And the Universal Truth, God, is ever-growing. Something that is stagnant, can it be a Universal Truth in an ever-evolving world? Observe which qualities allow your humanity and spirituality to keep growing positively and joyfully, into true freedom.
