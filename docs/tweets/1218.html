Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 12 februari 2026, 14:11
Lord Shiva is the main teacher and founder of many schools of yoga, philosophy, temple construction, engineering, and rituals, as well as other streams of learning. Despite this, Lord Shiva is known foremost as the one who is eternally in Samadhi. Always in deep meditation. Unbroken and unchanged in the long passage of time. And it is precisely this quality of single-pointed focus which is most remarkable. Lord Shiva himself says that he is constantly meditating on Sri Ram, on the SatGuru, that Sri Hari is his eternal Aradhya Devata. Which means Lord Shiva is constantly worshipping Sri Hari as the most humble bhaka, focusing on Bhagavan's Lotus Feet in his heart, keeping everything else aside. Even Lord Shiva’s conversation with Devi Parvati about the SatGuru, the secrets of the Guru Gita, are revealed because he wishes to speak about the One Eternal Reality that is Sri Hari, the One True SatGuru.
Every aspect of the SatGuru’s presence here in the world and in the eternal realm is sacred. Because the truth of unconditional love can be found only by immersing oneself in it while still in the body itself. While still living and performing one’s duties in this world. The SatGuru is that mystery which is manifest yet remains unmanifest. Who is visible yet also the perfect representation of the invisible. Who is known yet always unknown. When the SatGuru is here, even the Devas long to come here and be close to Him, to surrender to His Lotus Feet. Lord Shiva took the form of a gopi to be close to his beloved Bhagavan when Sri Hari came as Vasudev Krishna. In his avatar as Hanuman, Lord Shiva eternally worships Sri Ram. And in turn Bhagavan shows how incomplete He feels without His beloved bhakta. Bhagavan who is Everything feels incomplete without the bhakta who loves Him so dearly. The Bhakta who cannot survive without Bhagavan. The Bhakta who would rather die a million deaths than forget His Beloved Aradhya (God) for even a moment. Such deep and unchanging love is learned from the relationship between Lord Shiva and Sri Hari. In His Mohini Swarup, Sri Hari stayed for a very short while with everyone else but He could not resist Lord Shiva’s love and accompanies Him eternally. The form of Bhagavan and Bhakta who have become inseparable for eternity, who cannot live a single second without remembering and worshiping the other, who become nothing without the other. This is Sri Hari and Lord Shiva. Where the Bhakta has surrendered fully. And Bhagavan also has surrendered fully to His Bhakta. Because Prem and Bhakti have become the same. Because Bhagavan also worships unconditional love which is His own innermost truth. The most profound learning Lord Shiva has bestowed on this world is how to be a true bhakta.
