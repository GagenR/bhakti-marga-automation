Jai Ma Chandraghanta.
After her intense penance, Devi Parvati’s third form emerged wielding a bell in her hand. She had earned the blessing of constant awareness of Bhagavan’s lotus feet. The endless ringing of the bell was a continuous reminder to stay focused. To stay awake. She let go of sleep entirely. The body and mind were trained so thoroughly in Sadhana and Bhagavat Seva that His grace itself carried her body and mind. She received rejuvenation and performed her Dharma through the power of that grace, completely freeing herself of any tiredness or need to disengage from her set goal. Ready for battle with internal and external asuras, ready for deep Samadhi and vulnerability towards Bhagavan, ready for continuous Seva, within the blink of an eye She could switch from one to the other. Requiring no rest or time off. There was no concept of ego left within because every moment of her life was offered to Bhagavan. The moon on Her head was a reminder of the mind constantly oscillating between desires and aversions, peace and agitation. Devi Chandraghanta wears the moon as a crown because she has mastered the mind. She surrendered all thoughts at her SatGuru’s, Bhagavan’s, lotus feet. And He bestowed upon her the steady, equanimity of the mind. The names of Devi are very powerful reminders of how one should learn and progress on the spiritual path. May Devi Chandraghanta bless everyone with a steady and awake mind, ready to do what the Divine wills for oneself and the world in the blink of an eye. | Om Aim Hreem Kleem Chandraghantayai Namah ||
