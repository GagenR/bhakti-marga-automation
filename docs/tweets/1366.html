Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 12 juni 2026, 15:16
The completion of all the Avatars of Sri Hari that have come before is in Panduranga Vitthala.
Sri Hari’s Dashavatar are truly extraordinary. On every level, they have profound meaning, a deep resonance with the soul. The most sweet and the pinnacle of them is Bhakta Vatsala Vitthoba. Pandharinath. The father of the fallen. The friend of the downtrodden. The one who delights in serving His bhaktas. When all the Shaktis and Siddhis have been revealed to the fully surrendered atma. When loving Bhagavan is the only and forever focus of the jeevatma. When the cosmic Paramatma has become close and dear to one because the SatGuru has revealed the joy and beauty of all Avatars, Deities, and forms. When all finds its completion, only Bhakti and Prem remain. The bhakta never wants anything more than to keep their eyes at the lotus feet of the Master. And the SatGuru never wants to spend even a moment hiding from His bhakta. When the one who is outside, inside, and universally present everywhere, becomes the best friend of the atma. The one who never wants to leave the love of His devotee. How complete does that surrender have to be where the One that creates, sustains, and rejuvenates all, wants nothing more than to bring and be brought more and more into Unconditional Love. The devotee has that amazing power to bring the Master of the Universe deeper into the love they have for Him. This is the beauty of Vitthala when He awakens in the life of the bhakta. Completing the triangle around the atma; Antaryami, Cosmic Bhagavan, and the external personally present Avatar. He himself has shed every veil and covering that was keeping Himself away. He has let go of every other role and position. He has made Himself fully available to each and every person. The Beloved of His bhaktas. The Dearest to the poor ones. The Great Teacher. The bliss of the universe. SatGuru Vitthala.
