Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 14 augustus 2026, 12:55
Often people wonder, “if one was already with God at the beginning, then what is the need for creation, for coming here, or for going on the spiritual journey, just to go back to Him one day?”
This question only comes to those who have still not understood life. How extraordinary is this gift given by Bhagavan. That one is eternally with Him yet has the experience of separation, just to see how truly glorious He is. That Great Being which the atma loves with all its heart. In a simple way; when one identifies so much with the body, can you have gratitude for its divine origin? How perfect is its every organ, bone, and vein. How strong yet delicate it is. How the five elements come together so perfectly, that science has not been able to understand even the smallest atom of it. In the same way, to truly know Bhagavan, how sweet, how loving, all-pervading, omnipotent and omniscient He is - one takes on this illusion of separation for a short while. This is life. The atma willingly takes on this veil of forgetfulness so that in remembering again, it is struck with greater wonder how amazing Paramatma is. How amazing its relationship with Paramatma is. The atma is never separated from Bhagavan, but it consciously humbles itself into a forgetful little being, so that it can gaze up in wonder towards its Beloved. The one who it serves with love constantly. Even a small moment of this realization is so blissful that the atma is willing to go through anything just to know its dearest Bhagavan’s infinite prem. To be a servant of that Great Being, the atma is forever ready. The flow of creation from and towards Bhagavan is prem and bhakti themselves. Don’t look down upon this gift of life that one has been given. No matter how difficult things may seem in the moment, they are nothing in the face of Unconditional Love’s eternity. Enjoy thoroughly, love fully, and share your gratitude for God’s presence with all. Then all of life’s misery will flow away as one takes a dip in the innermost Sangam of prem, bhakti, and sharanagati.
