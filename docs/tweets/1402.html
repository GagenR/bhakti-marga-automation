Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

woensdag 29 juli 2026, 11:05
Guru Purnima is the day when the jeevatma is reborn in its pure state as the atma.
It is the day of celebrating all the teachers in one’s life. And when one is with the SatGuru, one should remember that every moment is a celebration of Guru Purnima in your life. The SatGuru’s presence in this world is against all odds. The possibility of His incarnation is almost impossible. Yet He comes here breaking all the cosmic laws and rules. Just for love. To give everyone who wishes to receive, the unending bliss of God’s presence and love. The best gift one can give in return is to not take Him for granted. We have been discussing learning about the path over the past few days. By deepening your studies, you need to ensure that your life becomes a testimony of love and care for all. Read about the lives of the saints. They became living proof of their Guru’s divinity, love, and mercy. And the bhakta who are with the SatGuru have with them everything they need. In so many ways the SatGuru has reached out to everyone. Have you allowed it to touch your heart? Have you experienced being unable to stop the tears of love from flowing till your eyes burned? Guru Purnima isn’t a festival only of worship and respect, it is a blossoming of the inside. Transformation into the depths of devotion where one’s being itself becomes longing for the SatGuru’s lotus feet. Longing is always exceedingly joyful, not an ounce of misery can remain. If one has not experienced Love that feels like the heart would just burst from one’s chest, then life has not shown its greatest colors to you yet. A single drop of Bhagavan’s love would burn everything you know until only bliss and surrender remained. Blessed are those who wish to be consumed by love because they have known God truly. On this radiant full moon day of the Guru, give the SatGuru the gift of your joy and remembrance. He has everything He needs but He wants you close to Him, so that your atma can fulfill its desired goal. Blessings and much love to everyone.
