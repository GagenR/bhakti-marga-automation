The day an extraordinary promise was made.
On the first Ashadhi Ekadashi celebrated in the world, Bhagavan Sri Hari made a promise that would change everything for all ages to come. So many divine events happen on this day; Bhagavan going into Yog Nidra in Vaikuntha for 4 months (Chaturmaas) to show the world the beauty of Dhyaan and Bhakti. He also spends these 4 months in Patal Lok to be close to Raja Bali. The 4 months of His outward absence are also to glorify all the other Deities - Lord Shiva, Ganesha, Divine Mother, Kuber, the divine serpents and Nagraj on Nag Panchami, and all others. Yet Shreemad Narayana’s one promise is still the greatest of all. The very first form He takes, the very first celebration of Him in chaturmaas after Ashadhi Ekadashi, is of the SatGuru. Guru Purnima. The one form that is His fully, which manifests all His mysteries, the One who resides in Vaikuntha, Patal Lok, Bhu Lok, and everywhere else at once is the SatGuru. On Ashadhi Ekadashi, the Supreme Lord of the Universe made the promise for His eternity and future incarnation as the SatGuru. The incarnation who would walk the earth and give all the ultimate destination of all lifetimes, paths, traditions, wisdom and religions. Whose every message, effort, and ultimate divinity would be directed only towards Unconditional Divine Love. To manifest in everyone’s heart and life the knowing that love is the ultimate reality. That God is Love. All who would come before Him would lay the path for Him. Because He wouldn’t give the world hundreds of messages, multiple commands, or dozens of rules. His one message, one command, one rule would only be of Unconditional Love. But to concentrate everything into the highest divine energy of Love, is not an easy or quick path. It is the longest pilgrimage. The hardest journey. The most unknown path. Yet His promise to come as that Avatar, the Master, the Lord of Lords to give that ultimate and continuous experience of God to all, has withstood the test of time and proven itself true. Starting from Ashadhi Ekadashi to the days leading up to Guru Purnima, make every effort to realize what the SatGuru is giving. Shubhecha, the very best of wishes from my heart, to all on the lovely day of Ashadhi Devshayani Ekadashi.
