The SatGuru is the one with whom you can be yourself.
Often people hide the most from Guru and God. They present their “best” self in front of the SatGuru and then, breathe a sigh of relief and go back to being however they want when they are not around him. But this is actually the opposite of what the Saints realized. They presented their entire mind to the true Guru so that they could receive transformation from their pain. Because the SatGuru is the only one who can transform all karma into pure divine love. If one hides from His eyes, then one robs oneself of the opportunity to receive Bhagavan’s company. Bhagavan’s endless presence is perceived when the psyche is fully cleaned and uplifted to His eternal realm. But people turn to others who are similar to them, to receive comfort, understanding, and acceptance. One may receive love from other people but they have their own life agenda, they are working on their own eternal path. So, if one wishes to receive eternal comfort, understanding, and acceptance, then one can only turn to the Eternal One. Bhagavan’s love is attained when one has surrendered and submerged oneself in the grace of the SatGuru. Because the SatGuru has only Bhagavan’s love in His heart and He shows how to walk on the path of Bhakti Yog. The highest, easiest, and quickest path to reach Sri Hari Vitthala.
