Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

maandag 22 juni 2026, 18:14
When a soul that has allowed itself to be polished so finely by the SatGuru leaves the body, Grace spreads around the world.
When a truly polished atma comes out of its mortal body to depart for Bhagavan’s lotus feet, it explodes His love everywhere. Like a huge divine shift in the energy of the world at the moment of its departure. Such an atma is surrounded with so much of the SatGuru’s love and mercy that it bestows the same blessing to all. It allows everyone to have a glimpse into the SatGuru’s eternal nature of love. The atma shows this secret to all for a split second. Because before leaving, such a bhakta wishes to reveal to all the greatest person, path, and destination that it has found. It wants all to turn to Him fully. Swamini Harisundari was such a beautiful person in this world. Her deep positivity and trust in her SatGuru. Battling with cancer and so many difficult moments, but just thinking of God and His abundant mercy. Her soul knew that her time in this world was very short. But she did everything and more to attain the ultimate aim of life, the lotus feet of Sri Hari. She spent her life serving and loving God in all she met. She wanted so much to stay to serve her SatGuru that God gave her 2 extra years to live, to fulfill that extraordinary desire of her atma.
Today is also the day that we celebrate Devi Dhumavati Jayanti, when Divine Mother releases all from tremendous diseases, bringing them to Bhagavan. It is miraculous how the Divine takes care and how God’s plan unfolds. May all keep praying for her soul and for Madhavadas as they go to Bhagavan’s home. These two dearest souls truly made their place in my heart. Focus on God in everything you do, in every moment of your life. Sadgati Prapti Rastu, Om Shanti.
