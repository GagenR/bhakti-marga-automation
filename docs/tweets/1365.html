Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 11 juni 2026, 14:12
Today is Parama Ekadashi, which only comes in Adhik Maas, once couple of years.
The extra month this year in the Hindu calendar is very auspicious. Adhik Maas or Purushottam Maas. And Ekadashi, Dwadashi, and Trayodashi are extraordinarily important days in this month. Parama Ekadashi is when Raja Harishchandra’s test was completed, and he received all the blessings from Bhagavan. Everything that Bhagavan had allowed to be taken away was given back to this topmost devotee of His. Observe Parama Ekadashi with joy and read the story of Harishchandra on this day with remembrance of Bhagavan Sri Hari. It can lead one to the state of surrender that Harishchandra attained, freeing one from any identification with the body, mind, and senses. Forever at the lotus feet of Bhagavan.
Dwadashi in adhik maas is also very important. It is also celebrated to honor the grace of Bhagavan Sri Hari. The sun transits through a zodiac sign every month; however, during Adhik Maas, the sun’s transit is very different. There could be no movement or even movement through two zodiac signs. Hence, the mysterious nature of Bhagavan’s love is remembered and worshiped.
Trayodashi is the crown jewel of adhik maas this year. The 13th day. There are so many divine facets to this day that it is impossible to describe. The highest combination of shiva and narayana; it is Shivratri in Purushottam Maas. The Purusha and His greatest bhaktas are all active during this time. May these days bring you closer and closer to the lotus feet of the SatGuru.
