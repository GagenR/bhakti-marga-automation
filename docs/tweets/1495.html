Maha-Amavasya and Shakti.
Ma Bhagavati is the pure potency of Bhagavan manifested in a Saguna form. She who holds the three worlds gently in Her palm, not allowing any lasting darkness to take over. She who is the gentlest towards the Saints, is also the fiercest towards the evildoers. Standing continuously in the darkness, She endlessly nurtures Her children to stay in the light. Her own light is the light of the atma. The atma that continuously reminds one of Bhagavan. How deep Her love must be to come in endless forms just to serve the Supreme Lord and show every atma from within itself, what it needs to strive for. Hence, like Bhagavan is called Prem Swarup, She is Prem Swarupini.
On Mahalaya Amavasya, Ma Bhagavati is present in her most subtle yet most pure form. She takes care of all Her children who are transitioning from the human world to the spirit world. She takes care of all those who are grieving the loss of their loved ones. She takes care of the balance of the world by emerging as the Nav Durga and giving everyone a cause for grand celebration, letting go of grief, and emerging into the bright light. Shailaputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, Kushmanda, Skandamata, Katyayini, Kaalrati, Maha Gauri, Siddhidatri. How endless is the love of Ma Bhagavati for everyone. She is Krishna Varnayai the one who is alike to Sri Krishna in appearance and nature. May Ma Bhagavati bless everyone on this day as we transition from delusion of the mind to the Reality of Bhagavan, from darkness towards light, from the cycle of life-and-death to immortality.
