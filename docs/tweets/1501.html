Jai Ma Brahmacharini.
The fiercest form of Ma Bhagavati is truly this second aspect. One often thinks that fierceness is what is shown externally. But in reality, the inner fire when it burns so consumingly that it burns one’s old self completely to be reborn from the ashes into one’s divine nature, this is the fiercest form that one can achieve. Because no pain is given to others, only divine service in every way. But towards the old self, the limited ego self, the Self keeps burning itself. Can you imagine how fierce one has to be for that process to continuously go on within the heart while still maintaining every duty and kindness towards all? The inner fierceness is continuously pushed back inwards to keep the fire of purification alive. This is true Brahmacharya. It is still a misunderstood concept, true Sanyas or Brahmacharya. Someone can be fully living in the world outside yet be fully free inside, due to their intense devotion to the SatGuru’s lotus feet alone. This intense determination to burn one’s ego for the inner purity to reveal itself, this is Brahmacharya, and everyone regardless of outside duty must walk this path. It is not an easy decision to leave everything except Bhagavan, yet one is endlessly willing to pay any price. Because living separated from the true inner vision of Bhagavan is too terrible and miserable for the jeevatma. This was the state of Ma Brahmacharini. There was an intense inner fire to attain the Lotus Feet that were hidden within her atma. She herself burned everything else within her. Her fierce focus shook the three worlds. This is the intensity and fierceness of focus a bhakta should have. The whole self must burn with love with Bhagavan, until nothing is left but that Love alone. May Ma Brahmacharini help everyone realize this infinite fire of love within the heart. Blessings. | Om Aim Brām Brahmachāriṇyai Namaḥ ||
