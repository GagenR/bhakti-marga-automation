Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zaterdag 24 januari 2026, 12:51
Two days ago was markandeya jayanti.
We pay respect to rishi Markandeya, the eternal rishi who is appears in many of our ancient stories. The entire Markandeya Puran is attributed to him. It is believed that Markandeya was son of sage Mrikanda (hence the name) who asked for a son from Shiva. Shiva mentioned that he could either have one son who is knowledgeable and illustrous but with a short life or a son who would live long but wont have any merit. The rishi chose the former and Markandeya was born who was destined to die at an early age. It is believed that because of immense knowledge, conduct and ascetic merit, he became favorite of gods and Brahma himself taught him the Maha Mrityunjay mantra that would give Shiva's protection against fatalities. It is said that he prayed to Shiva by worshipping shiva linga. AT the time, Yama the god of death came with his noose and tried to take him. According to some sources the noose fell on the lingam and in some sources Yama got proudy and declared that even Shiva couldnt stop him from taking life of Markandeya. At this Shiva appeared in an angry form from the lingam to attack Yama who begged forgiveness, and thus Shiva earned the name "Kaalantak" one who ended the kaal (death) of
Markandeya making the rishi eternal. He also appears in Mahabharat where he tells Yudhishthir that how he travelled after the great flood when the entire humanity was wiped out and then one day saw a little child on a Banyan tree who asked him to enter his mouth. When he did so, he saw all the different kinds of universe and its secrets and understood the endless and requested to come out. This form of Vishnu, Baalmukund was only seen by Markandeya.
Thus he is blessed by both Vishnu and Shiv. His quest as a seeker of knowledge again accentuates that our ancient rishis were first scientists who always questioned and tried to seek answers to unknown. May rishi Markandeya bless us with knowledge.
