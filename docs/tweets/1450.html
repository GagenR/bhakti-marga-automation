Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

Simply quoting the scriptures does not make you wise. Knowing spiritual concepts does not make you spiritual. What makes you spiritual is your deep love for God who is in everything and everyone, at all times.
Spirituality doesn’t mean to avoid pain or run away from disagreeable situations. It means to be happy knowing that true unconditional happiness can help solve the problems. When one is sad or angry, do the problems go away? They grow bigger instead. But when one strives to come out of the negative feelings and thoughts, then the problems go away quickly. Because the mind can easily and quickly see the solutions. This is why spirituality is a lived experience of unconditional love. It is not about anything external. It doesn't matter whether one can quote the scriptures. It doesn’t matter if one knows many philosophies or intellectual concepts. Because the inner state has to change. And that happens through endless love. Through trying to see God’s mercy in everything. It makes the mind naturally cleaner and open to Bhagavan’s, the SatGuru’s, grace. And this is pure joy. The joy of knowing one has come out of the negativity within oneself and started to see greater and greater beauty in life. Unconditional love is very easy to live, one simply has to turn to it. Allow His love to wash through oneself, immerse oneself in it.
