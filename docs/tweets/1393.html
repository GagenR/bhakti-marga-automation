Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 16 juli 2026, 08:59
The beautiful Rath Yatra of Bhagavan Sri Hari Jagannath has started and will continue for nine days.
Despite heavy rain, lakhs of bhaktas have gathered at Jaganannth Puri Dham. To celebrate their beloved Lord coming out of the Mandir and giving Darshan to all on the street itself. Not a small utsav murti like with most other processions, but the main murtis themselves are brought out. The bhaktas are not shortchanged in anyway. This is Bhagavan’s grace. This is His love for all. This is the way He yearns to be close to everyone. Every year during Rath Yatra, Bhagavan Jagannath's Rath halts for sometime at the samadhi of His dear devotee Bhakta Kavi (poet) Sant Salabega. Salabega was born to a muslim father but his devotion to Bhagavan Sri Hari Jagannath came from his brahmin mother. He was immersed in love for Bhagavan. Once Salabega was injured in battle but he accepted it all as grace and love from Bhagavan. And unable to stay away from such pure prem, Bhagavan miraculously healed Salabega within a single night. After that, Salabega gave up everything to devote his life to Bhagavan completely. One year, he was stuck in heavy rains and couldn’t reach Puri in time. He begged and prayed to the Lord to wait for him. And the most merciful beloved Lord, stopped His Rath from moving. No matter how much effort was exerted by those pulling it, the Rath wouldn’t move at all. Salabega arrived and in great ecstasy saw the Rath of the Lord that had stopped in the middle of the street for no apparent reason. Salabega ran forward with great joy and took Darshan in tears. In that moment, the Rath started moving again. The Lord keeps His promise to everyone. To love. To always be there. Mata Gundicha, Raja Indradumna, the residents of Vraj, Sant Salabega, everyone. But the saints believed in His promises with all their heart. They devoted themselves to Him when there was all kinds of opposition from society as well as from their own mind. Even when there was no external reason to love, they loved endlessly, unconditionally. And that great God of Love, Bhagavan Sri Hari Jagannath can never keep Himself away from such strong faith and laser-focused love. May this day inspire the devotees to grow their love for Bhagavan, to embrace His lotus feet in their heart and devote themselves to Him fully. Shubh Rath Yatra to all.
