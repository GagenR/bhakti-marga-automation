Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

Meditate on Sri Krishna.
Allow your mind to be permeated with His ever-new yet One eternal beautiful form. His extraordinary infinite names. His sweet voice that speaks to your soul and lifts it up with great bliss. Krishna is famously known as tribhanga, the one bent in 3 places, breaking the rigidity of the mind that perceives divinity as far from itself; removing any limitations and shackles of the three gunas prevalent in the three worlds. The Dust-Adorned Face (Godhuli Krishna), coming home after being in the forests of Vrindavan is infinitely more beautiful than any freshly polished jewel. His beautiful, mildly smiling face is completely dusted with the gray-golden earth kicked up by the hooves of thousands of returning cows. Sweat drops mingle with the sacred forest dust on His broad forehead, sparkling like fine diamonds under the rays of the setting sun. His beloved flute is on His lips playing a melody heard by each atma differently. The blue lotus petals of His eyes are slightly closed, enjoying the divine dance of Prem, Bhakti, and Sharanagati. His feet are bare with beautiful shining nails, and the earth itself becomes like butter as He gently takes each step. All of nature is uplifted by the presence of Love, chanting His name with their sweet calls. The unconditional love of the universe is Madhusudan Sri Krishna. The one who is showered by the nectar-like love flowing to Him from everywhere. Everywhere the bhaktas look, they only see their beloved Kanhaiya. Chant the Mahamantra as you meditate upon the glorious form of Sri Krishna. Om. Om. Om. Sri Vitthal Giridhari Parabrahmane Namah. Stay in this peaceful state as you contemplate upon Bhagavan’s beautiful form as He becomes the small divine child born in your heart, the Janamashtami that comes again and again. With the gentle waves of love, His cradle in your heart moves with joy. He comes to you longing for your pure love and nothing else. Sri Gurubhyo Namah Hari Om. May Sri Krishna shower His blessings on the whole world. Much love.
