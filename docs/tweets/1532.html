Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

donderdag 22 oktober 2026, 01:23
O mind, turn away from the distractions of the world and immerse yourself in the boundless glories of Sri Hari. Let His divine virtues become the treasure of your heart, and let His remembrance accompany you through every moment of life. The stories of the devotees bear witness to the transforming power of His grace. Pingala found a new direction when her heart turned towards the Divine, while Dhruva, through steadfast devotion, attained an imperishable state beyond fear and uncertainty. Gajendra, trapped in the deadly grip of the crocodile, discovered that when every worldly strength fails, sincere surrender to the Lord opens the way to divine protection. Sri Hari, the boundless Ocean of Compassion, does not turn away from the helpless soul that calls upon Him with faith. Even Ajamila, whose life had strayed far from righteousness, became an example of the extraordinary mercy contained within the remembrance of the Divine Name. These sacred accounts remind us that no heart is beyond the reach of grace when it sincerely turns towards the Supreme Lord. The SadGuru guides the seeker towards this eternal truth, teaching that liberation begins when the restless mind learns to rest in Sri Hari. Why, then, should one remain entangled in Maya, pursuing fleeting pleasures while forgetting the eternal source of peace? The Name of Sri Hari is a spiritual refuge, a light in the darkness of ignorance, and a bridge across the turbulent ocean of worldly existence. Vishwananda, keep the Lord ever present within your consciousness, for He is the Chintamani, the priceless jewel of divine fulfilment. When remembrance becomes constant and devotion grows sincere, the heart gradually loosens its attachment to illusion and discovers the freedom of loving surrender. Let every breath become an offering, every thought a prayer, and every action a humble expression of devotion. Take shelter in Sri Hari, and walk the path illuminated by the SadGuru, trusting that His grace can carry the soul beyond suffering towards the eternal abode of peace.
