Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zaterdag 21 november 2026, 12:02
The highest aspiration of the Atma is not merely to be liberated from the cycle of birth and death, but to be eternally immersed in loving service to Paramatma, where liberation itself becomes secondary to the sweetness of divine companionship. In this sacred relationship, humility becomes one’s ornament, surrender becomes one’s strength, remembrance becomes one’s nourishment, and Prem becomes one’s eternal abode. Tulsi Vivah is therefore not simply an event to be commemorated once a year, but a divine truth to be lived every day. It is the sacred journey from attachment to devotion, from self-centred desires to selfless love, from forgetfulness to remembrance, and from the illusion of separation to the realisation of eternal belonging. May the grace of Tulsi Maharani awaken within us that single-pointed devotion through which all other desires gradually lose their significance. May the blessings of the SatGuru illuminate our hearts so that we may recognise Bhagavan not only within the sanctum of a temple but within every aspect of existence. Vishwananda says may our hearts become the sacred dwelling place of Shreeman Narayan, where the flame of Bhakti remains forever radiant, untouched by the passing winds of worldly life. And may every Atma discover the boundless joy of belonging to its eternal Beloved, where love is without conditions, service is without expectations, surrender is without hesitation, and union is without separation. Shubh Tulsi Vivah to all. May the divine grace of Tulsi Devi, Shreeman Narayan and the SatGuru forever guide us towards the perfection of divine Prem. Jai Tulsi Maharani! Jai Shreeman Narayan
