Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

maandag 27 juli 2026, 10:26
Learning about the SatGuru’s path should make one more open-minded.
However, most often when one starts learning, one starts to put boxes and labels on things to store thoughts in the mind. One tries to do the same with the SatGuru all the time. Because it is very difficult for the mind to accept uncertainty or not knowing. However, a truly loving heart is always accompanied by a truly open mind. They cannot exist separate from each other. They help each other to become stronger and purer. So if one wants to walk on the path of spirituality, clean the mind and open it to infinite divine possibilities manifesting in each moment. Then both love and wisdom can arise. Because they always come in a pair. Bhagavan Sri Krishna did not tell Arjun to love Him and remain ignorant. He told Arjun that to be able to love Him fully, Arjun must first fully purify his mind. This is how it is explained in our Tadatmya Vedanta as well. The process with which one slowly moves to the anubhav or experience of oneness with Paramatma. Without this inner work, without completely purifying one’s mind, one can never surrender the mind fully. After complete purification, then the choice or willingness to surrender can actually arise. Before that, one is still working, trying to walk on the spiritual path. So even if the SatGuru showed one that His swaroop, roop, tattva, divinity, everything is the same as the Supreme Lord. That unlike for others, there is no difference in quantity or quality, for Him and the Supreme Lord. Would one be able to understand or accept? No. Because the mind has not become capable to receive such an enormous understanding or experience, the darshan of God is still far away. That is why it is not enough to read once and stop after the first wave of understanding. Read what the SatGuru has given so many times that it becomes one with your mind. Listen to His words so often that only His truth moves through your thoughts. Spend every hour of your life diving into the vast ocean that the SatGuru has opened for all. That is Love and Wisdom. Diving continuously in the ocean and collecting the pearls hidden within; Naam Smaran, Bhajan, Shravan, Paaraayan (devoted reading of divine words), and so many countless ways that the SatGuru has given to grow close to Him. Time always feels very short for a devoted bhakta. Because there are infinite ways to grow in His love, yet a finite number of breaths in this life.
