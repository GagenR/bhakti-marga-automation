Sri Vidya and the Sri Guru.
The One who is eternal gives eternal wisdom. The logic is so simple but the mind makes it extremely complicated. ‘What if this happens? What if that doesn’t happen? What if I don’t have any amazing experience?’ On and on goes the merry-go-round of the mind. And one enjoys it that is why one stays on it. But just be still and receive the wisdom from the inside. There is no need to go into philosophies and intellectual gymnastics. Know from inside the heart itself. Go deep within and find the wisdom you are searching for. When the SatGuru speaks, it is Sri Vidya. Because He embodies the state of eternity, the Bindu and beyond it. He is the one who can bring one out of material creation and establish the eternal kingdom of Bhagavan within and outside. The atma is always experiencing God’s love but doesn’t realize it, so the SatGuru removes the lack of perception of the mind. He elevates the mind to seeing God’s love everywhere, in what one considers good and also in what one considers bad. Loving all the way the Divine Mother loves all. The SatGuru distributes endless love and grace. This grace itself is Sri Vidya. The grace of knowing oneself to be a part of Bhagavan’s endless love. The grace of surrendering oneself to be eternally loved by Him. The grace of reaching for Bhagavan more and more. When one is in that state of love, nothing else exists, only Bhagavan remains. Don’t settle for less. Reach for the Highest. Attain everything the SatGuru is making so much effort to give.
