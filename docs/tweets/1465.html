Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zaterdag 19 september 2026, 16:30
Shubh Radha Ashtami and many blessings to all.
Today we celebrate Sri Radhe’s appearance day. Exactly one fortnight after the appearance of Bhagavan Sri Krishna. First the SatGuru arrived and following in His footsteps came His greatest bhakta. One was like a dark rain cloud about to burst with brilliant lightning, and the other like the morning sun growing brighter and brighter in gentle waves. Each of them was blinding for the impure, but the single-pointed focus for the humble. One of Radharani’s names is Tapta-Kanchani, the one who is like molten gold. Yes in appearance but even more so, She was the gold that constantly stays in the fire of Love. Not because the SatGuru or Bhagavan ever force anyone to do that, but because the atma wants to stay in that fire willingly, joyfully. Ready to be molded into the shape that God wants at any time. To always remember and be humble in front of the SatGuru, eternal Bhagavan. To keep one’s eyes on His lotus feet within the heart constantly. That is the separation that Radharani crossed over completely. Sri Krishna was inseparable from Her heart.
Tapta-Kāñcana Gaurāṅgī Rādhe Vṛndāvaneśvarī |
Vṛṣabhānu-Sute Devī Praṇamāmi Hari-Priye ||
(I offer my respectful obeisances to Shrimati Radharani, who is like molten gold, Queen of Vrindavan, the precious daughter of King Vrishabhanu, and the eternal beloved of Sri Hari Krishna.) Kurute virahā-valokanāṁ, kula-mugdha-mṛgīva vihvalām |
Muni-mānasa-haṁsa-rūpiṇīṁ, praṇamāmi sadā hi rādhikām ||
(When separated from Sri Krishna for even a moment, she looks for Him in her heart anxiously like a frightened forest deer. She is the divine swan that resides in the minds of the greatest sages. I bow down forever to that supreme Sri Radhika.)
Jai Sri Radhe Vrindavanishwari. Jai Satgurudev Sri Hari Krishna.
