Shubh Ganesh Chaturthi.
The appearance of the deity of wisdom, Ganpati. One first receives life from the mother. Just like Ganpati’s mother, Devi Parvati gave life to him. She lovingly formed his body with turmeric, chandan, and reddish earth. From her own self, the mother nurtures her child. She breathes life into her child. A piece of the Divine Mother is within each jeevatma, all are her children. Then she gently sends her child into the world, strong, full of energy, filled with purpose. But the child hasn’t yet come face to face with Bhagavan. The one hidden within the child’s heart. The one hidden within the Divine Mother’s heart. When innocence, purity, dharma, and love reach their perfection, when one has become truly Sattvic, then Bhagavan comes. But does He come softly? Often, no. Because Sattvic ahamkar, spiritual ego, has taken over the jeevatma. That is how Lord Shiva came and cut off the ahamkar that was preventing the jeevatma from recognizing the true self. When the ego is cut off fully, it is the death of the lower self and the emergence of the divine self. Ganpati lets go of his old self fully and receives a divine identity from his SatGuru. That divine personality is bestowed by the SatGuru, one cannot get there through one’s efforts alone. Because it requires the removal of the entire ego identity. Yet Ganpati received it because he wanted it. He was waiting in true Sadhana, in complete awareness, when Devi Parvati left his side. He wanted to know for what he had been created with so much love. He wanted to know who was the focus of his mother’s single-pointed focus. Ganesh Chaturthi is the celebration of wisdom, right knowledge, which only awaits the arrival of the SatGuru, to free one from all confusion and receive eternal life. Blessings to all.
