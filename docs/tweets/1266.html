Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

maandag 16 maart 2026, 12:43
Everyone is a universe within themselves.
The atma and Antaryami eternally together, are the fullness of love within themselves. There is no external force, being, or person that can come between them. There is nothing external that can make them feel complete. Only within themselves.
It is this place of endless togetherness that one has to reach. Where the atma is ever fulfilled in love. Where the Antaryami can express His love fully. To receive His love is to receive everything. But it is not so easy to receive His immense love. Because one still has parts of oneself that have not come back inside to be with God. These are the distractions and attachments that are not yet fully anchored within God.
One needs to give up nothing, only offer everything to Bhagavan. If there is pain, offer it to Him. If there is anger, offer it to Him. If there is despair, offer it to Him. If there is joy, offer it to Him. Everything, every time, just offer it to Him.
Like a child rejoices in the mud he is playing with and offers it as a great treasure to the mother when She lifts him up with love. The same way we are all covered in mud, playing in the mud of maya. Just offer your heart to Him, keeping aside all doubts and concerns. In His enormous joy of loving you and seeing you turn to Him fully, He will pull you deep into His embrace. Love is never far from you. God is never far from you. He knows your pain. He knows your struggles. He is using them to bring you closer to Himself within your heart. To give you the eternal companionship you have always longed for. To bring you to the state of only experiencing the Antaryami, in all places, within everyone and everything, for eternity.
This is why the SatGuru in His completeness is found within the heart. He is that same Antaryami who has been waiting for you to turn to Him for long, long ages of the world.
