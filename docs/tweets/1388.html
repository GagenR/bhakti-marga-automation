Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

When the surrendered atma is moving towards Paramatma after leaving the body, so many divine stages are passed through.
At death, the atma comes out of the body as naturally as a flower embedded in ice comes out of the ice block when the water around it melts. The Will and Power holding the flower inside the ice block melts and becomes subtle; in a similar way, the atma is released from the body. Death is not painful. Especially not for a surrendered soul. Because Bhagavan makes it very simple. A natural letting go of the heavier body into the lighter atmic body. The first stage the atma progresses through is letting go of the physical body completely. This is why in the Sanatan Dharma, offering the body to fire is the final Yagna. The human body is composed of the five sacred elements of nature: Prithvi (Earth), Jal (Water), Agni (Fire), Vayu (Air), and Akasha (Ether). Cremation uses Agni as the ultimate divine purifier to instantly return these components back to the cosmos. Hence, the five elements are completely freed from holding that form and the atma also quickly moves forward from that attachment. It realizes that it had, has, and always will have a greater identity than the physical body. This is of great help to the atma itself to continue its journey towards Sri Hari’s lotus feet. And, it also awakens everyone else to the unpredictable and temporary nature of life here. Love everyone you meet with all your heart. Love your life, it is absolutely perfect the way God has given it to you. Just keep loving endlessly. There never is any time to waste.
