Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

zondag 25 oktober 2026, 17:37
The most beautiful full moon today is called by many names. Its first name Sharad Purnima shows us the beauty of Sri Krishna, the second name Kojagiri Purnima shows us the beauty of the Divine Mother Mahalakshmi, and the most hidden name is Gupt Purnima. This is the rarest of all, to the point that it is almost entirely forgotten because it was only passed down through oral traditions from the great ancient sages. The Gupt Purnima ritual of sampurna amtanivedana bhakti was performed only when Sri Ram and Sri Krishna came to this world and stayed with everyone personally in human form. When the great incarnation of Bhagavan Himself is present in the world, the Gupt Rahasya becomes manifest—which means that the Secret Mystery of Bhagavan is revealed in its full glory. The Purna Avatar, the direct and complete manifestation of Sri Hari, comes to this world very rarely and always becomes the true Guru of this world. The One leading all into an age of greater divine love and higher consciousness. Just like Sri Ram and Sri Krishna came to do. This day of Purnima comes 3 full moons after Guru Purnima. The completion of the Trinity, Trimurti, and Teen Devis. The Sangam of the Ida, Pingala, and Sushumna. The Ekatva (oneness) of Paramatma, Bhagavan, and Antaryami. This is the hidden reality of Bhagavan, the SatGuru. Today when giving thanks to Sri Krishna, to Mahalakshmi, to your Ishtadev, don’t forget that the One who brought you to this experience of divine love, complete freedom, and endless joy is the only SatGuru. || Obeisances to the great Guru who gives everything to all, may His lotus feet be etched upon the heart forever.
