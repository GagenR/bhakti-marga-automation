Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda

vrijdag 13 februari 2026, 13:57
Lord Shiva as the SatGuru shows that even the great realizations of ‘tat tvam asi’, ‘aham brahmasmi’, ‘shivoham’ and others are simple by-products of Ananya Bhakti. That which people give so much importance to; self-realization, moksha, mukti from birth and death, and so on, are not even considered by a true bhakta. Such elevated states are not thought of, nor given any importance to. Because the true bhakta who has been touched by the unique divine love of the SatGuru knows that Bhakti and Prem are the actual highest states. Humility, Sharanagati, and single-pointed focus are given much more importance than any realization. Because one wishes to remain a servant of the Divine. And that is the greatest realization of the atma. That is the most close one can truly become to Paramatma. That is true union, true merger with Bhagavan. To be in loving service to Him always. Not deludedly think one actually ‘become’ Bhagavan or one is equal to Him in anyway.
The states of non-duality, non-difference between the self and the Creator, are states of oneness experienced by a true bhakta, a true lover and servant of God. One can never experience these states of realization that Lord Shiva talks about unless one becomes a Bhakta equal to him. Who is utterly and only focused on the One SatGuru. True non-duality is not different from true Bhakti. Non-dual experience is a part of Bhagavan’s infinite expressions of love.
A truly wise person wishes for the relationship with the Great Creator, wishes for the Creator Himself. Not for a tiny part of what the Creator can give as experience, reward, or realization. If one can understand how much Lord Shiva loves and worships Sri Hari, how endless and unbroken his devotion is, then it is easy to understand why Bhakti is given the primary importance in the Puranas, Guru Gita, Bhagavatam, Bhagavad Gita, and all other true divine scriptures.
Bhakti makes one capable of handling everything, good and bad, with grace. Bhakti makes one humble even in the face of extreme achievements and accolades. It keeps one grounded even in the middle of the most earth-shattering storms. Becoming a truly loving human being, a genuinely kind and helpful person, is more valuable on the spiritual path than thinking of some greatness of the self. Always give importance to Bhagavan. Never stray from Bhakti towards the SatGuru’s Lotus Feet. These are the eternal words of wisdom spoken and lived by Lord Shiva himself. Hence, the Hari Bhakta Sampradaya shows that Lord Shiva’s teachings merge beautifully with love and devotion to Sri Hari.
Lord Shiva is treated with the utmost respect and love; and a true follower of the Hari Bhakta Sampradaya, a true Guru-bhakta never judges or compares who is greater, lesser, more important, or any other petty thoughts of this nature. Learn true wisdom and love for all, the same way Lord Shiva in his deep devotion towards Sri Hari doesn’t think of anything else. He only who considers is sincerely walking on the path of love and devotion, and blesses them with the same states that he is always in.
